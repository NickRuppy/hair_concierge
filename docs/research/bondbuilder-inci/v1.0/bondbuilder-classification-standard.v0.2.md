## 1. Category boundary

Unchanged: five recognized technology families, specialized at-home treatment role, formula-compatible classification, DE/EU research targets and cosmetic-only limits. Ordinary conditioning/repair/protein marketing alone remains excluded. An identity/INCI conflict still prevents a final target-product category finding.

## 2. Input authority

Retain both raw and normalized inputs and all conflicts. A replacement source must document why it resolves the exact size/version/market gap; a visibly unrelated shampoo block is displaced with a reason, not treated as an equally plausible treatment formula. Dated source-version findings are not supplied-pack or catalog verification.

Record normalization repairs explicitly. Cross-market same-identifier evidence can corroborate a typographical/translation issue, but cannot silently overwrite a local raw list or establish worldwide formula equality.

Scientific and experience sources are separate evidence lanes, not interchangeable ranks. Record access level, affiliation/commercial context, exact-product versus technology/system/predecessor scope, findings and limitations. A video's title or routine link is not its efficacy verdict; an inaccessible transcript is not an inspected opinion.

## 3. Direct properties and evidence record

Retain the three public trust levels. Add only provenance/hold detail to the research envelope, not another public score:

- `trust_basis`: `owner_anchor`, `research_judgment`, or null when trust is held/excluded.
- `holds`: separate arrays for `identity`, `boundary`, `claim_trust`, `protocol`, and `fit`.
- `experience_evidence`: dated original statement/context, exact product, duration/repetition when supported, stated benefit/limitations, expertise/practice relevance, commercial context and access status.

Boundary and technology can be finalized for a compatible exact source-version treatment while its trust or fit is held. A trust gap alone must not erase observed markers or an established category finding. Identity/formula conflict still holds target-product boundary/technology; retain the blind candidate markers separately. Protocol and fit gaps block their own readiness, not unrelated research findings.

## 4. Evidence lexicon

No ingredient-family change. Exact v0.1 lexicon and exclusions apply. A high-trust anchor does not let brand names manufacture a marker or alter blind observations.

## 5. Deterministic versus judgment decisions

Replace v0.1's trust definitions/experience restriction with the following. Trust describes practical confidence in a bounded repair/reinforcement benefit, not confidence in every molecular slogan, expected effect magnitude or guaranteed personal success.

**High:** converging relevant support with a convincing product/formula/use bridge; informed practice strengthens the conclusion. Popularity, a patent, brand affiliation or creator agreement alone is not a general high shortcut. Nick explicitly designated **original OLAPLEX No.3 and OLAPLEX No.3PLUS** as gold-standard high-trust calibration anchors. Apply `trust_basis: owner_anchor` to an exact eligible anchor identity in Stage B. Carry relevant predecessor evidence forward; do not reopen this settled choice solely for the v0.1 evidence gap. This is an owner-approved calibration ruling, not a newly audited comparative experiment or independent proof of superiority. It applies to these two named treatments, not every OLAPLEX product or future reformulation. Original No.3 is contextual anchor material, not a ninth pilot row or unseen holdout.

**Medium:** a credible recognized approach, verified compatible targeted formula/use and a reasoned applicability bridge, supported by either relevant empirical technology/product research **or** sustained, informed positive experience alongside credible technology/formula support. Explain which route supports the practical benefit. Exact-bottle laboratory validation is not mandatory. Unknown retail active concentration or unpublished study conditions is a limitation, not an automatic hold; explain why the remaining information is adequate and what could change the conclusion. An incompatible tested concentration/vehicle/protocol, pivotal unresolved identity, or missing information that makes the bridge indefensible still causes a hold. A marker plus marketing remains insufficient. Softness alone must not be presented as proof of structural reinforcement or bond restoration.

**Low:** a completed bounded search supports a dependable conclusion that the repair claim is weakly substantiated. Record scope and strongest counterevidence. Do not turn missing source access into evidence of no effect.

**Hold:** unresolved pivotal input/source access, incomplete research, or genuinely balanced neighboring-tier interpretations prevent a dependable call. Identified inaccessible information is pivotal only if it could reasonably change the conclusion and no sufficient independent route remains; do not require every optional source to be accessible.

For every research judgment retain benefit, support, applicability, counterevidence, limitations, neighboring-tier rationale and confidence. For anchors retain the same evidence limits but identify the owner basis rather than fabricating a lab rationale.

Commercial context does not automatically invalidate informed experience. Record explicit sponsorship/affiliate/ambassador or own-brand interest where verified. A code demonstrates promotional context, not undisclosed payment. Evaluate actual reasoning, repeated use, product scope and relevant expertise. A one-off promotion is not enough; no fixed follower threshold, mandatory endorsement count or maturity score. Contradictions are judged for applicability/quality; mixed consumer reviews alone do not cap high. Repeated statements by one person are not independent sources or trials.

## 6. Formula-first sequence

Fresh independent replay lanes first receive v0.1 + this overlay + the frozen anonymous packet. The two named anchor products are calibration references, not expected answers for formula facts. Do not supply source URLs, creator opinions or prior labels in the anonymous packet. Freeze each Stage-A result before named evidence. The named packet then exposes source evidence and exact anchor membership. No source additions happen inside a sealed lane.

## 7. Classification confidence

Retain high/moderate/low confidence separate from trust. Owner-anchor high trust is not high empirical certainty; formula identity/conflict and protocol/fit still have their own confidence/holds. Research admission requires all v0.1 conditions, including at least moderate relevant classification confidence and completed product-specific review. A positive trust value does not bypass those gates.

## 8. Product truth versus user fit

Unchanged: no trust-based automatic ordering, no price-to-potency inference, no mechanism-based person ranking, no new Routine Plan depth/layout or comparison page. German explanations preserve the difference between practical benefit, supported empirical endpoint and proposed technology mechanism. An anchor can have mixed user experience and may not suit a particular user.

## 9. Calibration and holdout

Replay all eight existing pilot rows, preserving holds. Report owner-anchor rows separately from independently judged trust rows; agreement on prescribed anchors does not validate upper-tier judgment. No independent empirical high anchor is asserted by this ruling. Original No.3 and same-formula/predessor variants cannot become unseen holdouts.

Source-constant control: assess the immutable v0.1 packets under v0.2 to isolate rule changes. Reuse an already sealed Stage-A observation only if the input formula/flags are unchanged, with a read receipt; do not count reused blind observations as new independent tests. Then assess the amended packet under the same v0.2 rules. Changed formula/source inputs require fresh anonymous records. Compare rule-only and source-only deltas separately; if a delta cannot be disentangled, say so. Replay is learning, not unseen validation. Existing holdout targets remain; freeze unused identities and concrete denominators before new labels.
