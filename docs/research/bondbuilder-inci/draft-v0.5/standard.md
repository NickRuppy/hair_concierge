# Bondbuilder classification standard — v0.5 provisional draft

Semantic version: `bondbuilder-inci-v0.5`; wire versions: `bondbuilder-research-envelope-v1` and `bondbuilder-research-profile-v1`. **Unexecuted, unsealed and owner-lock pending.** This draft supersedes v0.4 only as the next proposed method; it neither rewrites immutable v0.1–v0.4 artifacts nor authorizes research, intake routing, catalog writes, recommendation, or publication.

Decision references: [v0.4 draft](../draft-v0.4/standard.md), [owner review](../../../../plans/bondbuilder-research-engine/owner-review-2026-09-30.md), [application follow-up](../../../../plans/bondbuilder-research-engine/application-followup-2026-10-01.md), [storage contract](../../../../plans/bondbuilder-research-engine/storage-contract-proposal-2026-10-02.md), and [implementation plan](../../../../plans/bondbuilder-research-engine/implementation-plan-2026-10-02.md). The synchronized [runbook](runbook.md), [researcher prompt](researcher-prompt.md), and [blind guide](blind-instructions.md) are mandatory future freeze inputs.

## 1. Status and authority

Only a future owner-approved common freeze, named packet, sealed stages, comparison and adjudication may produce a v0.5 result. Provisional adapter pins validate frozen v0.4 fixtures only; they do not lock this method. Engine routing remains disabled and ordinary Product Intake remains the live submission route.

## 2. Boundary

Include exact, at-home cosmetic targeted hair-shaft repair/reinforcement with a specialized treatment role and one recognized §4 family. Exclude generic conditioning/protein/amino-acid repair, branding alone, salon additives, and medical/scalp or hair-loss treatment. Unknown technology is `category_review`, not a guessed family. Category membership never claims native-bond restoration or efficacy.

## 3. Identity, formula, and source selection

Bind exact product, market, size/source-version, complete ordered raw INCI, normalization, raw/list SHA-256, source IDs and conflicts before assessment. Never splice formulas or infer pack equality. The settled Redken target is Douglas DE Intensive Treatment 190 ml / article 1192227 with its selected 16-ingredient source-version; retain the displaced manufacturer list as historical/conflict evidence. The v0.3 hold is historical, not a current v0.5 hold.

## 4. Closed technology lexicon

The only families are `sulfur_targeting_dimaleate` (Bis-Aminopropyl Diglycol Dimaleate), `designed_peptide` (sh-Oligopeptide-78), `maleate_ester` (Diethylhexyl Maleate), `acid_calcium_management` (Citric Acid with the specified supporting system), and `gluconamide_gluconate` (both Hydroxypropylgluconamide and Hydroxypropylammonium Gluconate). Complete input is required for conclusive absence. Presence proves neither dose, supplier, delivery, rank, pH nor effect.

## 5. Strict envelope and profile

Every candidate uses the strict envelope `{version, submission_id: UUID|null, profile}`. The profile is closed at every object boundary and retains all researched properties: method pins/artifact/output digest; identity; formula/markers/conflicts; assessment; technology reference; typed application; science/practice evidence; German explanations; inspected source registry; independent fit; per-lane holds; and review digest/references. Factual source IDs must close over inspected non-uninspected registry records. Bounds, SHA-256 integrity and source closure fail closed; no lossy reducer may replace the profile.

## 6. Assessment and owner authority

`boundary_status`, family, trust/basis, confidence, limiting factors, policy reference and reasoning are separate. Exact registered owner identities use their exact owner ruling. A resolved new in-scope identity is always `low` / `owner_default`; no model, reference similarity, copied registry key, digest, or source can promote it. Owner grades are policy provenance, not scientific certainty. A technology reference cannot inherit efficacy, tier, recommendation or protocol.

## 7. Producer application facts

Directions are independently source-bound: placement, physical applied format, role, state, amount/dilution, distribution, timing/contact versus wait, longer wear, conditioner before/after, sequence, rinse, partners and source variants. Preserve null plus reason when unknown. Never derive instructions from INCI or collapse later conditioner rinsing into a treatment rinse.

## 8. Cadence and protocol compatibility

Keep initial, maintenance and conditional cadence branches typed and source-scoped. Aveda’s overlapping manufacturer damage-label branches and cross-market facts remain distinct; no universal weekly schedule, fixed maximum, or fabricated runtime protocol. Exact executable protocol is a later separately source-bound compiler/adapter decision, not a profile completeness result.

## 9. Evidence lanes and explanations

Keep product, technology, system, predecessor and practice attribution distinct. Scientific and practical support/counterevidence are independent, bounded and limitation-bearing. Inspected creator/professional statements may be practical evidence but are not experiments. German concise/deeper explanations must describe uncertainty and cannot become commands, guarantees, rankings, or medical advice.

## 10. Fit and holds

Fine/normal/coarse are independent nullable facts with evidence, confidence and reasons; manufacturer positioning is attributed evidence, not a fit default. Holds are separated by identity, boundary, trust, protocol and fit. A missing fit/protocol fact does not erase established research or alter existing approved eligibility.

## 11. Projection and readiness

The full profile is persisted unchanged beside any narrow compatibility projection and exhaustive coverage receipt (`direct`, `mapped`, `retained_only`, `held`). No intensity/axis is invented. `verified_product_protocol` may identify only an exact valid product/role protocol; no competitor fallback. Research never writes origin, eligibility, recommendation, lifecycle, activation, or publication. Catalog-intake readiness and global-recommendation readiness remain distinct and both false until their separate gates pass.

## 12. Validation scope and non-claims

The completed v0.4 positive validation covers only one of five families; it is not v0.5 execution, freeze, owner approval, method lock, or activation evidence. A future v0.5 validation must explicitly cover the chosen families/negative controls and report gaps without inventing results. Historical artifacts, receipts and prior rulings remain immutable.
