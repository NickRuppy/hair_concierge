# Conditioner pilot - recalibration review

Status: v1.6 logic locked; product-level review remains active
Profile version: v1.6
Scope: 11 eligible rinse-out Conditioners; one leave-in excluded

## What changed

- v1.6 adds `care_direction` (`protein` / `moisture` / `balanced`) and `repair_support_level` (`low` / `medium` / `high`). These are product-formula comparison properties: care direction is not a user deficiency, repair support is not a structural-repair claim, and formula-only values remain E2. Independent Reviewer G completed the two fields for all 11 eligible formulas with `22 / 22` exact agreement against the accepted key. Nick locked the reusable logic after reviewing the NEQI, OGX, and Guhl Bond+ anchor patterns. The fields remain subject to each product's local Lab approval; unchanged approvals for the earlier seven fields remain intact.

- `rinseability` was removed from the lean profile. `weight_potential` remains the practical ingredient-informed deposition signal; actual `rinse_behavior` remains `unknown` in the detailed trace unless an exact finished-product protocol exists.
- Bali Curls 75 ml now uses the manufacturer formula as authority. HAGEL confirms the exact EAN `4262391991626`; the divergent dm transcription is retained only as source history, and Flaconi EAN `4262391990056` is a different identity rather than a same-GTIN conflict.
- Focus selection now excludes baseline conditioning, requires a differentiating product purpose for the research headline, and keeps all supported capabilities available for later flat functional-benefit mapping. The full method is in `04_focus-selection-decision-guide.md`.
- Damage Fit now reserves `highly_damaged` for high conditioning plus a distinct protein/peptide/keratin film, named bond chemistry, exceptional corroborated protection, or relevant exact-product test. Ordinary rich conditioning no longer qualifies by itself.

## What is complete

The historical seven-field pilot has its retained evidence and approvals. v1.6 requires the two new properties for every eligible product before whole-product approval. Reviewer G independently classified only the two new fields, exactly matching the accepted key for all `22 / 22` cells. The active composite preserves frozen Reviewer F judgments for the earlier seven fields and adds Reviewer G only for `care_direction` and `repair_support_level`; it is not a fresh de-novo nine-property reviewer output.

The historical human-approved seven-field key is `63 / 77 = 81.8%` against the frozen Reviewer F baseline. The v1.6 composite comparison is `94 / 99` pre-adjudication and `85 / 99 = 85.9%` post-adjudication, with `68 / 77` non-focus. Five differences remain in `primary_focus` or `secondary_focus`; two are deliberate NEQI weight/thickness overrides; seven are the approved Damage Fit recalibration. These adjudicated differences remain visible rather than rewriting the blind artifact. A fresh de-novo blind rerun covering all nine properties is required before making a full v1.6 repeatability claim.
