## Conflicts and ceilings

- Studies use different hair origins, damage states, simplified formulas, rinse protocols, instruments, and proxies. Cross-study ranking is not valid without harmonization.
- Many formulation studies are industry-authored or industry-funded; they support mechanisms and methods, not unqualified product rules.
- Evidence is especially thin for textured hair, repeated multi-product routines, and real-life cumulative residue.
- No source supports a universal ingredient-to-thickness, curl-pattern, scalp-safety, repair, or buildup rule.
