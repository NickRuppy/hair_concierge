Damage Fit: stricter differentiation

Decision question: should ordinary conditioning continue to qualify almost every product for highly damaged hair, or should that value require a distinct damage-specialist route?

Current Lab behavior

Schädigungs-Fit

moderately_damaged · highly_damaged

Why this exact classification?

  • High conditioning plus any lubrication, film, or bond candidate is enough.
  • Generic conditioner lubrication therefore frequently behaves like specialist damage support.
  • Result: 10 of 11 eligible pilot products receive the same fit.
Problem: the property is technically populated but provides little comparative value.

Recommended Lab behavior

Schädigungs-Fit

moderately_damaged · highly_damaged

Why this exact classification?

  • High conditioning establishes adequate care intensity.
  • Hydroxypropylgluconamide + Hydroxypropylammonium Gluconate form a distinct bond-chemistry candidate beyond the ordinary conditioner base.
  • That specialist route supports prioritising this product for moderately to highly damaged hair.
  • Healthy hair is not unsafe or incompatible; it is simply not the product's strongest comparative match.
Threshold: ordinary silicone, oil, panthenol, cationic polymer, or repair naming alone does not unlock highly_damaged.
healthyLow conditioning only. Rare edge case; retained until the wider cohort tests whether it exists.
healthy + moderately_damagedNormal lightweight, moderate, or high general conditioning without a distinct specialist route.
moderately + highly_damagedHigh conditioning plus a meaningful protein/peptide film, bond candidate, unusually robust protective-lubrication system, or relevant product test.
