# Shampoo v1.4 holdout-v3 operator clarifications

Status: explanatory, non-normative

These examples were recorded after adjudicating the ten-product holdout-v3 disagreements. They clarify how to apply the already-frozen `shampoo-classification-v1.4` focus rules; they do not add values, change thresholds or alter the policy used for the raw repeatability result.

## Moisture and nourishment within the existing focus vocabulary

When exact-product positioning is dominantly restorative for dry, very dry, damaged or stressed lengths, and the formula has a compatible restorative route, use the existing `repair` value. Use `general` when the language is only generic care or moisture without a dominant restorative target or compatible route.

## Shine as a secondary focus

Do not add `shine` as secondary when shine is merely an outcome of the same smoothing, deposition or restorative route already used for the primary focus. A secondary `shine` value still requires a distinct exact-product claim and an independent compatible formula route.

These clarifications explain the adjudicated direction for L'Oréal Hydra Hyaluronic, Kérastase Nutritive Bain Satin Riche and Wella Invigo Nutri-Enrich. The raw lane artifacts remain unchanged so the reported agreement stays an honest measurement of the original independent pass.
