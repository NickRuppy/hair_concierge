**What this file is.** One versioned table (§1) that says, for every ingredient family the Styling engine recognises: which INCI names belong to it, what the family may support at E2, which property or S0 rule reads it, what it must never be read as, and how well the placement is evidenced. **What it is not.** No hold strength, no weight from product form, no dose, no performance. The lexicon names families; the standard decides values.

## 0. Reading rules (apply before any entry)

### 0.1 Evidence ceiling and the assignment ladder

Reused unchanged from LI §3.1 (assignment ladder) and charter §11:

| The read is | Level |
|---|---|
| An ingredient present, at a literal rank (an INCI observation); a verbatim directions transcription | **E1** |
| Any value read from the frozen formula (a mechanism, `hold_route`, `weight`, a family membership used as evidence, a marker reading) | **E2** — the formula-only ceiling |
| A C1/C2 claim, including a hold level | **E0** (LI §2.4.1 rule 5) — authority decides whether a claim exists, never whether it is true |

EU 1223/2009 Art. 19: ingredients above 1 % are listed in descending order; below 1 % in any order; the boundary is invisible. No entry below may be read as a concentration, a dose or a performance level (LI G4).

### 0.2 The two tail markers (PH1-1, PH2-9)

PH1-1 rules two markers, one per purpose. Every record states both. **PH2-9 rules that T_S is the full Mask rule** — preservative, perfume, declared allergens/aroma chemicals, preservative boosters in a pair, chelators (the classes below) — as approved under PH1-1, not the ledger's short form „first preservative or perfume“.

| Marker | Used by | Marker-eligible classes (the **earliest** member sets the marker) | Never marker-eligible |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T_S** — Styling marker ("Mask rule") | Every Styling-owned property: `result_mechanisms`, `hold_route`, `weight`, the buildup note, the neutral fact (PH1-9), `provides_heat_protection` (standard §6.7; the heat check reads T_S, PH2-4), subtype extras | (a) fragrance: `Parfum` / `Fragrance` / `Aroma`; (b) declared fragrance allergens and named aroma chemicals declared in their own right (EU Annex III list incl. the 2023/1545 additions; e.g. Limonene, Linalool, Hydroxycitronellal, Tetramethyl Acetyloctahydronaphthalenes); (c) preservatives — PR-1 (§1); (d) preservative boosters — PR-2, **only when a PR-1 preservative is declared anywhere on the same list**; (e) trace chelators — CH-1 | CI colourants (Mask R8 precedence), Citric Acid, Sodium Hydroxide, Sodium Chloride, other pH adjusters (PH-1), botanical essential oils named as oils (peel/leaf/wood/flower/bark oils), **Annex V antioxidants (sulfites) not declared „as preservative" `[RG2-23]`** |
| **T_LI** — Leave-in border marker | Only the S0 border rules that run Leave-in logic: LI G0 (both halves, T15), the D6 care test (PH1-13 R2), the D7 middle-band test, and the "both engines could claim" check (PH1-16) | The LI §3.1.1 concentration-capped list: Phenoxyethanol, Sodium Benzoate, Potassium Sorbate, Benzyl Alcohol (as preservative), Hydroxyacetophenone, Ethylhexylglycerin, Levulinic Acid / Sodium Levulinate, Chlorphenesin, Dehydroacetic Acid, Caprylyl Glycol (in a preservative pair); **plus any other EU Annex V preservative** (LI's own "chiefly the common preservatives … at or below ~1 %"; e.g. DMDM Hydantoin, parabens, Benzoic Acid, Piroctone Olamine, Benzalkonium Chloride, Diazolidinyl Urea, Salicylic Acid) | Fragrance and the declared allergens (LI T17 removed them), chelators, colourants, pH adjusters, **Annex V antioxidants (sulfites) not declared „as preservative" `[RG2-23]`** |

"Above the tail" means **strictly before** the marker (LI §3.1.1; Mask §3.1). Within-list only: marker positions are never compared across products (LI mandatory limit 5).

**T_LI vacuity — essential oils in the fragrance block `[RG-1, R1-T]`.** For the LI §3.1.1 clause 6 vacuity test, an essential oil or other botanical fragrance oil (e.g. Citrus Aurantium / Citrus Limon Peel Oil, Pogostemon Cablin Oil, Juniperus Virginiana Oil) declared **at or after** the `Parfum`/`Fragrance` entry, or **among the declared allergens**, counts as a fragrance declaration — closed set (b). The same oil ranked **above** `Parfum` is outside the set (a species whose job its INCI name does not settle). This is the Styling side's reading of the unchanged LI clause; it changes which T_LI reads `vacuous`, never a T_S. Round 1: the oil sat inside the allergen block on all 7 split records (`round-1/diff.json` RG-1), so lane A's `vacuous` reading is the one this rule gives on all 7; it is a review trigger only (standard §8, RG-18) and moved no route or Styling value.

**Annex V antioxidants are not markers `[RG2-23, R2-T]`.** T_LI admits "any other EU Annex V preservative", while T_S class (c) is the enumerated PR-1 list, so an Annex V substance used as an antioxidant (sulfites: Sodium Bisulfite, Sodium Sulfite, Sodium Metabisulfite) could set T_LI but not T_S. It sets **neither** marker unless the list declares it „as preservative"; otherwise it is NC-1 (antioxidant), recorded E1. Round-2 case: `balea-overnight-wellenspray` (Sodium Bisulfite r7) — both markers are Potassium Sorbate r13, as both lanes read them.

**Benzyl Alcohol above `Parfum` reads PR-1 `[RG2-23, R2-T]`.** Benzyl Alcohol is both a PR-1 preservative and an EU Annex III fragrance allergen. Ranked **above** the list's own `Parfum`/`Fragrance`, it is read as the preservative (T_S class (c); on LI's list for T_LI), so it is never "a declared allergen ranked above the list's own Parfum" and never fires the third limb (i) below. At or after `Parfum` it stays an allergen declaration (class (b)), as before. Round-2 cohort (node): Benzyl Alcohol sits above `Parfum` on 12 of the 139 records; both lanes' T_S readings already follow this reading on all 12 (it is T_S on 2 and the R2 marker on 4), so no value moves. Deciding case: `redken-wax-spray` (sealed slot 6) — T_S Benzyl Salicylate r7 is `unresolved` (an allergen above Parfum r18), and the R2 marker is Benzyl Alcohol r9 read as PR-1.

**T_S plausibility (Mask §3.1.1, E2/E13, adopted unchanged with PH1-1).**
- *Establishing species* = the members that establish the record's `result_mechanisms` and `weight` (property set §0). A below-marker extra never makes the marker implausible; where crediting it would change a value it carries `candidate_below_tail` and routes to review (Mask E13).
- *Third limb (ordinal inconsistency)*: the marker is `unresolved` when the species chosen as T_S is (i) a declared allergen ranked above the list's own `Parfum`, or (ii) a booster ranked above every PR-1 preservative on the list (Mask round-2 triage note: the limb tests only orderings that involve the marker species itself).
- *Absent*: no T_S class anywhere ⇒ `tail_marker: absent`, the whole list is read as above-tail, confidence ≤ `moderate`, review (Mask §3.1).
- What an `unresolved` T_S does to a value is the standard's rule (standard §3.3), not the lexicon's.

**Cohort check (node, 70 Styling records).** T_S plausible 61 · unresolved 8 (7 booster-above-preservative, 1 allergen-above-Parfum: `loreal-paris-elnett-haarspray-extra-starker-halt`, Hydroxycitronellal r4 vs Parfum r28 — the Mask E2 worked shape) · absent 1 (`balea-pure-styling-volumenpuder-spray`). On all 8 unresolved records the two readings (marker as listed vs. the next ordinally consistent marker) give the **same** `hold_route` and `weight`, so no value rests on the limb today.

### 0.3 "Aqueous" (used by the S0 pair rules and two entries)

A formula is **aqueous** when `Aqua`/`Water` is declared **above T_S** — LI G0's "Aqua leads or is materially present", made rank-readable. Water declared only in the tail (got2b Curlz r13, got2b gotGloss r9, taft x Gliss Shine Coat r10) or not at all (Elnett Haarspray, Balea MEN Pomade, Feschi) is **non-aqueous**. This is distinct from the molding extra `base` (property set §3: `anhydrous` = no water declared anywhere), which is display data only.

**Propellant-led aerosols `[RG2-23, R2-T]`.** Propellants count as the continuous phase when they lead: water declared **below a VC-1 propellant** is not aqueous, even when it sits above T_S (`schwarzkopf-taft-haarspray-classic-halt-3`: Dimethyl Ether r1, Aqua r3 ⇒ non-aqueous; `balea-overnight-wellenspray`: Butane r2, Aqua r9 ⇒ non-aqueous). Round-2 cohort (node): this flips 12 of the 139 records from aqueous to non-aqueous (both lanes' T_S agree on all 12). No route moves: the 9 lane-A Styling routes among them move their pair rule from standard §4.3 3.4 (ii) to 3.5 (same `styling`, not held); the others are evidence-gated, `mixed` or Dry Shampoo. F values that read „aqueous" move only on non-Styling routes in round 2: `wella-eimi-volume-dry-me` (Dry Shampoo; Tapioca Starch r5 NF-U ⇒ PT-2) and `schwarzkopf-taft-texturspray-aloe-boost` (`mixed_promise` in both lanes; Distarch Phosphate r4 is no longer credited as natural film, Tapioca Starch r8 ⇒ PT-2). No generic Acrylates Copolymer (FX-2c) sits on any of the 12.

### 0.4 Normalisation, aliases and transcription repair

1. Match on normalised INCI: case-folded; parentheticals stripped (`Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter` → `butyrospermum parkii butter`); `*`/`**` origin marks stripped; whitespace around `/` and `-` collapsed. Latin binomials retained.
2. List separators are `,`, `•` and `·` at bracket depth 0. A comma **inside** an ingredient name is not a separator (LI §2.4 packet requirement: `1,2-Hexanediol` is one token).
3. **Transcription repair, recorded never silent.** A fragment that is not itself an INCI name and that forms a known INCI name with its neighbour is re-joined, and the repair is logged in the record's `formula_observations[]`. Cohort cases: `balea-locken-crunchgel` „HYDRO, LYZED CORN STARCH" and „POLYGLYCERYL, 6 CAPRYLATE"; `balea-leave-in-spray-sea-salt-care-define` „TETRAMETHYL, ACETYLOCTAHYDRONAPHTHALENES"; `balea-professional-definier-spray-traumlocken` „ALPHA, ISOMETHYL IONONE"; `langhaarmaedchen-lockenspray-halt-sprungkraft` „…FERMENT, FILTRATE"; `cantu-lockencreme-kokos` „SIMMONDSIA CHINENSIS (JOJOBA), SEED OIL". A **missing** separator is not repaired by guesswork: `balea-locken-revitalizing-spray` „CITRONELLOL SODIUM CHLORIDE" is logged as one unreadable token plus a `transcription_defect` note (tail position; it moves no F value, but it hides a `salt` flag — standard §6.6). A repair that would move a rank above T_S needs a second capture (LI T18). **The freeze applies the re-join and renumbers `[RG2-24, P2-4]`:** every split is flagged, the frozen list carries the repaired tokens with the new ranks, and packets and lanes share that one numbering (round 2: `balea-locken-crunchgel` „HYDRO"/„LYZED CORN STARCH" was split and unflagged in the packet, and the lanes renumbered differently — T_S Parfum r7 vs r10). A defect confined to the tail no longer blocks S0 (standard §4.1, RG2-24).
4. **Typos are aliases only when unambiguous**: `GLYERIN` → Glycerin, `BETANE` → Betaine, `PAPKII` → Parkii, `VITAMNIN` → vitamin.
5. **Names, marketing words and retailer chips are never aliases** and never enter the match: „Gel Wax", „Texture Clay", „Fiber", „Haaröl", „Lockencreme", „Hitzeschutz", „ohne Alkohol", „silikonfrei", „vegan". They are identity or claim data (standard §2, §9).

### 0.5 Evidence tiers for lexicon entries

| Tier | Basis |
|---|---|
| **T1** | An owner ruling (ledger) or a list adopted unchanged from a locked engine (LI v1.0, Mask v1.0) |
| **T2** | Regulation or peer-reviewed evidence (EU 1223/2009 Annex V, Art. 19; Zhou et al. 2011; McMullen & Jachowicz 1998) |
| **T3** | Supplier / trade / formulator convention, consistent with the INCI function name |
| **T4** | Lexicon placement (v0.1 or later) with no external anchor — **calibration-testable**; a blind-lane disagreement on a T4 placement is fixed here, never per product |

### 0.6 What the lexicon must not become

- **No family sets a hold strength** (D8, D8b, LI T7, FS-9). `fixative_film` says a hold *route* exists, nothing about *how much*.
- **No family sets weight from form** (LI G9; PH1-2). A spray, a mousse or a "cream" weighs what its above-tail families weigh.
- **No single ingredient is a verdict.** Only the closed fixative-class list (FX-1…FX-4; generic Acrylates Copolymer only under the FX-2c corroboration rule), the closed L9 list (HP-1) and the PH1-3 alcohol trigger (AL-1) are deterministic memberships; every other family feeds a rule the standard owns.
- Every counter-signal, confidence note and review flag stays internal (research uncertainty is never user-facing).

Any new acrylate copolymer name lands in FX-U until a lexicon bump.
   **Cohort (node, 70 Styling records) — every record with generic Acrylates Copolymer:**
