# Oil supplementary research policy v0.1

2026-10-15. OE59 clarifies the owner-locked [property set v0.4](01_property-set-v0.4.md): its eleven values are the required minimum, not a prohibition on additional research. Nick explicitly permits researching more now, using only some properties in selection, and retaining others for later use.

Additional useful evidence/properties may be collected as optional, explicitly named supplementary research with source, method/version and limitations. Keep existing richer v0.8/v0.9 profiles intact. Previously removed/deferred properties need not be researched for every product; this permission does not silently restore them as required work or reintroduce repair/growth/toning efficacy classifications.

A researched property does not become a matching criterion merely by being stored. New ranking/filter behavior needs its own concrete rule and verification against the user/routine inputs. Until then the extra research is available for explanation or future integration. Optional research introduces no mandatory user question, universal completeness gate, automatic matching-tag conversion or production write. Candidate/envelope schemas and exact existing method pins remain unchanged by this clarification.
