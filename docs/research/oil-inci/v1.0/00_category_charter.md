## 1. Category and population

Category: cosmetic products sold for hair/scalp use as oils or oil-labelled serums, across plant-oil, silicone/emollient, mixed and water-based forms. Cosmetic care of hair fibres and explicit scalp application is the scope. Water-based oil-labelled members are explicitly retained under the earlier ruling; water does not imply low weight. Multi-use positioning neither excludes a genuine hair-use oil nor creates a new fit tag.

Ordinary neighbouring creams, conditioners, styling gels/sprays and medicinal products do not enter solely because they contain oil. Food-oil declarations are not silently rewritten as cosmetic INCI. The original dmBio food-jar slot remains an input-boundary case; a cosmetic coconut reserve is proposed. E08's oil-labelled sun/styling form remains an explicit category probe, with inclusion/exclusion recorded before downstream labels. This boundary proposal needs resolution at the method/cohort checkpoint; do not claim a frozen scope meanwhile.

Market: exact current German-market identities, with documented DE/EU equivalence where required. Product identity includes market/variant/package and formula version; matching names or sizes do not by themselves establish equivalent formulas. No live catalog coverage percentage/count is asserted. The first eight development candidates are an exploratory contrast set, not an 80/20 shelf census.

Medical boundary: no growth, hair-loss treatment, dandruff treatment or individual tolerance prediction. Scalp botanical presence is exposure/watch-out metadata only; medically adjacent marketing remains source material, not assessed efficacy. No repair, protein/moisture direction, porosity fit, multi-use fit tag or toning flag is added.
