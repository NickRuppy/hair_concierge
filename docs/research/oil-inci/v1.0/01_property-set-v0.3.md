# Oil research property set v0.3

2026-10-15. Local research successor documenting the owner-approved persistence boundaries. Scope remains oil only, source-supported retained leave-on fibre application. Pre-wash removal does not determine this value. Other category standards/adapters remain unchanged.
Current compact successor to [approved proposal v0](01_property-set-v0.md), carrying later approved weight, fit and persistence decisions. [Standard v0.9](02_classification-standard-v0.9.md) owns operational rules. This replaces the binary build-up aspect; it does not add another scored property.

Version boundary: these documents govern new local v0.9 research. The existing 37-profile Lab dataset and local adapter/projection remain pinned to v0.8; this documentation neither repins nor revalidates them. Historical sources, records and methods remain immutable. The [disclosed boundary anchors](12_persistence-boundaries-v0.1.md) are operator-only developmental calibration evidence; never include that document or its labels in a blind packet.

Other inferred properties keep the existing research metadata convention; the new persistence property explicitly does not use it. Broken identity, incomplete declarations or missing persistence context are repaired before admission rather than encoded as another persistence class. Historical research unknowns remain unchanged.

Fit is derived separately from direct properties and source-supported scenarios. [Thickness](05_thickness-fit-standard-v0.1.md): retained light is suitable for fine/normal and conditional for coarse; medium conditional for fine and suitable for normal/coarse; rich conditional for fine/normal and suitable for coarse. Shampooed pre-wash fibre use has no weight-only exclusion. [Pattern](06_curl-pattern-fit-standard-v0.1.md) and [damage](07_damage-fit-standard-v0.1.md) add no selection preference for established fibre use. Scalp-role fibre fit remains inapplicable/unknown under the existing overlay contract. Persistence introduces no additional fit penalty.

No per-product main strengths, repair, protein/moisture direction, porosity, multi-use tag, toning or growth efficacy. Inherited base effect wording still needs its separate final wording checkpoint. Existing v0.8 local full-profile/adapter artifacts retain their own receipts. New v0.9 research requires its own calibration/verification and an explicit later bridge-version decision before intake use; this property update alone does not establish intake readiness.

For persistence, low is a less-persistent remainder rather than zero residue; moderate is ordinary retained coating; high needs an affirmative cohesive/adherent/structured conditioning-film reading. Material names, weight and vehicle alone do not determine the class. See [guidance v0.3](10_persistence-guidance-v0.3.md) for the complete generic rules.
