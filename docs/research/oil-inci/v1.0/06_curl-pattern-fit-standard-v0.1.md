# Oil curl-pattern fit — research standard v0.1

Approved 2026-10-05 under OE43. Nick accepted **no additional oil-weight preference from curl pattern alone**, after the recommendation and fine-coily/coarse-straight examples. This is a neutral compatibility policy for the pattern axis, not a measured efficacy or curl-definition claim. It supplements the unchanged OE42 thickness policy.

## Rule and output meaning

For an established, complete fibre application scenario, all four pattern values (`straight`, `wavy`, `curly`, `coily`) receive `suitable` on the pattern axis, with reason `no_additional_pattern_gate`. This means no additional pattern-based exclusion or weight preference is imposed. It does not mean ideal conditioning, adequate curl hold/definition or universally suitable overall fit.

Never use a suitable pattern cell to erase a conditional or unknown thickness result, an application restriction or an unresolved other axis. Fine coily hair retains fine-hair weight caveats; coarse straight hair retains coarse-hair finish preferences. Pattern does not change formula weight, penetration, build-up, dose or source-established use permission.

## Source and scenario gates

- `pre_wash_fibre_treatment`: exact source must establish fibre application followed by shampoo. Keep dose/contact time/site/removal coherent; no removal or benefit guarantee.
- `leave_on_fibre_conditioning` / `dry_finish`: exact source must establish retained fibre application with its applicable stage/site and directions.
- `scalp_application`: all four fibre-pattern cells are `unknown`, with reason `scalp_role_not_fibre_fit`. A shared hair/scalp instruction is split by supported role; it cannot turn scalp suitability into fibre fit.
- Missing/contradictory application evidence remains unknown. Incomplete identity/formula/source review blocks derivation rather than manufacturing suitable values. Completed review with no established role yields no scenario rows.

Retain exact source/direction IDs, coherent scenarios, current policy ID and source hashes. These are estimated compatibility verdicts; the applied overlay retains low confidence and no sensory-validation claim. Ingredient/source facts keep their own authority. Unknown weight can still permit this narrow no-pattern-exclusion verdict, while retained thickness fit remains unknown; the pattern axis does not resolve the weight gap.
