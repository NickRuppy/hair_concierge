## Decisions and transferable boundaries

1. **Low means less persistent, not no residue.** It needs affirmative support for a mainly mobile/dispersible remainder after considering the full retained system. An empty low band is acceptable. Search plausible candidates and document coverage; a bounded search cannot prove no low-persistence hair oil exists.
2. **Moderate positively describes ordinary retained coating.** Fluid/lipid conditioning can feel light or heavy. A missing grade or uncertain mechanism is not a moderate fallback: compare the neighboring readings, commit one scalar and explain the tipping assumption separately.
3. **High needs affirmative conditioning-film architecture.** A coherent gum-supported, adherent or structured system can favor high. A plausible dimethiconol gum route with complementary conditioning fluids is one route; an INCI name, pair, rank or count is not an automatic verdict. Exact commercial grades and proportions must not be invented. Dimethicone alone does not establish high.
4. **Distinguish film and vehicle roles.** A rheology/texture polymer does not automatically provide the persistent conditioning film. A mobile, volatile or aqueous vehicle does not cancel the film left by other constituents. Natural materials can qualify as high when their exact-material/whole-system mechanism supports it; neither botanical nor silicone origin determines the answer.
5. **Keep the endpoint separate.** Weight, viscosity, stickiness, nonvolatility, penetration, dose, marketing, spray format and base are supporting context at most, not standalone persistence verdicts. The endpoint concerns source-supported retained leave-on hair-fibre application. It establishes neither measured duration, wash counts, shampoo resistance nor repeated-use accumulation; it adds no repair claim or fit penalty. Pre-wash/scalp-only cases are outside this property run, not low controls.

Every admitted finished product receives only `persistence_potential: low | moderate | high`. Research reasons, exact ingredient references, considered ranks and source/context provenance stay in a separate trace. No confidence, estimate, unknown, alternatives or conditional computational field is introduced.

## Transfer and verification scope

The seven disclosed anchors include six named product discussions and one material-context recommendation. They are not seven fresh, fully admitted product envelopes. No source, rank, ingredient quantity, grade, dose conversion or market equivalence may be supplied from these summaries alone. New runs resolve and freeze exact declarations, source-supported contexts and function evidence before isolated assessments.

New trials should prefer exact DE/EU product identities. Other markets may supply explicitly scoped developmental calibration, while subsequent catalog candidates need exact European product research. Previously explored formulas remain exposed cases even if source market/packaging changes; check formula exposure independently of product-name freshness.

The v0.9 documents govern new local research only. Existing 37-profile Lab research, v0.8 projection/adapter pins and approval bindings remain unchanged. A new persistence-only trial is neither a complete product intake package nor authorization for database writes, matching tags, publication or activation. Two-lane agreement measures repeatability, not accuracy. Report class coverage and any plausible low candidates that did not qualify, without a balancing quota or exhaustive-market claim.
