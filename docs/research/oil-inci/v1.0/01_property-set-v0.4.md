# Oil final research property set v0.4

Locked by Nick on 2026-10-15 (OE58): “Okay let's lock in the final properties that we always research.” This confirms the immediately preceding lean-property proposal. This is the prospective research-scope contract; existing v0.8/v0.9 artifacts retain their original methods and values.

## Always researched

Six main properties and five individual boolean ingredient flags: eleven direct values, grouped into seven research aspects. Every admitted retained-use hair-oil product receives all eleven values; supplementary UV protection is still a required reviewed yes/no field.

| Property | Exact values | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `base` | `pure_plant_oil`, `plant_oil_blend`, `silicone_led`, `ester_alkane_led`, `hybrid`, `water_based`; existing permitted research gap `unknown` | Whole carrier/conditioning architecture; single-oil base does not mean analytical purity. |
| `weight` | `light`, `medium`, `rich`; existing permitted research gap `unknown` | Approximate hair-load tendency from the full formula, exact natural-material reference v0.10 and blend policy v0.2. |
| `persistence_potential` | `low`, `moderate`, `high` | One committed scalar for source-supported retained fibre use. No unknown, confidence or estimate field for this scalar. |
| `supported_uses` | Unique subset of `pre_wash_fibre_treatment`, `leave_on_fibre_conditioning`, `dry_finish`, `scalp_application` | Exact coherent maker directions. Multiple uses allowed; preserve application site, stage, dose, contact time, removal and restrictions separately. |
| `provides_heat_protection` | `true`, `false` | Accepted explicit exact-product maker/pack heat-protection claim; no ingredient AND requirement. |
| `provides_uv_protection` | `true`, `false` | Both recognized exact ingredient filter and accepted explicit maker hair-UV claim. Completed review with no accepted combination is false. |
| `ingredient_flags` | Five required booleans: `silicones`, `mineral_oil`, `drying_alcohol`, `fragrance`, `scalp_active_botanicals` | Exact reviewed matches; preserve the existing closed scalp-active set and ingredient-form distinctions. |

Permitted base/weight unknowns retain the prior contract and name the decisive evidence gap; absent percentages alone do not require them. They are not fallback classes, zeroes or automatic eligibility decisions. This lock reduces the property set without changing retained-property evidence thresholds or forcing new confidence mechanics. Reasons, rank evidence, source bindings and research limitations remain trace information.

## Outside recurring oil classification

- **Advertised-oil prominence:** removed. No new hero list or leading/supporting/trace assessment. Ingredient identity remains in the formula ledger.
- **Dispenser:** ordinary factual product/package metadata, alongside package size, purchase URL and images. May support application instructions; no oil-suitability score or research-property gate.
- **Penetration potential:** deferred. Preserve existing research, but do not require a fresh penetration judgment for every product or use it in selection. No presumption that oils have identical penetration or that penetrating means repairing/better.
- **Curl-pattern and damage fit:** no new per-product scored/researched properties while the oil rules add no distinguishing preference. This does not remove the user's curl pattern or damage context from the general routine system.
- **Thickness fit:** derived separately from weight and the exact application scenario under the approved matrix; not another manually researched product property. Preserve conditional suitability and the pre-wash/scalp distinctions.

No main strengths, repair level, protein/moisture direction, growth efficacy, porosity, toning or multi-use fit tag is introduced.

## Product truth versus selection

Product research records supported uses; the recommendation layer chooses the appropriate application from the user's goals/routine. It must not invent product directions or a universal best-use property. This lock adds no mandatory user question.

Persistence remains researched but neutral in general ranking. An explicitly stated preference about a lingering film may inform a separately approved soft preference; fine hair, frizz, dry lengths or scalp oiliness alone do not establish that preference. Do not equate persistence with weight, accumulation, wash counts or efficacy. This is the locked policy direction, not a claim that preference matching is implemented.

Weight/thickness, source-supported application and relevant heat needs retain their agreed selection roles. Ingredient flags and UV remain available to later explicit preference/use-case rules. Legacy matching tags stay manually proposed and approved; no automatic matching conversion or new selector logic is authorized by this document alone.
