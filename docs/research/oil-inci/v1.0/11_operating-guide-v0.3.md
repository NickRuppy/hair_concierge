## Quick reference

- Low: less persistent mobile/dispersible conditioning remainder is the better whole-system reading.
- Moderate: ordinary retained fluid/lipid or removable conditioner coating is the better reading.
- High: coherent cohesive/adherent/structured conditioning film is the better reading.
- Moderate is not uncertainty; borderline formulas still get one decision.
- Weight, penetration, base, fit, oil count and silicone presence do not determine persistence.
- Old low/elevated values and material-weight priors are not canonical persistence targets.
- Computation consumes one scalar; reason, evidence and selected context remain research provenance.
- Every new run freezes its own inputs; previously explored formulas cannot become unseen holdout cases.

## Boundary application checklist

Use [persistence guidance v0.3](10_persistence-guidance-v0.3.md) for the generic boundary rules. Ask what retained conditioning architecture positively favors the chosen class. For high, identify a substantive cohesive/adherent/structured film route and challenge rheology-only readings; for moderate, identify ordinary coating rather than defaulting from missing grade; for low, explain a less-persistent remainder rather than treating residue absence as necessary. Dimethiconol-supported gum systems are one route, not a lookup rule. Unknown grades need a stated tipping interpretation, not an invented grade. Natural systems are not barred from high. Weight, viscosity, stickiness, nonvolatility, penetration, dose, marketing, spray and base are not standalone verdicts.

The [disclosed anchor record](12_persistence-boundaries-v0.1.md) belongs to operator/adjudicator review only. Exclude it, its product labels and its weight values from blind inputs. Check project exposure before selecting fresh cases; developmental anchors cannot become unseen holdout evidence. An empty low class is acceptable: report searched candidates and coverage rather than manufacturing balanced classes.
