## Meaning and result

`light | medium | rich | unknown` describes the **rough potential for greasy/coated feel and flattening on hair**, comparing a modest equal applied dose under similar leave-on conditions. Light means lighter among oil materials. It does not mean weightless, less retained mass, or automatically suitable for fine hair. Every oil can weigh hair down at sufficient dose. The INCI of one added oil cannot determine the weight of the finished blend.

The current list contains 66 botanical fixed-oil declarations and one processed C8/C10 MCT emollient: **67 reference entries and 106 reviewed names**. It contains **43 light, 14 medium, 9 rich, and 1 unresolved combined declaration**. Of 66 assigned tendencies, 64 are low confidence; only MCT and castor have moderate confidence in the approximate direction. These counts track exact declarations, not 67 unique physical oil types. None is a validated hair-weight class. Calendula guidance is separate and does not count as a fixed oil. There is no target class distribution.

Seven of the eight former unknowns now have evidence-backed approximate directions: Canola, evening primrose, borage, blackcurrant, Sunflower Hybrid and sea-buckthorn seed are light/low; Macadamia Integrifolia/Tetraphylla is medium/low. The combined sea-buckthorn fruit/seed declaration remains unknown because its fruit/seed ratio and exact material behavior are absent; its credible range is light–rich. It is fully researched and covered, rather than a forgotten row. Do not turn that absent ratio into a medium average.

Historical v0.7 changes relative to v0.6 were argan, moringa and black seed (light to medium, low confidence), with Cacay the only fixed oil added in that revision. v0.8 added hazelnut without altering those 65 predecessor entries; v0.9 adds the Coconut Fruit Oil declaration while preserving all 66 v0.8 entries. Approved sunflower light/low and marula medium/low remain intact. The supplied sheet was explicitly described by Nick as ordered by heaviness on hair, so it is practical weight evidence; thickness-fit labels alone are not a weight measurement. Physical flow observations remain counterevidence for the three sheet changes.

## Evidence used for hair, and its limits

The strongest available evidence concerns how an oil flows, spreads and forms a film, plus some primary maker guidance specifically about weight on hair. Direct comparative neat-oil hair-heaviness tests covering this list were not found. Physical observations therefore support **explicit engineering estimates**, not measured hair conclusions. Cosmetic raw-material data are used for bulk behavior only; skin absorption or skin greasiness does not decide a class.

- **Professional practice:** the [user-supplied hairdresser sheet](../../../../plans/oil-inci/research/hairdresser-natural-oil-sheet-2026-10-02.png), explicitly described as an ordering of weight on hair, supplies a practical anchor for OE23. Authorship and test conditions are unverified; confidence remains low. Existing catalog recommendations inherited from the same sheet are not independent corroboration.
- **Hair guidance:** a primary maker or ingredient supplier specifically describes coating or weight on hair for the identified material. This is a claim unless a protocol is supplied. A generic hair-care application listing is insufficient.
- **Material inference:** an actual neat-oil viscosity/flow observation supports a hypothesis about distribution over strands and concentrated oily coating. Lower viscosity does not establish lower deposited mass or lower build-up. Different temperatures, methods, extraction processes and units are never pooled into a quantitative ranking or cutoff.
- **Family inference:** an explicit cross-species, plant-part or grade transfer where exact evidence is missing. Keep low confidence, the identity separate and the transfer visible. Liquid state alone, density, fatty-acid ratios and penetration do not establish a weight class.

[Marsh, Knowles and Simmonds (2023)](https://www.cosmeticsandtoiletries.com/magazine/article/22862759/cosmetics-toiletries-magazine-choosing-the-right-plant-oil-for-hair-and-skin-benefits) connects flow/spreading and applied level to sensory effects and hair surface coverage. It supplies a mechanism, not a three-bin threshold. [Unilever’s hair-oil patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20020039564A1/en) gives a limited relative greasy-feel signal for a whole-coconut benchmark versus diluted formulations. [Keis et al.](https://1.oliveoiltimes.com/library/olive-oil-hair.pdf) measures hair adhesion and film changes, not perceived heaviness; it cannot rank this list by penetration or combing results.

The four independent research ledgers retain observations and exclusions: [common](../../../../plans/oil-inci/research/hair-weight-lane-common-2026-10-02.json), [midlist](../../../../plans/oil-inci/research/hair-weight-lane-midlist-2026-10-02.json), [ambiguous](../../../../plans/oil-inci/research/hair-weight-lane-ambiguous-2026-10-02.json), [specialty](../../../../plans/oil-inci/research/hair-weight-lane-specialty-2026-10-02.json). The v0.9 JSON carries forward the scoped v0.7 adjustments and research recommendations, including departures from those advisory proposals, and records the OE33/OE35 exact additions. Two-lane agreement does not increase evidence confidence. Blocked maker pages use explicitly retained/indexed source observations; current access and original-source limits remain disclosed.

## Read a new formula

1. **Resolve identity.** Preserve complete raw INCI/ranks; match the exact canonical names or reviewed aliases. Only trim/collapse whitespace and case for lookup. Do not erase parentheses/form/plant-part terms, guess botanical synonyms or convert an extract/derivative into an oil. A source-backed label-display correction is a separate exact-product receipt. An unresolved decision-relevant material needs review.
2. **Record material priors.** For every admitted fixed oil/MCT, record rank, reference entry, prior, confidence and alternatives. This is ingredient evidence, not yet the product answer. MCT means exact `CAPRYLIC/CAPRIC TRIGLYCERIDE`; “fractionated coconut” or “MCT” alone cannot establish that identity. This processed emollient is not an intact plant oil for base inventory.
3. **Read carrier roles.** Use the whole-formula role ledger and current blend policy v0.2: principal, secondary, auxiliary or unresolved. Rank helps interpret the carrier backbone but is not concentration. No fixed first-two rule, count vote, percentage, 1% boundary or presumed trace tail.
4. **Single material plus auxiliaries.** If one identified oil/emollient is the entire carrier and everything else is a recognized auxiliary, use its admitted prior as the working product estimate, retaining its evidence ceiling, confidence and strongest alternative. Unknown grade alone need not force unknown. An unresolved ingredient that could change the backbone prevents this shortcut. This permits a pure castor estimate from the entire carrier; it does not create a rich floor for any castor-containing product.
5. **Blend or competing carrier.** Judge the principal oil mixture together with volatile/mobile emollients, residual films/gums/resins and structural lipids. Consistent principal priors can support a class. Opposing priors require an affirmative explanation of the overall carrier/film architecture; rank alone cannot establish majority or decide a balanced case. Do not average classes or assign medium because light and rich oils coexist. A secondary/auxiliary rich oil contributes a counter-signal without forcing rich. A light oil does not cancel a substantial film system.
6. **Challenge and return.** State why the best-supported class wins over both lighter and richer interpretations; retain any decisive uncertainty. `medium` requires positive intermediate evidence, never missing evidence. If interpretations remain balanced or decisive material identity/film behavior is missing, return `unknown` with a specific gap.

Every substantive weight remains `{value, estimate:true, confidence:moderate|low, evidence, counter_signal, nearest_alternative, reason, unresolved}`. Product confidence cannot exceed the decisive material/architecture evidence; two-lane agreement never upgrades it. New evidence or an exact verified grade can support a separately logged derivation. No hidden fit or efficacy conclusion follows.

## Boundaries to check

- A mobile carrier with a late rich botanical oil must still be judged from its full residual system; presence cannot decide the product.
- A substantial gum/resin system can dominate light botanical priors; volatile carrier does not erase the film.
- An all-oil blend with opposing priors does not reveal ratios and may remain unknown.
- A recognized modified oil uses its own exact material evidence; `PEG-40 HYDROGENATED CASTOR OIL` and `COCO-CAPRYLATE/CAPRATE` do not inherit castor/coconut priors.
- An unfamiliar fixed oil stays outside the reference until researched. `unknown` is available; “all plant oils medium” is not a fallback.
- Refined and crude avocado can share INCI but differ in sensory descriptions. Grade-specific evidence can alter an estimate; ordinary INCI cannot identify the grade.
