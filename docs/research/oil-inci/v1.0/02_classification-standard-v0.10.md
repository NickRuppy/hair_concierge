Keep the [v0.9](02_classification-standard-v0.9.md) identity, exact complete declaration, formula-first independence, source/ingredient ledger, whole-architecture base/weight, ingredient-match flags, claim gates and coherent source-direction rules **only for the properties retained in v0.4**. Continue using exact natural-oil reference v0.10 and blend policy v0.2. Ingredient order does not establish percentages, a 1% threshold or measured performance.

Use [persistence guidance v0.3](10_persistence-guidance-v0.3.md) and the [shared persistence contract](../../shared/persistence-potential-v0.1.md) unchanged. Admit a source-supported retained hair-fibre scenario before assigning the scalar. Pre-wash/scalp-only examples remain documented scope exclusions for this leave-on assessment, never automatic low-persistence products.

Keep substantive base/weight research metadata and exact source/rank evidence. Persistence remains a scalar with a separate reason, all-rank trace and tipping assumption. Claim/flag booleans require completed review; absence of an accepted claim does not measure zero protection. No growth, repair or clinical inference follows from a flag.

## Reduced stages

1. Resolve and freeze exact product/market/package, complete raw INCI, canonical ordered ranks, exact function/material evidence and narrowly admitted neutral retained-use context. Record conflicts and limits.
2. Formula-first assessment: complete mechanism ledger plus **base, weight, persistence and the five ingredient flags**. Mechanism evidence is supporting research, not an additional product benefit. No required penetration verdict or advertised-oil prominence/suffix-to-prominence assessment.
3. Seal before controlled source unblinding. Complete **supported uses, heat and UV** from the exact product's maker/pack evidence. Preserve coherent scenarios; do not revise formula judgments to match positioning. Do not collect marketing heroes as a required classification input.
4. Keep dispenser and other factual shopping/package data in ordinary product metadata. Derive thickness fit outside direct research using the approved scenario-specific policy. Do not emit new per-product curl-pattern or damage-fit judgments under this scope.
5. Verify all eleven direct values, rank/source/context closure and immutable run/version provenance. Preserve explicit permitted gaps. Report actual coverage and repeatability without a low-class quota or accuracy/holdout overclaim.
