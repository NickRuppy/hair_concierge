# Oil persistence guidance v0.1

2026-10-09. The [shared persistence contract](../../shared/persistence-potential-v0.1.md) owns the endpoint and three values. This supersedes binary build-up for new oil research under [standard v0.7](02_classification-standard-v0.7.md); historical standards, priors and records remain reproducible. It introduces no weight/fit, repair, cleansing schedule, wash-count or measured repeated-accumulation rule.

1. Admit one exact complete declaration, its source-scoped product key and an explicit source-supported application context before classification. The judgment concerns the retained coating for leave-on use, or likely remaining coating after the documented removal for rinse/pre-wash use. Do not infer a shampoo step, fibre use or overnight permission from a scalp instruction. The chosen context and source belong in research provenance, not another consumer property or unknown output value. A missing declaration/context is an admission problem to repair first.
2. Freeze a source-neutral packet containing every ordered primary ingredient and separate contextual footnotes, plus the admitted neutral use context. Strip brands, identities, marketing/claims, earlier product labels and material weight/build-up values. Retain exact grade/derivative/plant-part distinctions and reviewed functions. An unreviewed function is not evidence of negligible contribution or high persistence. Do not expand fragrance footnotes into ranked parent-formula ingredients.
3. Judge all retained components as a system. Use the shared low/moderate/high anchors, challenge the neighboring readings, and commit the best value. Moderate positively describes ordinary retained coating. A mobile vehicle cannot cancel a substantive film; one polymer cannot establish a cohesive conditioning film on its own. No count, rank threshold, percentage, strongest-ingredient rule or universal botanical/silicone mapping.
4. Emit `persistence_potential` as one scalar. Do not emit confidence, estimate, unknown, indeterminate, alternatives or conditional gates for this property. Keep the reason, exact rank/name evidence, all considered ranks and admitted context/source in a separate research trace. Borderline assumptions can be described in ordinary prose without changing computation.
5. Preserve earlier binary labels solely as history. Whole jojoba's former low build-up prior is not a new persistence prior: its pilot was freshly judged moderate. Any material-function evidence reused from earlier work must be stripped of finished-product answers and binary value fields. Version a changed judgment; never rewrite a frozen run.
6. Two independent lanes read only the declared frozen method, neutral packet and exact-function dictionary. Seal all outputs before parent comparison/adjudication. The parent commits one value per admitted source-scoped product, retains reasons for disagreements and emits a flat projection with exactly product_key, policy_version and persistence_potential. Agreement measures repeatability only.

The standard requires explicit context admission. The current replay's choice between different supported contexts must be settled in its run plan before freeze; this guidance does not silently impose a cross-use aggregation rule. The six-product pilot remains its own frozen application-context test. No production consumer should combine a new persistence value and an old binary build-up value as two independent matching penalties.
