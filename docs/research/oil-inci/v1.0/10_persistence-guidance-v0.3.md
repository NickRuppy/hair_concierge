## Boundary decisions for new research

- **Low versus moderate:** low requires affirmative support for a less-persistent, mainly mobile/dispersible remainder after considering every retained component. Low does not mean no residue. Moderate is the positive reading of an ordinary retained fluid/lipid conditioning coat; it is not the default for missing grade or inconclusive evidence.
- **Moderate versus high:** high requires an affirmative cohesive/adherent/structured conditioning-film route in the complete system. A plausible dimethiconol gum route plus complementary conditioning fluids can support this reading, but presence, ingredient pairs, rank and counts never decide it automatically. Undisclosed silicone grades are not known fluid/volatile/gum grades; explain the tipping interpretation without inventing one.
- **Material and vehicle checks:** distinguish rheology/texture polymers from conditioning-film contributors. Mobile, volatile or aqueous delivery cannot cancel the retained system. Natural materials can qualify as high when exact-material/whole-system evidence supports that route; ordinary botanical coating is not a universal moderate rule. Weight, viscosity, stickiness, nonvolatility, penetration, dose, marketing, spray format and base alone do not set persistence.
- **Evidence ceiling and coverage:** these are committed qualitative readings, not measured duration, shampoo resistance, wash counts or repeated-use build-up. Empty low coverage is acceptable. Search plausible counterexamples and report actual coverage; never force a low case or claim exhaustive absence.
