# Oil research property set v0.2

Confirmed scope (2026-10-09): oil only; persistence is assessed for source-supported leave-on fibre application. Pre-wash removal does not determine this value. Other category standards/adapters remain unchanged. This successor preserves the earlier prepared contract.
2026-10-09. Current compact successor to [approved proposal v0](01_property-set-v0.md), carrying later approved weight, fit and persistence decisions. [Standard v0.8](02_classification-standard-v0.8.md) owns operational rules. This replaces the binary build-up aspect; it does not add another scored property.

Other inferred properties keep the existing research metadata convention; the new persistence property explicitly does not use it. Broken identity, incomplete declarations or missing persistence context are repaired before admission rather than encoded as another persistence class. Historical research unknowns remain unchanged.

Fit is derived separately from direct properties and source-supported scenarios. [Thickness](05_thickness-fit-standard-v0.1.md): retained light is suitable for fine/normal and conditional for coarse; medium conditional for fine and suitable for normal/coarse; rich conditional for fine/normal and suitable for coarse. Shampooed pre-wash fibre use has no weight-only exclusion. [Pattern](06_curl-pattern-fit-standard-v0.1.md) and [damage](07_damage-fit-standard-v0.1.md) add no selection preference for established fibre use. Scalp-role fibre fit remains inapplicable/unknown under the existing overlay contract. Persistence introduces no additional fit penalty.

No per-product main strengths, repair, protein/moisture direction, porosity, multi-use tag, toning or growth efficacy. Inherited base effect wording still needs its separate final wording checkpoint. Full research-profile consolidation, calibration/holdout and deterministic intake adapter/bridge remain later engine-completion gates; this property update alone does not establish intake readiness.
