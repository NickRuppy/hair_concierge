# Oil damage fit — research standard v0.1

Approved 2026-10-05 under OE45. Nick chose no interaction between damage level and oil selection, after rejecting the coconut pre-wash exception under OE44. This is a deliberately neutral selection policy; it does not claim that oils have zero protective benefit or reverse structural damage.

## Rule and meaning

For a complete, established fibre application scenario, `healthy`, `moderately_damaged` and `highly_damaged` each receive `suitable` on this axis with reason `no_additional_damage_gate`. Damage alone adds no preference, exclusion or bonus for weight, penetration, build-up, botanical identity or base. No repair property, protein/moisture direction or coconut-specific exception is introduced.

Suitable means no additional damage gate. It is not a repair, efficacy, sufficiency or overall eligibility verdict. Thickness conditions, pattern context, exact use restrictions and unresolved direct properties remain visible and cannot be overridden. Unknown weight or penetration does not force an unknown damage cell: this neutral axis does not depend on those properties, but does not resolve them either.

## Source and scenario gates

- `pre_wash_fibre_treatment`: authoritative directions must establish fibre application followed by shampoo. Preserve the coherent site, stage, dose, time and removal; do not invent a protective benefit or treatment protocol.
- `leave_on_fibre_conditioning` / `dry_finish`: authoritative directions must establish retained fibre use at the applicable stage/site.
- `scalp_application`: all three fibre-damage cells remain `unknown`, reason `scalp_role_not_fibre_fit`. Split shared hair/scalp instructions by role; damage neutrality does not establish scalp tolerance or fibre permission.
- Missing or contradictory directions remain unknown. Incomplete identity/formula/source review blocks derivation. Completed review without an established role yields no scenario rows.

Retain exact source/direction IDs, the complete source scenario, policy identity/hashes and applicable thickness/pattern context references. Applied compatibility estimates keep low confidence; approval does not establish measured performance.
