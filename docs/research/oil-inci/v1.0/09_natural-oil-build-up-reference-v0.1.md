Rationale: the existing material record describes a fluid liquid wax, supporting a practical weak-coating hypothesis at comparable small applications. This is a newly accepted approximation using that context; the original documentation did not measure low repeated build-up. Light weight, skin absorption and lack of gum alone do not establish the result. Dose, grade, repeated applications and washing affect actual residue. Low does not mean zero coating or easy shampoo removal.

For a complete single-ingredient whole-jojoba declaration, reuse this prior in future blind classifications, with estimate metadata and counter-signals. Organic-production wording is contextual, not another ingredient. Source-specific contrary evidence or a decisive grade gap requires fresh review. For a blend, use the material prior only as a constituent clue and assess the complete retained architecture. Jojoba Esters, Hydrolyzed Jojoba Esters, Hydrogenated Jojoba Oil, Isopropyl Jojobate and PEG derivatives are distinct materials. The JVN derivative gap is therefore still open.
