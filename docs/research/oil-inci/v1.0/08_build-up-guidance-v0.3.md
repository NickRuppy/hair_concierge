# Provisional oil build-up guidance v0.3 — binary practical estimates

Current local research successor under OE52 (2026-10-06). Complete-formula product build-up outputs are low or elevated; uncertainty stays in estimate metadata, alternatives and named gaps. OE51 whole-jojoba prior reuse is retained. Frozen v0.2 remains the historical predecessor. Frozen v0.1/round-7 inputs and results remain authoritative for that replay. Future classifiers must receive the frozen [natural base-oil build-up reference v0.1](09_natural-oil-build-up-reference-v0.1.md) and its structured companion alongside the formula dictionary.

Scope: new formula-only build-up estimates for cosmetic hair/scalp oils and oil-labelled serums, including water-based oil-serums. No weight, penetration, fit, safety, repair, heat/UV or production inference. Outputs describe relative persistent conditioning-film/load potential under comparable small applications; they do not measure accumulation across washes or guarantee cleansing. This trial refines operational consistency, not scientific validation.

B0. Read the complete literal declaration and reviewed exact material functions. Preserve plant part, derivative and grade distinctions; do not chemically repair a spelling. An unresolved product identity or incomplete declaration blocks completion. For a complete identified formula, undisclosed material grades inform the rough choice and its low confidence rather than producing unknown. Rank is order, not a percentage, top-N rule or 1% boundary.

B0a. Before full-formula estimation, match the separate material build-up reference by exact whole-oil identity and reviewed aliases. A complete single-ingredient whole-oil declaration may inherit an explicitly accepted rough prior, retaining estimate:true, low confidence, alternative and counter-signals. Whole jojoba has low under OE51. For blends the prior is only a constituent clue; complete architecture decides the finished product. An unassessed material, derivative, contrary source evidence or decisive grade gap follows B1–B4. This is a new human-approved approximation, not a measured result or automatic weight mapping.

B1. Judge the complete residual conditioning architecture. Plant/silicone origin, oil count, material weight, penetration, missing gum and manufacturer claims never decide build-up. An aqueous vehicle is not automatically low; a mobile/volatile carrier does not cancel a substantial retained conditioning film.

B2. Elevated needs affirmative support for a coherent substantial gum/resin/wax/polymer conditioning film. A structured or rich oil coating can contribute only with admitted material-function support, not merely coconut/castor presence or a botanical-only base. Challenge whether the film signal is secondary, whether the grade is unknown, and whether a thickener actually serves aqueous rheology rather than hair conditioning. One late gum is not sufficient by itself. Every elevated result is an estimate with low confidence, not measured accumulation.

B3. Low is a practical whole-system estimate when the mobile fluid-emollient reading makes a less substantial residual coating the more plausible direction. Examine every retained conditioning component and challenge the strongest elevated reading, including possible wax grades. Spreading, generic emollience, silicone-free positioning, missing gum and light weight do not establish low persistence; if these are the available clues, state that the choice is a weak hypothesis. Low is not zero residue or easy washout.

B4. For every complete identified formula choose the more plausible low or elevated direction after comparing both residual-system readings. When evidence is close or grades unresolved, still make the best rough judgment with estimate:true, confidence:low, the opposite class as nearest alternative and all named gaps. Explain what assumption tips the choice; there is no universal low default or automatic weight/ingredient-presence mapping. Unassessed material references remain unassessed and must not be converted into invented facts. Historical unknowns remain in frozen records. Other properties and missing-source completion gates are unchanged.

B5. Each result includes value (low/elevated for complete-formula product estimates), estimate:true, confidence low, exact ingredient rank/name evidence, counter-signals, nearest alternative, reason and unresolved gaps. Ingredient ledger covers every primary rank exactly once with roles copied from the frozen lexicon; contextual footnotes are retained separately, not converted into independently ranked ingredients. Do not infer parent fragrance percentages from its constituent sublist.

B6. Determine cohort eligibility from source-preflight evidence using the unchanged category charter, which includes water-based oil-labelled serums. Do not exclude a record because water/glycols lead. Source identities and product directions are revealed only after blind outputs are sealed; directions affect use relevance rather than rewriting the formula build-up estimate.

Previously accepted product decisions remain separate exact-formula precedents. OE51 additionally admits the narrowly scoped whole-jojoba material prior above; no broader material defaults or blind target answers follow. No automatic coconut/castor/ester/weight-to-build-up mapping is introduced. Any guidance revision after seeing this trial requires a new version/run; this method remains frozen.
