## v0.4 — model trim on Nick's rulings T1–T9 — 2026-09-05

Standard `leave-in-inci-v0.4` (`leave-in-classification-standard.v0.4.md`). v0.3 is retained unchanged as the frozen round-3 rule set; v0.2 and v0.1 remain the frozen round-2 and round-1 sets.

**Why this version exists.** Calibration round 3 re-ran both lanes on the same 13-product gold set under v0.3, from a fully frozen packet with no web research in either lane: **398 fields, 97.5 % exact agreement, 10 disagreements** (86.2 % → 93.0 % → 97.5 %). v0.4 is **not** a repair pass on that result — **none of the four round-3 items is addressed here**, and all four stay open. v0.4 is a **model trim**: after three rounds it is visible which dimensions the standard can actually defend as separately reviewable values, and the ontology was carrying more surface than either the evidence or a matching model needs. Nick issued rulings **T1–T8** on 2026-09-05, a further ruling **T9** the same day, and a further ruling **T10** the same day again, each folded into this same still-unfrozen draft; all ten are the sole authority for this version.

**What v0.4 does not do.** No science absent from the leave-on science review. No product-specific rule. **No anchor's evidence bar moved and no round-3 value was re-derived.** Every entry below removes a field, re-homes an observation the model already carried, or changes which items a human reviews. The reference key's v4 run is a **mechanical transform** of the round-3 key (`derived_from_run: reference-key-2026-09-04-r3`) containing no re-classification.

### Dropped dimensions

**1. SLIP dropped as a property (§7.2, §7.3, §6; T1).** `slip_combability_potential` and its bias qualifier are removed. The observation is absorbed into COND's evidence object as the **absorbed slip observation**: it records which M1 contributors are present as architecture and, where the architecture settles it, the wet/dry bias reading — and it **may not move the COND value**, because it is by construction the same M1 evidence the COND anchor already counted (G3). The two consumers that read a SLIP value now name the observation instead, at thresholds written out as the v0.3 anchors they encode: the `detangling` focus row (§10.2) and the `texture_fit` row-3/4/5 modifier (§10.3). **No product's `detangling` or `texture_fit` value changes.**
*Motivated by:* SLIP was already a *shared* property capped at `moderate`, reading the same deposit through the same mechanism as COND, and §7.3 itself said scoring wet and dry apart is the violation G3 exists to prevent. The residual half proved unreadable at scale — the bias qualifier returned `unknown` on 8 of 12 profiled round-2 records — while open gap §17.5 (whether wet and dry slip separate from formula at all) is unresolved. A separately reviewable score on that evidence base invites a reviewer to adjudicate a value the standard cannot defend. *Traces to:* Nick trim ruling T1.

**2. SFR descored into `smoothing_route` (§7.4, §7.4.1, §10.2, §10.2.1; T2).** `ambient_smoothing_alignment_potential` stops being a scored, separately reviewable dimension. It becomes a **typed trace input** — `silicone_film` / `cationic_alignment` / `emollient` / `fixative_film` / `none` — derived from the existing SFR and §6 mechanism evidence, i.e. a classification of the observation rather than a new judgment. The v0.3 two-observation anchor is retained verbatim as §7.4.1 and becomes the `smoothing` focus row's test, so **focus results do not move on the descoring**. `smoothing_route` appears only inside the focus row's evidence and is never projected (G14). The word "frizz" stays absent for the reason it was always absent (§7.9).
*Motivated by:* SFR's ceiling was `moderate`, its `high` value existed only to qualify one focus row, and v0.3 had already had to resolve an anchor/G3 contradiction in G3's favour to stop it double-counting COND's own film. What the model needed from it was *which* route, not *how much*. *Traces to:* Nick trim ruling T2.

**3. DOSE dropped entirely (§7.11, §7.5, §10.1.1, §10.3, §18; T3).** No dimension, no stored derivation, no `derived_from` entry, and the §18 caution string („Reagiert empfindlich auf die Menge …") is deleted. The two-phase dose-**variability** note, the §18 shake caution and the §14 two-phase review trigger survive attached to FORM, unchanged.
*Motivated by:* DOSE was by its own definition a restatement of the WT/M1 observation — that is why v0.2 made it derived rather than assessed and why v0.3 had to stop FORM setting it a second time — and its only output was one dosing sentence. The app's existing application-guidance layer already carries general dosing advice, so the standard was paying a field, a derivation rule, a projection rule and a user-facing string for a per-product assertion it derived from nothing WT had not already said. Open gap §17.1 stays open, re-stated as a limitation on WT, PERS and buildup; §17.26 records that "the app already says it" is a product fact this standard does not own. *Traces to:* Nick trim ruling T3.

**4. `scalp_application_fit` dropped from the model (§10.3.1, §2.4, §14, §16, §18, G1; T4).** The field, both its §18 strings and both its §14 triggers are removed. EXPO and its alcohol note and string, ROLE `ends_only`, G6 and the standing "root or scalp application, or any medically adjacent framing" review trigger are all unchanged.
*Motivated by:* three things the standard had already written about itself. The repo separates cosmetic guidance from medically adjacent scalp guidance, and a scalp placement verdict projected into a matching profile blurs that line. The field resolved to `unknown` or `avoid` on almost every record — v0.3 had to narrow the `avoid` trigger because a declared fragrance allergen fired it on nine of twelve profiled records, and `suitable_if_evidenced` was reached by nothing. And an exposure flag is not a tolerance prediction (§7.12, SR §M.12), which is why the field could never earn a positive value from formula. *Traces to:* Nick trim ruling T4.

### Emission and review surface

**5. The six demoted flags collapse into one `hinweise` record (§8, §8.6; T5).** SHN, CURL, R3, LAYER, buildup and transfer no longer occupy six always-present slots. One conditional record per product lists **only the flags that fired**, each with its one-line note; the record is emitted empty when nothing fired, so "no Hinweise" and "Hinweise not researched" stay distinguishable. §8.1–§8.5 are unchanged and still decide whether a flag fires. **R3's full enum and its review routing survive inside the record**: `r3_state` is always carried, including `none`, and only a non-`none` state appears in `fired`.
*Motivated by:* five of the six flags were empty on most products, and six always-present rows made a reviewer read five "nothing here" entries to find the one that mattered. The collapse is an emission change, not an evidence change: no flag gains or loses a state. *Traces to:* Nick trim ruling T5.

**6. Lean-profile echo fields stop being separately reviewable (§10.1.3, §10.1; T8).** `product_form`, `conditioning_level`, `weight_potential`, `persistence`, `hold_support` and `usage_role` are deterministic identity projections of exactly one dimension. Each is shown as an annotation on its dimension's row („→ Profilwert: rich"), and approving the dimension approves the projection. `cautions[]` and `uncertain_fields[]` are echoes of named field values and are displayed with the record rather than reviewed line by line. **Still reviewable:** the fits (thickness ×3, damage ×3, texture ×4), `focus.primary`, `focus.secondary`, the heat binary (a *policy* projection that can differ from the state producing it), `care_direction`, `repair_support_level`, the `hinweise` record, `g0_state` and every §7 dimension. *(T8 as first issued also named `specialist_functions.humidity_resistance` ← HUM as a seventh echo field, reviewed as the HUM row. Entry 9 below removes HUM entirely, so there is no seventh echo left — not a re-annotation, a deletion.)*
*Motivated by:* asking a human the same question twice invites the two answers to diverge, which is the failure mode G14 exists to prevent. **No value, anchor, projection or emission changes — only the review surface.** *Traces to:* Nick trim ruling T8.

### Removed field (T9 addendum, same day)

**9. HUM dropped entirely (§7.9, §2.4, §2.4.1, §4, §5 L4, §6, §10, §10.1, §10.1.3, G13, §14, §18; T9).** `humidity_resistance_evidence_state` and its lean-profile echo `specialist_functions.humidity_resistance` are removed — no dimension, no lean-profile field, no state ladder, no quoted-claim adoption. Unlike SLIP (entry 1, absorbed into COND) and SFR (entry 2, descored into `smoothing_route`), **HUM is not re-homed as an observation anywhere** — it carries no new content into any other field. Its row in the §4 mandatory-counter-signal table, its three §18 strings, and its entries in G13's claim-authority field list are all removed with it. A `humidity_frizz`-typed entry already frozen in a product's `claims[]` (§2.4) is **untouched, unconsumed upstream data** — the capture machinery is not edited; the model simply has no field left to project it into.
*Motivated by:* three things, stated together. **(1)** An unverifiable manufacturer claim is not a comparison axis this standard can defend — HUM's ladder above `not_claimed` was reachable only via a C1/C2 claim, and `product_tested` never once fired across three calibration rounds, the same open state HEAT's own `product_tested` carries (§17.8). **(2)** The anti-frizz *user need* HUM partly served is already reachable, without ever asserting humidity performance, through the `smoothing` focus (§10.2, fed by `smoothing_route`, §7.4) — ambient-condition surface alignment is the mechanistically-defensible part of "less frizz" a formula can actually support. **(3)** HEAT becomes the only claim-led field this standard has (§2.4.1, G13): HUM was built as HEAT's mirror, its claim requirement was already reversed once on its own reasoning (v0.2 → v0.3, §21.1 change 14 / §21.2 change 9 in the standard), and reaching `formula_plausible` on at most one gold-set product per round is not evidence the second claim-led ladder was worth repairing again. The humectant-is-not-anti-frizz guardrail is not lost with the field: it survives generally as **FS-6/FS-15**, binding any future consumer of a humectant observation rather than a HUM-specific mandatory counter-signal. *Traces to:* Nick trim ruling T9.

### Presentation form (T10 addendum, same day)

**10. `product_form` becomes the presentation form, not the FORM architecture class (§7.1, §10, §10.1, §10.1.3, §10.3, §17; T10).** The lean profile's `product_form` stops projecting FORM's architecture value (`aqueous_or_hydroalcoholic_solution` / `emulsion` / `microemulsion` / `two_phase`) and instead carries the **presentation form** — what the user actually holds and uses: `spray | milk | lotion | cream | serum`, `unknown` where identity capture cannot settle it. It is captured independently at identity (E1: pack, exact product name, directions of use) — the same evidence G1 already requires — not derived from FORM. FORM's own architecture value flips to trace-only and is never projected. A `two_phase` architecture typically presents as `spray` — every `two_phase` product in the round-3 gold set does — but this is recorded as an **observed correlation, not a derivation rule**: nothing mechanically sets `product_form` from FORM, ever. `product_form` is removed from the T8 echo-field list (§10.1.3) — it was never a deterministic projection of a single dimension once T10 lands — and moves to the separately-reviewable list beside `care_direction`, since it is its own identity-level read rather than someone else's projection. `hair_thickness_fit`'s stale `derived_from: [weight_potential, product_form]` reference (§10.3) is corrected to `[weight_potential]`: the table never actually used `product_form`, and keeping it would now misstate the rule, since §7.1 itself already says the presentation word "carries no decision weight". **The "vor Gebrauch gut schütteln" shake instruction is explicitly not moved by this ruling.** It is a per-product handling instruction, not a format fact, and belongs conceptually in the app's application-guidance/protocol layer as a per-product step — but this standard does not build that layer. The §18 shake string and the §14 two-phase review trigger stay exactly where T3 left them, attached to `FORM = two_phase`, unchanged; the migration is recorded as an open handover item for the production adapter (§17 item 27), not designed here.
*Motivated by:* the standard's own §7.1 had always said the presentation word "carries no decision weight" and treated it as metadata, while simultaneously making the *architecture* class the profile's `product_form` value — the field a downstream matching consumer would reasonably expect to answer "what does the user hold", not "what colloidal system is this". A production adapter or recommendation surface asking "is this a spray" cannot be answered from `aqueous_or_hydroalcoholic_solution` / `emulsion` / `microemulsion` / `two_phase` without an undocumented, product-specific lookup — exactly the kind of silent inference G4 and G14 exist to bar. Flipping which side of the FORM/presentation pair is trace-only and which is projected fixes that without touching any evidence bar: it is a re-naming of *which observation the profile answers*, not a re-derivation of either one. It also brings the field back into line with HO §11's own original vocabulary (`spray | milk | lotion | cream`) for this slot, which the v0.1 divergence had moved away from; `serum` substitutes for HO §11's `two_phase` because `two_phase` names an architecture, not a thing a user holds. *Traces to:* Nick trim ruling T10.

### FORM stops being a scored dimension (T11 addendum, 2026-09-10)

**11. FORM demoted from a scored dimension to a trace reading convention (§3.1.2, §7.1, §6, §7.5, §10.1, §11 G9; T11).** `product_form_architecture` is removed from §7 entirely: no row, no property-evidence object, no confidence band, no ceiling of its own. Nothing about the *content* moves. The **presentation form** T10 already made independent — the profile's `product_form`, captured at identity — is untouched by this ruling; T11 only concerns the half T10 had already made trace-only. The **architecture taxonomy** — the four in-category architectures plus the anhydrous exclusion, the decision order, the solution↔microemulsion threshold and the LGN/non-LGN notes, **including the microemulsion/two-phase reading rules, which survive verbatim** — is retained in full as a new **§3.1.2**, stated alongside §3.1.1's tail-marker convention. It is consumed **inline**, in the consuming rule's own `threshold_reasoning[]`, wherever a rule already needed it:

- **G0's `excluded_anhydrous` rationale (§2.3) is unchanged.** G0 already carried its own architecture evidence directly ("No Aqua, or Aqua absent from the top of the list; cyclomethicone/dimethicone/oils lead") and never depended on a separate FORM row to decide the boundary.
- **WT's ceiling and both G9 clauses (§7.5)** now read "the architecture read (§3.1.2)" where they previously read "FORM" — same anchors, same thresholds, same gate. The two-phase dose-variability note, the §18 shake caution and the §14 two-phase review trigger move from a standalone FORM record into WT's own G9 clause 3, unchanged in substance.
- **COND's routes (§7.2)** and the §6 `M6_VOLATILE_CARRIER` mechanism feed the architecture read the same way they always did; only the label of what they feed changes.

Scored dimensions drop from **9 to 8**. Section numbering §7.1 is retained, on the same pattern §7.3 (SLIP), §7.4 (SFR), §7.9 (HUM) and §7.11 (DOSE) already use, so earlier citations resolve and no reader mistakes a demotion for an oversight.

*Motivated by:* T10 had already done the substantive work — FORM's architecture value became trace-only and never projected, leaving `product_form` (the presentation form) as the only part of the dimension the lean profile actually used. A dimension whose value is never projected, and whose only remaining job is to feed reasoning inside the rows that consume it (G0, WT, COND), does not need its own reviewable row, confidence band or ceiling — it needs to be read correctly wherever it is used, which is what a reading convention is for. This is the same kind of demotion T2 already made for SFR (a typed trace input, `smoothing_route`, rather than a scored dimension) and T8 made for the lean-profile echo fields (an annotation on their dimension's row, rather than a separately reviewable item) — applied here to the one dimension T10 had already made trace-only. No anchor's evidence bar moves and no product's projected value changes. *Traces to:* Nick trim ruling T11.

### ROLE dropped as a scored dimension; application timing captured as identity (T12 addendum, 2026-09-10)

**12. ROLE removed as a scored dimension and a profile property; application timing becomes identity capture (§7.13, §2.4, §7, §10, §10.1, §10.1.3, §10.2, §10.3.1, §11 G1/G13, §14, §16, §17; T12).** `usage_role[]` — `post_wash` · `refresh` · `heat_styling` · `curl_styling` · `ends_only`, scored and projected into the lean matching profile — is removed entirely: no dimension, no lean-profile field, no T8 echo-field entry. The five roles are disposed of individually, not blanket-dropped:

- **`post_wash` is dropped, carrying no information.** It was true for nearly every in-category leave-in and never discriminated between products.
- **`heat_styling` is dropped as a role and is not re-homed.** The production heat binary (`provides_heat_protection`) already requires a `pre_heat` direction to reach `formula_plausible`, so the role restated evidence the heat evidence state and the `heat_styling` focus already required. The **`heat_styling` focus itself stays** (§10.2) and is re-keyed to fire on `provides_heat_protection = true` **and** `application_stage` including `pre_heat` — the same evidence shape, on the production binary and the new identity field instead of the trace state and the removed role.
- **`curl_styling` is dropped, carrying no information the `curl_definition` focus's own curl/wave positioning gate did not already require directly (§10.2).**
- **`ends_only` becomes an application-guidance note**, not identity data and not a score — the same undesigned layer the two-phase shake instruction already belongs to (§17 item 27).
- **`refresh`'s one informative bit — dry-hair usability — survives**, not as a scored role but as **identity data**: `application_stage: dry_hair`, transcribed at G1 (§2.4) exactly like a GTIN or a pack format.

`application_stage[]` is a **new identity-level field**, captured at G1 next to directions, using the vocabulary production already consumes on the DB's leave-in specs: `towel_dry | dry_hair | pre_heat | post_style`. It has no anchor, no ceiling and no confidence band — it is transcribed, not assessed, and never derived from formula. **It is sourced from G1's general directions hierarchy, not ROLE's C1/C2 claim-tier gate** — the one deliberate divergence from the T9–T11 removal pattern, where each successor field inherited its predecessor's sourcing rule unchanged: `application_stage` is identity data, transcribed the way a GTIN or pack format already is, not a scored or claim-keyed field, so it carries none of the extra claim-authority restriction ROLE needed. **Ambiguous directions route to review as a documented identity conflict**, exactly as a source or formula conflict already does; otherwise there is nothing to review per product, because a clean transcription of a clean direction is not a reviewable judgment. `provides_heat_protection` is unaffected by this ruling — it was never role-derived (§13.3); only the `heat_styling` focus reads both it and `application_stage`. Scored dimensions drop from **8 to 7**. Section numbering §7.13 is retained, on the same pattern §7.1 (FORM), §7.3 (SLIP), §7.4 (SFR), §7.9 (HUM) and §7.11 (DOSE) already use, so earlier citations resolve and no reader mistakes a removal for an oversight.

*Motivated by:* three things, stated together, and a fourth about what survives. **(1)** `post_wash` was true for nearly every in-category product — it is not a comparison axis, the same shape that motivated dropping DOSE (T3) and HUM (T9). **(2)** `heat_styling` is derivable from evidence the model already captures: the production heat binary already requires a `pre_heat` direction, so scoring a role from the same direction sentence double-stated a fact. **(3)** `ends_only` is application *guidance*, not a property a matching model scores — it belongs with the shake instruction in the app's existing application-guidance layer, the same reasoning T3 applied to DOSE's dosing sentence. **(4)** The one bit that was genuinely informative — whether a product's own directions permit dry-hair use — is not discarded with the rest: it survives as `application_stage`, captured judgment-free at identity exactly the way T10 already captured the presentation form at identity rather than deriving it. *Traces to:* Nick trim ruling T12.

### Repair-row marketing prong deleted; §10.2.1 permissive reading adopted (T13, 2026-09-10)

**13. The `repair` focus row's marketing-position prong is deleted; §10.2.1's beyond-baseline-conditioning test resolves permissively (§10.2, §10.2.1; T13).** Two independent findings, adjudicated together while reviewing the GLISS gold-set record:

**(a) Positioning never creates a focus.** Every version through this v0.4 draft let `repair` qualify on either R2 ∈ {`candidate`, `tested`} (§7.10) **or** protein/silane actives named in the product's C1/C2 marketing position. The second prong contradicted §10.2 principle 4 ("Official positioning may corroborate but never creates a route") on its own terms — a route created by positioning alone, over a formula whose only protein is a plain hydrolysate, is exactly what principle 4 forbids. The contradiction was recorded but not resolved as early as the round-1/round-2 defect passes (the standard's own §10.2 note that the row and §7.10 no longer "contradict" addressed a different overlap — the R2 `candidate` definition, not this prong). **Resolved in principle 4's favour.** The prong is deleted from the anchor table, the rank-order precondition and the R3-secondary-focus allowance; `repair` now requires R2 ∈ {`candidate`, `tested`} and nothing else, as both primary and secondary focus.

**(b) The over-satisfaction question resolves permissively.** §10.2.1 defines "beyond baseline conditioning" for the ordinary case (one admissible COND-establishing set) but left open what happens when COND's anchor is over-satisfied — more than one admissible set could independently have established it. Round 3's two lanes split on exactly this: the reference-key lane read conservatively (beyond baseline under **every** admissible set), the blind lane permissively (beyond baseline under **any one**), and the split alone decided `focus.primary` general-vs-smoothing on four gold-set slots plus one slot's secondary focus — the largest remaining round-3 disagreement (round-3 report, item 1). **Nick adopts the permissive reading: a single admissible COND-establishing set suffices.** Rationale, in Nick's own phrasing: it matches product identity where the formula supports a dedicated route (Olaplex's own "Bond Smoother" naming is the clearest case), and it keeps `general` meaningful for genuinely unfocused products rather than for products that merely have more than one way to reach their COND value.

**Consequence on the gold set.** Both rulings apply together to every in-category record. Four records change `focus.primary`/`focus.secondary` value (`plans/leave-in-inci/research/gold-set/reference-key-v4/transform-notes.md` §12 carries the per-product table): slot 8 (Schwarzkopf GLISS) moves `repair` → `smoothing` — its only route to `repair` was the deleted prong, and its silicone film system (Dimethicone/Phenyl Trimethicone/Dimethiconol + apricot kernel oil lubrication) clears the smoothing route's two-observation test and, under the permissive reading, is beyond baseline conditioning; `repair` does not survive as a secondary focus either, since it never had an independent route. Slots 2 (ISANA), 6 (Curlsmith) and 10 (Olaplex) move `general` → `smoothing` — each carries a two-observation smoothing route (§7.4.1) that was blocked only by the conservative reading, which the permissive reading now clears. Slot 9 (Redken) was independently considered — its historical repair-candidate shape also touched the marketing prong in an earlier round — but its current record's R2 is `none_visible` (the qualifying silane sits below the tail marker) and its smoothing route fails the two-observation test outright (only one observation survives above the marker), so it stays `general` regardless of either ruling; this is re-derived and documented, not assumed unchanged. The remaining in-category records (slots 1, 3, 4, 5, 11, 13) are re-checked against both rulings and are unaffected — none had a marketing-only repair route, and none has a smoothing route that clears the two-observation test.

*Motivated by:* T13 is the one ruling in the v0.4 series that is not a scope trim — it is a direct adjudication of round-3 report item 1 and a defect found while applying the standard to a real record, resolved the way R11–R14 (round-1) and the round-2/round-3 defect passes were: by naming the contradiction, choosing a side, and re-deriving every affected record. Neither half moves an anchor's evidence bar or introduces new evidence; both are policy calls about how the existing evidence is read and combined. *Traces to:* Nick binding ruling T13; round-3 report item 1.

### Cross-market manufacturer claim exception (T14, 2026-09-10)

**14. §2.4.1's claim-authority operating rule 3 gains a narrow, identity-gated cross-market exception, adjudicated against the Olaplex gold-set record (§2.4.1 rule 7, new tier `C2_cross_market_verified`; §13.2, §13.3, §11 G13; T14).** Through this point in v0.4, rule 3 read every non-German-market manufacturer page as C5, full stop — a US or other foreign brand page could never create a claim for the German market, no matter how well the product's identity was otherwise pinned down. Reviewing the Olaplex N°.6 Bond Smoother gold-set record exposed the cost: its „450ºF/232ºC" heat-protection claim is genuinely the manufacturer's own claim, made identically on olaplex.com and the EU-facing es.olaplex.com page, and corroborated by douglas.de (C3) — but under rule 3 alone it could never rise above `claim_authority_gap`, because no page addressed specifically to the German market carries it. Nick ruled a **narrow** exception rather than reopening rule 3 generally: a non-German-market manufacturer page **can** create a claim, **but only when both (a) product identity is verified** — the German-sold unit's formula fingerprint is identical to the claimed product's own published INCI (set-for-set, order noise aside), or a shared GTIN is confirmed at a German retailer — **and (b) at least one German-market retailer (C3/C4) corroborates the claim.** Such claims are recorded at a new tier, `C2_cross_market_verified`, with the identity evidence (the formula diff or the GTIN match, with its own source) attached to the claim record; they are still E0 like every other claim (G4) and are consumed by HEAT (§13.2, §13.3) exactly as a C1/C2 claim is. **Without identity verification, rule 3 stands unchanged** — corroboration alone never substitutes for identity, because corroboration under the ordinary reading only ever corroborates an *existing* C1/C2 claim.

**The guarding counter-example: Cantu.** Cantu's own DE-pack formula (dm.de, gold-set slot 3) and its US-market formula are already documented as genuinely different formulas under different GTINs (`known_conflicts`, same record) — a materially different cationic/emollient architecture, not a labelling variant. Whatever a US or other non-German Cantu page says about heat protection, clause (a) fails for it on the evidence already on file: identity across markets is not shown, so this exception cannot apply, and Cantu's heat statement stays exactly what rule 3's ordinary reading already made it — C3-only (dm.de), non-creating, `not_claimed`. The exception is written narrowly enough that Cantu's record needed no change of its own to demonstrate the limit; the counter-example is stated in the rule text itself (§2.4.1 rule 7) rather than requiring a new capture.

**Consequence on the Olaplex gold-set record (slot 10).** `dimensions.heat_protection_evidence_state` moves `not_claimed` → `claim_only`: the closed L9 list is checked against the formula and finds no member (Dimethicone and Phenyl Trimethicone are generic silicones, not L9 species), so `formula_plausible` is not reached — the formula sanity-check runs exactly as §13.3 rule 2 requires for any other claim-only record. `profile.specialist_functions.provides_heat_protection` moves `false` → `true`. The record's `claim_authority_gap` review trigger — which existed precisely because no C1/C2 source could be found — is resolved, dated 2026-09-10, now that a `C2_cross_market_verified` source has been; it is replaced in `review_routing.triggers` by the standing `heat_claim_without_l9_member` trigger (§13.3 rule 2, §14), the same trigger every other claim-only-without-L9 record in this gold set already carries (GLISS, Redken, Neqi). The new §18 caution string for that trigger — „Hitzeschutz ist ausgelobt; in der Rezeptur finden wir dafür keinen belegten Wirkstoff. Wir prüfen das." — is added to the record's `cautions_de`. `profile.focus.primary`/`focus.secondary` are **not** re-derived by this change: the `heat_styling` focus route still requires `application_stage` to include `pre_heat` (§10.2), and Olaplex's frozen `directions_of_use` supports only `towel_dry` — so the route fails on the application-stage prong exactly as it did before, and `focus.primary` stays `smoothing` (T13). Full per-field detail: `plans/leave-in-inci/research/gold-set/reference-key-v4/transform-notes.md` §14.

**The `heat_retailer_tier_only` open adjudication is retired, not merely reworded.** The fixture build's adjudication banner previously flagged two records — Olaplex (slot 10) and Cantu (slot 3) — under one shared "heat claim exists only at retailer tier" question. Nick has now ruled both halves: Olaplex's resolves via the cross-market exception above, and Cantu's resolves by the exception's own guarding counter-example (identity gate fails, rule 3's ordinary `not_claimed`/`claim_authority_gap` reading stands, which is exactly what the record already carried). With both halves settled, nothing remains for a reviewer to adjudicate — Cantu's outcome is now a documented rule application, not an open question — so the banner and its two slot-level assignments are removed from `scripts/leave-in-research/build-lab-fixture.mjs` rather than reworded to name Cantu alone, on the same precedent T13b used to retire `focus_permissive_vs_conservative` once its question became a settled rule.

*Motivated by:* the standard's C5 treatment of non-German pages (rule 3) was right as a default and wrong as an absolute — it could not distinguish "we have no idea if this is the same product" from "we have verified this is the same product, sold globally, with a German retailer standing behind the same claim." The exception is scoped tightly enough (both identity *and* corroboration, not either alone) that it cannot be satisfied by marketing copy or retailer enthusiasm on its own, which is exactly what rule 3 exists to guard against. *Traces to:* Nick binding ruling T14, adjudicated during the Olaplex gold-set review.

### Repair and hold

**7. `repair_support_level` added (§10.3.2, §10.1, §14; T6).** A three-state output — `low` | `medium` | `high` — aligned with the conditioner engine's `repair_level` consumer, so one downstream consumer serves both categories. ### Tail-marker plausibility conditional (T16, 2026-09-11)

**16. §3.1.1's tail-marker rank prong becomes conditional on the marker's own plausibility, adjudicated against the Redken gold-set record (§3.1.1 clause 5, §7.10, §10.3.2).** Through this point in v0.4, §3.1.1 clause 1 made rank the single deterministic prong of "present as architecture" unconditionally: any ingredient at or below the marker was disqualified from anchor purposes, however early or unreliable the marker itself was. Reviewing the Redken Extreme Anti-Snap Leave-In Treatment gold-set record (slot 9) exposed the cost: its marker, Phenoxyethanol, sits at rank 3 of 24 — **before** the product's own main conditioning silicone, Amodimethicone, at rank 4 — which is not a real 1 % boundary, it is a listing artifact that would place the product's own headline conditioning ingredient in the unordered sub-1 % tail. Both calibration lanes had independently flagged this record as the tail-marker stress case (§17.14, §17.18) without a rule to act on it. Nick ruled a **conditional** prong rather than reopening §3.1.1 clause 1 generally: the strict-rank prong (clause 1) and the below-marker routing (clause 3, §7.10's `candidate_below_tail` and its §7.6/§7.9 siblings) may disqualify a route or ingredient **only when the marker is plausible** — checkably defined as sitting *after* every ingredient that establishes the product's COND (§7.2) and WT (§7.5) values. Where the marker sits *before* that architecture, it is **implausible**: it may not be used to disqualify any route or ingredient on that record, which instead is read on its own merits, and the record carries a `tail_marker_implausible` note and **routes to human review** (§14) in place of the below-marker mechanisms. The plausibility test is itself a rank comparison, so determinism is preserved — it is not a judgment call. **Applied to the Redken gold-set record (slot 9) only:** `repair_surface_film` `none_visible` → `candidate` (the silane, Hydrolyzed Vegetable Protein PG-Propyl Silanetriol at r14, is no longer below-marker-disqualified); `repair_support_level` `low` → `medium` under §10.3.2's own unchanged rule — the gold set's first record to exercise the `medium` row; `damage_fit` opens R14's repair-film path (row 3b: `healthy` `recommended` → `conditional`, `highly_damaged` `conditional` → `recommended`, `moderately_damaged` unchanged); `focus.primary` `general` → `repair` (a dedicated repair route now exists, §10.2/T13a); `care_direction` `moisture` → `protein` (§9's protein anchor is now met). Its `candidate_below_tail` review trigger is replaced by the standing `tail_marker_implausible` trigger, with a dated resolution note. **Every other in-category record is independently re-checked against the same plausibility test and stays unchanged** — the marker in each case sits after the ingredients establishing its own COND/WT (Olaplex's LGN pair at r2/r7 above its marker at r14; GLISS's silicone package at r3/r5/r8 above its marker at r14; Curlsmith's LGN pair at r3/r6 above its very-late marker at r36; and so on for the rest, documented per-slot in `reference-key-v4/transform-notes.md`) — so no other record's marker is implausible and no other value moves. This resolves the `repair_support_level_uncalibrated` adjudication: the `medium` row is now demonstrably reachable, not merely an applied-but-untested rule; `openAdjudications` drops to zero. *Motivated by:* the gold set's designed protein/surface-repair archetype (slot 9) sitting on the tail-marker stress case both calibration lanes had already flagged; §17.14, §17.18. *Traces to:* Nick binding ruling T16.

### Hard-rule audit adoption, seven items (T17, 2026-09-11)

**17. The conservative-failure invariant, and seven items from the hard-rule audit Nick commissioned against T16, adopted together (§1.1, §2.3.2, §2.4, §3.1.1, §7.6, §8.5, §14; T17).** T16 fixed one hard rule that failed toward a confident wrong value on an implausible input instead of toward review or the conservative default. Nick asked whether any other hard rule in the standard had the same shape; the resulting audit (`plans/leave-in-inci/research/hard-rule-audit.md`) rated every hard threshold, closed enumeration and gate in the standard against the gold set and its own residual-ambiguity registers, and recommended seven items as cheap, evidence-backed, and worth adopting at freeze without waiting for an unseen-product test. Nick adopted all seven, plus the general test itself as new §1.1: **"Every hard rule must fail toward review or toward the conservative value — never toward a recommendation."**
- **H1 (§3.1.1 clause 6).** A tail marker so late that everything at or below it is only capped material, fragrance/allergen declarations or colourants is **vacuous** — it separates nothing, and cannot establish "present as architecture" for any anchor on rank alone. The record carries `tail_marker: vacuous` and routes to review (`tail_marker_vacuous`). Symmetric with T16's clause 5: neither an implausibly-early nor an implausibly-late marker may disqualify **or** qualify anything on rank alone.
- **H2 (§3.1.1's marker list).** The declared 26 EU fragrance allergens are removed from the marker-eligible list — declared last by EU convention, they guaranteed a near-terminal marker and pre-empted the honest `none_visible` escape mandatory limit 2 already defines for preservative-free formulas. A formula whose only near-terminal declaration was the allergen block now reads `tail_marker: none_visible`.
- **H3 (§2.4 rule 2).** The rinse test is scoped to the direction governing the product's own application step. A safety/hazard sentence never fires it; a dual-use "leave-on or rinse" direction keeps the record in-category (`dual_use_directions`, routed to review) instead of `excluded_other_form` — the least recoverable, no-lean-profile output in the standard, previously reachable with no escape at all.
- **H6 (§8.5/§7.6).** The buildup caution is emitted when `weight_potential` projects `high` with a persistent non-volatile family present as architecture, in addition to `persistence` projecting `permanent_cationic`. Closes a user-visible inversion: a heavy, monomeric-quat-led leave-in previously carried no buildup caution while a much lighter cationic-polymer spray did. `persistence_removal_class`'s own projected class is untouched (G3 rule 4).
- **H8 (§14).** New trigger `l9_member_without_claim` — an L9 closed-list member above the tail marker with no C1/C2 heat claim routes to review instead of silently emitting `provides_heat_protection: false` with nobody checking whether the manufacturer source was simply not found. The binary itself is unchanged (claim-led policy, ruling 6); the mirror trigger (a claim with no L9 member) already existed and had fired four times.
- **H9 (§2.3.2).** A cross-rule consistency clause: one species at one rank read as substantive architecture by one rule (§2.3.2/§7.7) and as disqualified trace by another (§7.10) routes to review (`species_reading_conflict`) instead of silently carrying both readings. Neither reading is overridden.
- **Housekeeping (§14).** Formal definitions added for the five tail-marker trigger names the reference key already used with no definition anywhere in the standard (`tail_marker_dependence`, `very_early_tail_marker`, `early_tail_marker`, `very_late_tail_marker`, `tail_marker_allergen_block_only` — the last superseded by H2) plus the new triggers H1/H3/H9 add — the structural gap the audit's own opening observation names: nine of thirteen gold-set records routed on a marker condition the standard had no defined trigger for.

**Applied to the reference-key-v4 gold set, mechanically, per §21.3 row 17 and `reference-key-v4/transform-notes.md` §20–21:** slots 6 and 12 gain the vacuous-marker note and trigger (H1); slot 4 loses its allergen-block marker, reading `none_visible` with a one-step confidence reduction on five dimensions per mandatory limit 2 (H2); slots 3 and 10 gain the buildup caution (H6); slot 13 gains `species_reading_conflict` — Silicone Quaternium-18 at r11 is T15's named substantive HOLD architecture and simultaneously §7.10's `candidate_below_tail` disqualified trace, on the same record (H9). H3 and H8 are defined but unexercised on this gold set — no captured direction or L9/claim shape matches either condition. **No dimension or profile value moves toward a recommendation on any of the thirteen records** — every change is a new caution, a new review trigger, or a confidence/marker-reading correction that leaves the underlying value where it already was.

*Motivated by:* Nick's own question after T16 — "which other rules have that shape?" — and the audit's structural finding that the tail-marker trigger names alone were already lane conventions the standard had never formalised. *Traces to:* `plans/leave-in-inci/research/hard-rule-audit.md`; Nick binding ruling T17.

### Same-market formula-set conflicts: convergence first, conservative fallback otherwise (T18, 2026-09-12)

**18. A two-tier precedence rule for same-market formula-set conflicts, and a defined `formula_source_conflict` trigger (new §2.4.2; §14; §1.1; T18).** §2.4 required source and formula conflicts to be "preserved rather than resolved by preference" and offered `verified_with_minor_source_difference` and `provisional_formula_conflict` as identity states, but through T17 it never said **which of several disagreeing captures of the same German-market unit is the formula of record.** Nick ruled a two-tier precedence rule:

**(a) Tier 1 — convergence resolution, the primary route.** Where **≥3 independent sources — at least one of them a GTIN-anchored German retailer capture — return the identical ingredient list in identical order**, and the manufacturer's own printed formula markers match across them where visible (F.I.L. fragrance codes / formula prefixes; **a trailing revision-digit difference does not break the match**, a different code family does), **that convergent list is the formula of record.** Consequences, all of them: the packet's primary is **(re-)anchored to it via a dated amendment-log entry**; outlier captures are **demoted to additional captures with notes, never deleted**; `identity_status` becomes **`verified_with_minor_source_difference`**; **confidence on every affected dimension drops one step** below what a single-source clean capture would earn; the record carries the standing **`formula_or_identity_conflict`** review trigger so a human still sees the residual; and **classification then proceeds from the convergent list normally — no blanket `unknown`s.** Resolving the conflict is the point; a resolved conflict is not re-punished by withholding the values it decided.

**(b) Tier 2 — conservative fallback.** Any set conflict that cannot reach that bar: affected dimensions take **`unknown`**, the record carries the named **`formula_source_conflict`** trigger (§14), routes to review, and under adapter decision **AD-2** (`plans/leave-in-inci/adapter-decisions.md`) **cannot commit to the catalog** until resolved — by a fresh C1 pack capture, or by later reaching tier 1's bar.

**(c) The rejected reading, named.** Classifying from whichever capture the packet froze as primary — the "G5 smallest-scope" reading lane B applied in the unseen test — is **not** an admissible resolution: it bakes an arbitrary source preference into a product's values while presenting itself as procedural restraint. **u1 is the standing proof that the frozen primary can itself be the outlier.**

**(d) Scope boundary — T18 is same-market only.** A **cross-market** formula difference (a US formula genuinely differing from the German one) is **not** a T18 conflict; it is an identity question under **§2.4.1 rule 7 (T14)**, whose named counter-example is Cantu's DE/US split. Convergence counting is invalid across markets: three non-German sources agreeing say nothing about the German-sold unit. ### `balanced` gains a second, co-equal film-led reading; `unknown` is reserved for evidence failure (T19, 2026-09-12)

**19. §9's silicone-led `unknown` rule is replaced by a checkable film-led rule resolving to `balanced`; open gap §17.22 closes (§9, §17.22, §19.2.1; T19).** Since v0.2 (change 34, DL C9, BR §2.15) a **film-led** architecture returned **`unknown`**. The reasoning was sound as far as it went: `protein` is defined over L6/R2, `moisture` over L1/L3/L4, **L2 appears in neither**, and a film route may not be interpolated into either. **That premise is kept.** What T19 replaces is the conclusion drawn from it. `unknown` is an abstention — it says the evidence did not settle the question — and on these records the evidence settles it completely: the architecture is read, the legs are read, and what the reading returns is that the product is **neither moisture-directed nor protein-directed**. That is a value, not a gap, and the field's own title already carries its name. **Nick ruled that a film-led architecture resolves to `balanced`**, which gains a **second, explicitly co-equal reading — directional neutrality — beside its existing substantive-mixed reading.** Neither reading is the other's fallback.

**No enum changes anywhere, on either side.** `balanced` is already the middle member of the production check constraint `product_leave_in_specs_care_direction_check` (`moisture | balanced | protein`, root-repo migration `20260811211000_personal_plan_mask_leave_in_authority_v3.sql`) and already the middle member of §9's own value set. Adapter decision **AD-1**'s single-enum principle is satisfied without adding a member to either side, which is why this ruling is a rule change and not an integration change.

**The rule, as four rank clauses (§9).** Reading (b) fires when **all four** hold: **(1)** no R2 route present as architecture (`repair_surface_film` ∉ {`candidate`, `tested`} above the marker); **(2)** a substantive film — an L2 silicone system, a silicone quat or a §5 equivalent, read through §3.1.2/§5 — with at least one film species **above** the tail marker; **(3)** **no** L1, L3 or L4 species above the marker, a leg species present only below it being **subordinate** and creating no direction; **(4)** every film species of clause 2 ranks above every candidate leg species of clause 3, so the reading does not rest on the marker's exact rank alone. Output: `balanced`, **E2**, `evidence_scope: formula`, ceiling **`moderate`** (§9's existing ceiling, unmoved), stepped **one down to `low` and routed to review** where the record's own `tail_marker` is `plausible: false` (T16), `vacuous` (T17/H1) or `none_visible`, or where clause 4's ordering fails. The row stamps itself `row: film_led_neutral` — the same self-identification the humectant-led minimum row already carries — and §4's marker counter-signal is **mandatory**, naming the marker, the film species and every subordinate leg with its rank, so the value reads as a positive neutrality finding with its evidence attached rather than as a default.

**The gate is made checkable, not lowered — and this is the half of §17.22 that could have been over-read.** §17.22 had two halves: the vocabulary has no value for a film-led architecture, and the `unknown` rule is "gated too narrowly" because a silicone-led product with *any* emollient or humectant leg returns `moisture`. The first half is closed by the new value. The second is closed by making the gate deterministic — clause 3's present-as-architecture bar plus clause 4's ordering — **not** by admitting products with a real leg. A film-led product whose humectant or emollient leg sits above its own marker is moisture-directed on its architecture and **stays `moisture`**, including the blow-dry primer §17.22 names by its claim: gold-set slot 13 (Neqi), whose glycol humectant leg sits at r2/r6 above its r9 marker. Its C2 „Feuchtigkeits*schutz*" positioning does not enter the reading in either direction (§9 constraint 3 — marketing never sets the value, and that cuts both ways). That boundary is now a **ruled product call**, not a residual defect.

**`unknown` is reserved for genuine evidence failure, and this is stated in the anchor table itself.** It survives only where the formula or the identity cannot be read — an unresolved or partial capture, an identity conflict, a **§2.4.2 tier-2** formula-set conflict (T18, which is the shape that legitimately produces it). It is **never** the film-led answer. This is the operative half of the ruling for shipping: under **AD-2** (`plans/leave-in-inci/adapter-decisions.md`) no `unknown` reaches the catalog, so the adapter refuses projection and the product sits in the research queue as `needs_research`. An abstention taken on a *complete* formula therefore costs the catalog a record for no evidentiary reason — which is exactly how this ruling surfaced (see *Motivated by*).

**Conformance with §1.1.** The film-led row is a positive architecture read, not a failure mode. It cannot fail toward a recommendation: `care_direction` may not drive weight, persistence, hold or heat matching (§9 constraint 1), and `balanced` under reading (b) asserts **neither** emphasis — it is the conservative statement about a product that makes no directional claim on its own formula. **Why this version exists.** Calibration round 2 re-ran both lanes on the same 13-product gold set under v0.2, with round-1 outputs sealed off and the packet extended with verified directions (R11): **398 fields compared, 93.0 % exact agreement, 28 substantive disagreements** — up from 86.2 % and down from 50. The residual disagreement mass again sat on **standard defects** — six clusters plus an overshoot list — rather than on erratic judgment. v0.3 repairs exactly those. It introduces **no science absent from the leave-on science review**, adds **no product-specific rule**, and adopts no change chosen to make two lanes agree about a particular product.

**No new Nick ruling was issued for v0.3.** R11–R14 remain the standing adjudications. Entry 8 below extends R12's scope (claims join directions in the formula freeze) rather than creating a new ruling, and entry 9 reverses a decision v0.2 made on its own reasoning, not a Nick ruling.

### Claim authority and the formula freeze

**1. Claim capture added to G1 (§2.4, gates G1/G13).** The formula freeze now carries `claims[]` — for every product, each claim that touches a claim-keyed field, with `claim_text` (verbatim, German where the source is German), `source_tier` (C1–C5), `source_url` and `retrieval_date`. Where no C1/C2 source is found, that fact is recorded explicitly with the sources searched, §2.4.1 rule 1 applies, and the record routes to review as `claim_authority_gap`: "not captured" and "does not exist" are different states. **Classification lanes consume frozen claims and never re-research them** — a claim found mid-classification reopens the freeze and is re-frozen before the record completes. A claims change reopens HEAT, HUM, the `heat_styling` focus, the §10.1.2 conflict tag and every „… ist ausgelobt" string.
*Motivated by:* R11 made *directions* part of the freeze and R12 then made *claims* decisive for `provides_heat_protection`, HUM, the `heat_styling` focus and the §18 strings — but the G1 checklist was never extended. Round 2's reference lane had to research five claims mid-classification; without them every heat binary would have been `false` and the round-2 HEAT comparison vacuous. The single most consequential gap the round found. *Traces to:* Cluster 5, ref A19; extends R12.

**2. House-brand clause (§2.4.1 rule 6).** Where the retailer and the brand owner are one legal entity, the retailer's page for its **own** private-label product is that product's manufacturer page and is **C2**, not C3. Named German-market cases: dm ↔ alverde and Balea, Rossmann ↔ Isana. Narrow by construction — own brand only, that retailer's page only — recorded as `claim_tier_basis: house_brand` with the ownership relation named, and routed to review. The same retailer's page for a third-party brand stays C3.
*Motivated by:* read literally, the "retailer copy never creates a claim" rule stripped four German mass-market products of `usage_role` and their claims, although those pages are the brand owner's own product data. The rule exists to stop retailer-*invented* copy about a third party's product. *Traces to:* Overshoot, ref A1, blind A1.

**3. C2-row / rule-3 contradiction resolved in rule 3's favour (§2.4.1).** The C2 row read "manufacturer's German/**EU** product page" while operating rule 3 said non-German EU pages are C5 — directly contradictory for an EU-but-not-German manufacturer page. **Rule 3 governs** (the conservative reading) and the C2 row is reworded to "**German-market** manufacturer page". A Swedish, Spanish or US brand page is C5 whether or not it is in the EU; a German-language German-market page of a foreign brand is C2.
*Motivated by:* four round-2 products sat on the contradiction; the two readings moved `usage_role` on three of them and one HUM claim. *Traces to:* Overshoot, ref A2, blind A2.

**4. Packet-side fix instructions recorded at G1 (§2.4).** Packets must stamp every source with the standard's own **C1–C5** tier rather than a private tier scheme, and INCI normalization must **not split inside an ingredient name** — round 2's normalizer split „1,2-Hexanediol" into `1` and `2-HEXANEDIOL`. Both are packet-builder fixes, recorded in the standard because the packet is the artifact G1 freezes.
*Motivated by:* the packet's own `T1/T2/T3` tiers do not map onto C1–C5, so reconstructing the tier was a reviewer inference on every record — and the tier decides claim-keyed fields. The comma split was harmless where it occurred but would corrupt every rank count on a product with a comma-bearing INCI name higher in the list, and the §3.1.1 tail marker is a rank observation. *Traces to:* Overshoot, blind A27, ref A19.

### Boundary

**5. G0 boundary precedence stated (§2.3.2, new; §2.3 trap 3; G13).** The styling-first test has two halves — the HOLD architecture read, and directions/positioning leading on durable hold or texture — and **G13 binds the positioning half at C1/C2**, closing v0.2's omission of G0 from the "fields governed" list. Ordered rule: both halves at C1/C2 with nothing pointing the other way ⇒ `excluded_styling_first`; C1/C2 evidence materially contradicting the exclusion ⇒ `provisional_boundary`; a positioning half resting only on C3–C5 with no contradicting C1/C2 evidence ⇒ `excluded_styling_first`, with the weak tier recorded. **`provisional_boundary` is reserved for genuine evidence conflicts**, and **§2.3.2 governs over the trap-3 sentence**.
*Motivated by:* two of thirteen round-2 records landed on `provisional_boundary` for the same rule conflict, in opposite directions, and neither is a product a human would find genuinely ambiguous. *Traces to:* Cluster 3, ref A14, blind A15.

### Reading conventions

**6. Rank is the single deterministic prong of "present as architecture" (§3.1.1).** v0.2 defined the phrase with two prongs — rank and coherence — and never said which governs. For every §7 anchor decision, **rank alone decides**. The coherence read survives as a recorded counter-signal that lowers confidence one step and may neither raise a value rank does not support nor lower one it does. A qualifying route below the marker takes its rank-supported value **and** carries the below-marker note its dimension defines **and** routes to review. The resolution is labelled a determinism decision, not an accuracy claim, and its error direction — under-stating persistence, hence under-warning on buildup (FS-20) — is stated.
*Motivated by:* four polymeric or silicone-functional quats sat just below their markers; the two lanes adopted opposite conventions and up to six projected fields moved on one product. *Traces to:* Cluster 4, ref A3, blind A3/A8.

**7. Confidence vocabulary defined (§4).** Exactly four values, matching the conditioner engine: `low` / `moderate` / `moderately_high` / `high`. The §7 ceiling language is mapped onto them, with **"low–moderate" ⇒ a ceiling of `moderate`**. A ceiling caps a value and never sets it; confidence stays in the trace (G14).
*Motivated by:* v0.2 required a `confidence` value on every property-evidence object and never defined the permitted set. The two lanes adopted different scales, so confidence could not be diffed at all. *Traces to:* Overshoot, blind A25.

### FORM, WT and DOSE

**8. Solution row reworded to exclude the LGN pair, not the fatty alcohol (§7.1).** The `aqueous_or_hydroalcoholic_solution` anchor excluded "a long-chain fatty alcohol" while §7.1's own note told the reader to ignore exactly that observation and classify on the rest of the architecture. The row now excludes the **LGN pair** (a long-chain fatty alcohol *paired with a cationic surfactant*), and the note governs.
*Motivated by:* a cream with a lone Cetyl Alcohol at rank 5 and no cationic partner matched no row at all, so the decision order returned `unknown` for no decision-relevant reason. *Traces to:* Overshoot, ref A13.

**9. Microemulsion threshold tightened (§7.1).** Test (i), the solubiliser package, carries **no** position requirement; test (ii), the oil/silicone load, **does**. The asymmetry is stated, which makes the example set exhaustive: the only ambiguous middle is a **single** solubiliser plus a **single** oil or silicone species at or below the marker.
*Motivated by:* a product whose two solubilisers sat below the marker while its silicone sat above it; the two readings changed `product_form`. *Traces to:* Cluster 6, blind A16.

**10. §7.5's G9-resolution clause 1 corrected — the `high` anchor row governs (§7.5).** A two-phase or microemulsion product reaches WT `high` **only by satisfying the `high` anchor on its own terms**. A bulk oil above the tail in an unemulsified system is recorded as a strong supporting observation; it is not a second, easier route to `high`.
*Motivated by:* the clause said `high` and the anchor said `moderate` for a single-family unemulsified bulk oil with no enumerated rich-band member — a shape round 2 met directly. G14 requires a projected value to be exactly what its anchor says. *Traces to:* Overshoot, ref A12.

*Motivated by:* the `high` row fired on `two_phase` regardless of WT while the same section's "DOSE follows WT" paragraph called any disagreement with the WT input a G3 violation. ### SFR, HUM, R2 and HOLD

A Dimethicone/Dimethiconol pair, or an amino silicone plus its own carrier, is one observation and supports `moderate`.
*Motivated by:* six of thirteen round-2 products were affected, and the disagreement propagated straight into `focus.primary` through the §10.2 `smoothing` row. *Traces to:* Cluster 2, ref A4.

*Motivated by:* §7.10's `none_visible` row enumerated only non-qualifying materials plus a plain hydrolysate at any position, so a qualifying silane below the marker fell into a hole — on the set's designated surface-repair product, whose highly-damaged tier and `repair` focus turn on it. ### Projection, focus and fit

**17. The weight conflict tag closed (§10.1.2, new).** The `high` → `moderate` downgrade fires on four conditions and nothing else: WT `high` **from formula alone**; a **C1/C2** intended-finish or positioning statement about the finish this product leaves that materially contradicts it (C3–C5 never triggers it); no finished-product evidence resolving the conflict; and a material rather than decorative contradiction. Effect is fixed — project `moderate`, mark the field uncertain, preserve the `high` trace result and the triggering statement — and **a tag applied without a recorded C1/C2 statement is invalid**. DOSE continues to derive from the WT dimension value, not from the projection.
*Motivated by:* v0.2 named no evidence tier for "intended finish", so one lane applied the tag on a C2 statement and withheld it on a C4/C5 one, by its own admission a lane choice rather than a rule. Six fields moved on a single slot. *Traces to:* Cluster 1, blind A20.

**18. *Motivated by:* the anchor was satisfied by any above-tail fixative polymer with compatible COND/WT and required no curl evidence at all, while the rank order places `curl_definition` above `heat_styling` — so a blow-dry straightening spray formally qualified as a curl product and outranked its own verified heat route. *Motivated by:* three of the four round-2 products carrying a C1/C2 heat claim and projecting `provides_heat_protection: true` could not reach the `heat_styling` role or focus at all. ## v0.2 — round-1 defect repair — 2026-09-04

Standard `leave-in-inci-v0.2` (`leave-in-classification-standard.v0.2.md`). v0.1 is retained unchanged as the frozen round-1 rule set.

**Why this version exists.** Calibration round 1 ran a reference-key lane against a sealed blind lane on a 13-product gold set: 363 fields compared, 86.2 % exact agreement, 50 substantive disagreements, and identical G0 exclusions on both designed boundary cases. The disagreement mass sat on **standard defects** — anchors with no matching row, rules contradicting their own anchors, and decisions the standard never made — rather than on erratic judgment. v0.2 repairs those defects. It introduces **no science absent from the leave-on science review**, and it makes no product-specific adjustment: no rule below was chosen to make two lanes agree about a particular product.

**Nick's adjudication rulings, applied.**

- **R11** — formula freeze / G1 must capture *and verify* directions-of-use, not just INCI.
- **R12** — a marketing claim exists only if the manufacturer's German/EU page or the current German pack states it; retailer copy never creates a claim, only corroborates. Applied as the general claim-authority rule.
- **R13** — the "≥2 persistent non-volatile families, none rich-band" WT anchor is `moderate` with a mandatory counter-signal; counter-signals and confidence stay research-trace-only and never project into user-facing fields.
- **R14** — a genuine repair-film route (R2 `candidate` or better) qualifies a product for the highly-damaged tier alongside the high-conditioning path; an R3 bond flag alone never does.

### Identity, directions and claims

**1. G1 now requires a verified directions capture (§2.4, gate G1).** Directions must be captured verbatim with source tier and date, and verified against the exclusion test before any formula analysis. An explicit rinse instruction sets `excluded_other_form` at G0 regardless of form, architecture or name. Absent directions are a documented gap that forces `usage_role` and `scalp_application_fit` to `unknown` and routes the record to review.
*Motivated by:* a gold-set product frozen and classified as a leave-in whose authoritative German directions read „Nach 9 Sekunden gründlich ausspülen" — a rinse-out. Both the blind lane and the original identity lane missed it because only the INCI was captured; the slot was lost. *Traces to:* DL C1, R11.

**2. Claim authority defined (§2.4.1, new gate G13).** A five-tier claim hierarchy (C1 pack, C2 manufacturer German/EU page, C3 exact-GTIN retailer, C4 other retailer, C5 secondary). Only C1/C2 can *create* a claim; C3–C5 corroborate only. No C1/C2 source ⇒ the claim does not exist, the field takes its no-claim value, and the record routes to review as `claim_authority_gap`. Non-German-market pages are C5, not C2. Claim authority decides only *whether* a claim exists, never its strength — a C1/C2 claim is still E0.
*Motivated by:* v0.1 defined a source hierarchy for directions and none at all for claims, while making the production field `provides_heat_protection` claim-led. Round 1 produced two different production binaries from the same product, and separately saw a retailer invent "the heat protecting molecule" for a plain PVP the manufacturer makes no heat claim about. *Traces to:* DL C5, BR §2.9, R12.

**3. System-level claims handled but not resolved (§2.4.1 rule 4, §13.2).** A claim conditioned on system use is recorded verbatim, tagged `claim_scope: system_level`, keeps §13.3's claim-led binary unchanged, and routes to review. Whether such a claim *should* set a product-level production binary is recorded as an open adjudication item (§17.13) rather than decided.
*Motivated by:* a gold-set product whose heat claim is explicitly "when used as a system of shampoo, conditioner and treatment"; §13.3 assumed a product-level claim and gave no rule. *Traces to:* BR §2.13, and the claim-authority root cause in DL C5. Scope deliberately left open — it is a product-policy question, not a claim-authority question, and no ruling covers it.

**4. ROLE sourcing bound to claim authority (§7.13).** Role values may be read only from C1/C2 directions; retailer application copy corroborates but never creates a role. Each emitted role value carries the verbatim direction sentence that establishes it, and a per-role table states what each sentence must show. A shake instruction establishes no role. Silence is `unknown`.
*Motivated by:* three products where `refresh`, `ends_only` and `heat_styling` were assigned from differently-sourced directions across the two lanes — a sourcing defect, not a judgment difference. *Traces to:* DL C10, R12.

### Reading conventions

**5. "Present as architecture" / "above the tail" operationalised (§3.1.1).** The rank of the first concentration-capped preservative (or preservative-adjacent material) in the list is the tail marker; ingredients above it read as above the tail. Explicitly a **bounded rank observation**, never a percentage inference (G4). Five mandatory limits: the marker and its rank must be recorded in `threshold_reasoning[]` with a `limitations[]` entry; no marker ⇒ no tail boundary and one confidence step down; a very early marker is a strong counter-signal and never a mechanical collapse to `low`; the marker never creates absence; never compare marker positions across products.
*Motivated by:* almost every §7 anchor turns on this phrase and v0.1 never defined it. Both lanes had to invent a method, and in one product a preservative at rank 3 would, read strictly, have put an entire conditioning architecture in the unordered sub-1 % tail. *Traces to:* DL register, BR §2.2.

**6. Mandatory counter-signal register added to the property-evidence object (§4).** Four anchors are now reachable only with a recorded counter-signal, and a record lacking it is invalid.
*Motivated by:* R13's requirement that the new WT row carry a mandatory counter-signal; generalised to the other three anchors already relying on unrecorded caveats. *Traces to:* R13.

### FORM

**7. Solution anchor: cationic requirement dropped (§7.1).** `aqueous_or_hydroalcoholic_solution` no longer requires a cationic polymer or short-chain quat.
*Motivated by:* water + oil + alcohol systems and an eleven-ingredient detangling spray that the clause pushed to `unknown` for no decision-relevant reason. *Traces to:* DL C2, BR §2.12.

**8. Non-LGN emulsion sub-type added (§7.1).** The `emulsion` row now covers **any** true O/W emulsifying system: (a) LGN emulsions as before, and (b) nonionic, polymeric or silicone-emulsifier systems with no LGN pair. The sub-type is kept in the trace as `emulsion_subtype` and is not projected.
*Motivated by:* an extremely common leave-in architecture had no row at all; the blind lane had to assign `emulsion` on the FORM *definition* rather than its anchor at two products and returned `unknown` at a third. *Traces to:* DL C2, BR §2.12.

**9. `two_phase` decidable from architecture (§7.1).** An unemulsified water + oil/silicone architecture sets the row; the „vor Gebrauch gut schütteln" direction corroborates and is no longer required.
*Motivated by:* a product with oil at rank 2 and no emulsifier came out `unknown` in the blind lane solely because the shake direction could not be found. *Traces to:* DL C2.

**10. Solution ↔ microemulsion threshold defined (§7.1).** One test: a solubiliser package **plus** a real oil/silicone load above the tail ⇒ `microemulsion`; a solubiliser with no such load ⇒ solution; a single solubiliser plus a single tail-position silicone ⇒ `unknown` and review. Product clarity corroborates and never decides, since it is not an INCI observation.
*Motivated by:* one product read as `aqueous_solution` by one lane and `microemulsion` by the other on the same solubiliser observation. *Traces to:* DL C2, BR §2.12.

**11. FORM decision order stated (§7.1).** Rows are tested anhydrous → two_phase → emulsion → microemulsion → solution → unknown.
*Motivated by:* the added rows overlap at the edges; without an order the same formula can match two rows. *Traces to:* DL C2 (implementation of the same fix).

### WT and DOSE

**12. Multi-family `moderate` row added to WT (§7.5).** Two or more persistent non-volatile families present as architecture, none of them a rich-band member, no LGN pair ⇒ `moderate`, with a **mandatory** counter-signal naming every family and its rank and stating that the absent rich-band member holds the value below `high`. Confidence caps at `moderate`; the FS-13 double-check still runs.
*Motivated by:* both lanes independently flagged the missing row. It was hit three times, and the blind lane resolved two such products in opposite directions on nothing but its own judgment about list depth. *Traces to:* DL C3, BR §2.1, R13.

**13. Rich-band membership fixed as a closed enumeration; unenumerated liquid vegetable oils read medium-band (§7.5).** Sunflower, high-oleic sunflower, soy, argan, apricot kernel, macadamia, safflower and canola do not satisfy the rich-band test but do count as a persistent family for the multi-family row. Recorded as a reading convention and as open gap §17.15; the L3 band table is deliberately **not** extended.
*Motivated by:* the new R13 row turns on "none rich-band", which is undecidable while band membership is open. *Traces to:* BR §2.1. The science review supports the organising variable (spreading), not a per-oil placement — hence a convention, not a science claim.

**14. The two form-derived WT clauses removed; G9 restated as bidirectional (§7.5, gate G9).** v0.1's `high` anchor ended "or a two-phase or microemulsion carrying a substantial oil/silicone phase" and its `unknown` anchor read "Form or non-volatile architecture unresolved". Both read WT off the FORM label. Both are deleted: two-phase and microemulsion products reach `high` only through the observed rank of their oil/silicone load, and `FORM = unknown` never forces `WT = unknown`. `FORM = two_phase` still drives DOSE and the shake caution — a dose-variability statement, not a weight statement.
*Motivated by:* a direct internal contradiction, decisive on at least one product, where applying the clause gave `high` and applying G9 gave `moderate`. *Traces to:* DL register, BR §2.3.

**15. DOSE follows WT; two row corrections (§7.11).** The multi-family WT `moderate` gives DOSE `moderate` and is not re-argued upward on family count. The `low` row now keys on a volatile- **or water**-dominant carrier and drops its FORM condition; the `unknown` row no longer fires on `FORM = unknown`.
*Motivated by:* DL C3 requires DOSE to track the new WT row. The row corrections come from the blind lane's observation that water is not a volatile carrier in the M6 sense, so a purely aqueous low-weight product satisfied no DOSE row at all. *Traces to:* DL C3, BR §2.18.

### PERS

**16. `permanent_cationic` redefined as polymeric or silicone-functional quats, enumerated by INCI name (§7.6).** The "high-charge-density polyquaterniums" gate is deleted as a G4-barred inference and replaced by a name-visible structural test. Charge density survives as prose explanation, never as a threshold.
*Motivated by:* the blind lane reached the top class exactly once in thirteen products, and only on a literal family match, while materials the standard's own FS-20 names as among the most substantive in the category sat outside the enumeration. *Traces to:* DL C4, BR §2.11.

**17. Monomeric long-chain quats given a home, with a required note (§7.6).** Behentrimonium/Cetrimonium/Distearyldimonium and comparable small-molecule quats are permanently charged but surfactant-removable, so as the dominant persistent species they place the record in `neutral_non_volatile` with a mandatory note stating exactly that. A quat whose structure the INCI name does not settle is not promoted, is recorded `quat_structure: unresolved`, and routes to review.
*Motivated by:* two products where none of the four classes described the dominant persistent species; the blind lane had to assign `volatile_or_water_soluble`, under-stating persistence and therefore under-warning on buildup — the direction FS-20 exists to guard. *Traces to:* DL C4, BR §2.11, and the science review's removability ordering.

### HEAT and HUM

**18. L9 tail-member rule (§5 L9, §13.2, §13.3).** Membership of the closed list is necessary, never sufficient: an L9 member at or below the tail marker earns no state upgrade, and an L9 member with no C1/C2 claim earns no upgrade and leaves the binary `false`. An upgrade to `formula_plausible` now needs an L9 member **above the tail** *and* a C1/C2 claim.
*Motivated by:* hydrolyzed wheat protein is both an L9 member and a routine tail ingredient, so read literally v0.1 promoted an unclaiming naturkosmetik spray to `formula_plausible` — and v0.1's trace state and binary could point in opposite directions for the same product. *Traces to:* DL register, BR §2.13.

**19. HEAT ladder and binary bound to claim authority (§7.8, §13.2, §13.3).** "Claims" throughout §13 means a C1/C2 claim. Retailer-only copy sets `false` with the text recorded and the record routed to review under `claim_authority_gap`.
*Motivated by:* the same root cause as entry 2, but recorded separately because it is the one place the defect reaches a **production** field. *Traces to:* DL C5, R12.

**20. HUM `formula_plausible` decided claim-not-required, with constraints (§7.9).** The state stays reachable without a claim, deliberately and for stated reasons — HEAT projects a production binary and needs a claim precondition, HUM projects only its own evidence flag and asserts no benefit. Three constraints make it safe: confidence never exceeds `low`; a claim-free `formula_plausible` emits **no** user-facing string; the state records what it is.
*Motivated by:* v0.1's `not_claimed` required both no claim and no route while `formula_plausible` required only a route, so any silicone-film leave-in with no humidity claim asserted a plausibility the brand never made. Hit three times. *Traces to:* DL register, BR §2.4a.

**21. The humectant rule made self-consistent (§7.9).** Two steps: a *dominant* humectant architecture blocks `formula_plausible` outright; a humectant materially present but not dominant does not block it, but its observation is a mandatory counter-signal and confidence is capped at `low`. Glycols are not automatically humectants — record the reading, and where the formula does not settle it take the humectant (conservative) reading and mark the field uncertain.
*Motivated by:* v0.1's state requirement said "no *dominant* humectant architecture" while its mandatory counter-signal said humectants materially present "lower this state and never raise it"; the two gave different answers for a material-but-not-dominant humectant, and a glycol-as-solvent-vs-humectant reading decided the state for another product. *Traces to:* BR §2.4b, §2.18.

### R2 and repair

**22. R2 `candidate` narrowed to a closed three-route list (§7.10, §5 L6).** Cationised protein, silane derivative, silicone quat. Non-silicone cationic polymers are removed (the charge-density inference G4 bars, same defect as entry 16) and peptides do not qualify on their own. L6 remains the wider mechanism dictionary; an L6 observation that does not clear the R2 list feeds PERS and SFR, and R2 stays `none_visible`.
*Motivated by:* a mid-list plain hydrolysate read as `candidate` by one lane and `none_visible` by the other, and the §10.2 repair-row contradiction where the row's qualifying condition and its own exclusion clause overlapped. *Traces to:* DL singles, DL register, BR §2.5.

**23. `none_visible` covers a plain hydrolysate at any position, with a mandatory trace note (§7.10).** What makes a protein an R2 route is cationisation or silane functionalisation, not rank.
*Motivated by:* v0.1's anchor described "a plain hydrolyzed protein sitting **in the tail**", leaving no rule for a plain hydrolysate at rank 6; the blind lane had to resolve it from the `candidate` list instead and flagged the hole. *Traces to:* DL singles, BR §2.18.

**24. §18 R2 German copy split by route.** „Protein-Film" is no longer emitted for a silicone-quat route; each route has its own string and neither is used for the other.
*Motivated by:* a silicone-quat `candidate` emitted copy describing a protein film that is not in the product. *Traces to:* DL register.

### Focus and fit

**25. Focus selection procedure defined (§10.2).** Collect qualifying routes (distinct from baseline conditioning) → `primary` is the strongest → a fixed rank order breaks strength ties → positioning breaks only a remaining tie → `general` when nothing qualifies, `primary` marked uncertain when two routes stay unresolved.
*Motivated by:* v0.1's rules contradicted its anchors and nothing ranked two qualifying special-purpose routes, so the same content produced a mirror-flipped primary/secondary pair across the lanes. *Traces to:* DL C6, BR §2.6.

**26. `detangling` anchor rewritten (§10.2).** Reachable via detangling-led C1/C2 positioning with SLIP ≥ `moderate`, or via a slip-dominant light architecture (SLIP ≥ `moderate`, COND ≤ `moderate`, WT `low`). SLIP `high` alone no longer sets it.
*Motivated by:* the v0.1 anchor made `detangling` reachable by every capable emulsion while rule 1 said baseline conditioning must not make it distinctive — and simultaneously made a dedicated light detangling spray come out as `volume_lightness`, inverting the product's actual purpose. The new anchor makes detangling reachable without making it universal. *Traces to:* DL C6, BR §2.6 and the slot-11 finding.

**27. `repair` outranks `smoothing` only with a dedicated repair route; the repair-row contradiction removed at source (§10.2).** Dedicated means R2 `candidate`+ or protein/silane actives in the C1/C2 marketing position. The exclusion clause and the qualifying condition are now disjoint because §7.10 no longer admits cationic polymers.
*Motivated by:* an undefined smoothing-vs-repair order producing a mirror flip, plus the row's internal contradiction. *Traces to:* DL C6, DL register, BR §2.5.

**28. R3 can no longer set `repair` as primary (§10.2).** `chemistry_candidate` may support a *secondary* repair focus only with an open review flag, an independent moderate+ observation, and a review route. R3 keeps its flag; it loses its focus-setting power.
*Motivated by:* allowing it would let a product's primary user-facing purpose be set by a bond claim whose salon evidence G8 bars and whose chemistry independent spectroscopy failed to confirm. *Traces to:* DL C6, and R14's parallel ruling on damage-fit.

**29. Secondary focus capped at two and gated on independent moderate+ support (§10.2).** A route qualifying only through the primary's evidence is the primary described twice (G3).
*Traces to:* DL C6.

**30. `curl_definition` requires its fixative route above the tail (§10.2).**
*Motivated by:* a trace PVP at rank 32, sitting among complexing carriers, made `curl_definition` the primary focus of a conditioning cream — any conditioner with a trace fixative polymer became a curl-definition product. *Traces to:* DL C6 (focus-anchor repair), BR slot-6 finding, operationalised through §3.1.1.

**31. `damage_fit` gains an independent repair-film path to the highly-damaged tier (§10.3).** Row 3b: R2 ∈ {`candidate`, `tested`} at `conditioning_level ≥ moderate`, without needing COND `high`. R3 is explicitly excluded from the table.
*Motivated by:* v0.1 gated row 3 on COND `high`, and COND `high` was itself gated on an LGN pair — so a specialist leave-in for highly damaged hair built on a silicone/silane architecture could not reach the tier written for it. *Traces to:* DL register (fork 4), BR §2.8, R14.

**32. `texture_fit` matrix completed (§10.3).** Rows are weight-led; slip modifies only within the high-weight band; a new row 4 covers high weight with moderate or low slip at uniform `conditional`; a new row 5 is an explicit `unknown` fallback. Row 4 and row 5 records route to review.
*Motivated by:* a clean four-cell disagreement on a product with high weight and moderate slip, for which the table had no row, plus an unstated weight-led reading the blind lane had to invent. *Traces to:* DL C8, BR §2.7.
Consequential completion in the same section: `hair_thickness_fit` and `damage_fit` gain matching `unknown` rows, because entry 37 makes `weight_potential` and `conditioning_level` able to return `unknown` and every weight-/conditioning-led table needs a row for it. This states only that a prior cannot be formed without its input value; it is not a new prior.

**33. `scalp_application_fit` fully specified (§10.3.1).** Default `unknown`; `avoid` for heavy-occlusive/oil-led architecture, EXPO-flagged irritant load, or explicit avoid-the-roots directions; `conditional` for a stated non-scalp placement; `suitable_if_evidenced` only with explicit scalp-directed C1/C2 directions **and** clean EXPO. Ordered test, `avoid` first. Every positive value routes to review; G6 applies throughout; tolerance remains underivable from an INCI list.
*Motivated by:* five products where one lane invented a default and the other varied, and no rule existed for directions stating a placement without mentioning the scalp. *Traces to:* DL C7, BR §2.18.

### care_direction

**34. Silicone-led architectures resolve to `unknown` (§9).** `protein` is defined over L6/R2 and `moisture` over L1/L3/L4; L2 appears in neither, and that is treated as a deliberate abstention rather than a gap to interpolate. The silicone architecture is recorded so the `unknown` reads as an abstention, not a missing analysis.
*Motivated by:* three silicone-led products with no anchor covering them, in a field whose own constraint restricts it to protein-versus-moisture vocabulary. *Traces to:* DL C9, BR §2.15.

**35. Humectant-led minimum added; the "how much moisture leg" line drawn (§9).** A humectant leg above the tail with no R2 route ⇒ `moisture` at `low` confidence, with the thin-architecture counter-signal. Any one of L1/L3/L4 above the tail suffices for `moisture`, with confidence scaling by how many legs are present. Marketing direction never sets the value.
*Motivated by:* v0.1 did not define the minimum moisture threshold and did not say whether `moisture` needs all three legs or any of them; the lanes drew the line differently on two products, and a keratin-positioned product's value had to be argued from architecture against its own marketing. *Traces to:* DL C9, BR §2.15.

### Emission, projection and gates

**36. Excluded-product emission contract defined (§2.3.1).** An excluded record carries identity, G0 state and rationale, `out_of_category: true`, the formula record, optional dimensions marked informational and non-authoritative, and **no lean matching profile at all**. Absence of the profile is the machine-readable exclusion signal. `provisional_boundary` is explicitly not an exclusion.
*Motivated by:* §2.3 said excluded products "do not classify" while §16 said they retain a record, and the two lanes could have emitted anything from nothing to a full profile. *Traces to:* DL register, BR §2.17.

**37. `unknown` added to four lean-profile enums (§10).** `product_form`, `conditioning_level`, `weight_potential`, `persistence`. Emitted only when the §7 dimension resolved to `unknown`, always accompanied by an `uncertain_fields` entry, never meaning out-of-category. **`hold_support` is deliberately excluded**: the blind lane's register listed HOLD, but §7.7 is a coarse three-state presence read with no `unknown` state, so adding one would create a profile state the standard cannot produce. General rule recorded: a profile enum gains `unknown` only where its §7 dimension can actually return it. **The adapter consequence is recorded as an open item (§17.17) and the adapter is not designed here.**
*Motivated by:* four §7 dimensions can produce `unknown` under their own ceilings while their profile enums could not represent it; the blind lane emitted `null` plus an `uncertain_fields` entry four times, an undeclared convention. *Traces to:* DL register, BR §2.16.

**38. Trace containment rule (§10.1.1, new gate G14).** Counter-signals, confidence, evidence levels, threshold reasoning, limitations and reading conventions (`emulsion_subtype`, `tail_marker`, `quat_structure`) never project into a matching or user-facing field and never modify a projected value. Two narrow exceptions: `uncertain_fields[]` carries the fact of uncertainty (a field name only), and §18 strings are emitted by named field values, never by a confidence level.
*Motivated by:* R13's principle, made necessary by R13's own WT row — without it, "moderate + counter-signal" would drift into a fourth value between `moderate` and `high` and the anchor would become un-calibratable. *Traces to:* R13.

**39. Gates G13 (claim authority) and G14 (trace containment) added; G1 and G9 restated (§11).**
*Traces to:* R11, R12, R13. Gates were added rather than leaving the rules as prose so they are enforceable at review, consistent with how every other cross-cutting rule in this standard is expressed.

**40. Human-review triggers extended (§14).** Eight new conditions: `claim_authority_gap`, system-level claim carried into a product field, missing directions capture, unresolved quat structure, no visible tail marker on a marker-dependent record, R3 carried as a secondary repair focus, `texture_fit` row 4 or 5, and every positive `scalp_application_fit`.
*Traces to:* DL C5, C7, C10; BR §2.7, §2.9.

**41. §19 rewritten; §17 gained five open gaps; version stamps bumped.** §19 now records the round-1 result and the round-2 re-run requirement in place of "no calibration has run". New open gaps: system-level claim scope, the unvalidated tail-marker heuristic, spreading-band membership for unenumerated oils, SLIP bias readability, and lean-profile `unknown` vs the production adapter. `model_version` → `leave-in-matching-v0.2`, `category_standard_version` → `leave-in-inci-v0.2`, envelope → `leave-in-research-envelope-v0.2` (promotion to v1.0 at lock unchanged).
*Traces to:* DL verdict; BR §2.2, §2.13, §2.14, §2.16.

### Deliberately not changed in v0.2

Flagged by the blind lane but **outside the round-1 fix list**. Changing them would be an untraced rule change, and each carries a product consequence that belongs in a Nick fork rather than in a repair pass.

- **COND `high` gated on an LGN pair**, capping silicone-led products at `moderate` (BR §2.10). Real ontology question with a downstream effect on `conditioning_level` and `damage_fit`. R14's repair-film path removes its worst consequence: a specialist repair product no longer needs COND `high` to reach the highly-damaged tier.
- **SLIP bias qualifier** unusable for non-film persistent lipid loads and monomeric-quat-only architectures (BR §2.14). The WET+DRY merge is already provisional and its resolution needs the open evidence question in §17.5. Recorded as open gap §17.16.
- **COND `low` vs SLIP `moderate` from a single short-chain-quat observation** (blind lane, slot 11). Not resolvable without deciding whether a monomeric quat is "a route" for COND — the same question C4 answers for PERS but was not asked to answer for COND. Recorded so round 2 can measure whether it still bites.
- **L3 band-table extension** for unenumerated oils (BR §2.1). v0.2 adopts a reading convention instead, because the science review supports the organising variable, not a per-oil placement.
- **L5 rheology-exclusion enumeration** (Polyacrylamide, Polysorbate 20, PQ-16, VP/Methacrylamide/Vinyl Imidazole Copolymer) (BR §2.18). Round 1 resolved these by analogy without producing a disagreement.
- **Transfer-caution threshold** (BR §2.18). §7.5 already states no published instrumental transfer method exists; a threshold would be the unsupported precision the standard forbids.

### Status

**Nothing in v0.2 has been tested for repeatability.** Round 1 measured v0.1. Round 2 must re-run both lanes, under the same seal discipline, on every field the changes above touch, before any v0.2 repeatability claim is made. Round-1 values on changed fields are superseded, not carried forward: a field whose rule changed must be re-derived, never re-labelled.

**Stop condition unchanged.** No catalog value, recommendation, Product Intake rule, Supabase row, user-facing copy or production matcher changes on this document's authority.
