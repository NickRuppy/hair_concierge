# Hair Tools route-led product candidates

## Purpose and approval boundary

This document extends the four-tab `Produklisten` snapshot only where a distinct Personal Plan route lacks a sufficiently specific example. It applies the confirmed product vocabulary and eligibility rules in `product-spec.md`; it is not a general best-products ranking.

The Google Sheet remains unchanged. Every row below is research evidence for review, not an approved catalog product. No product row, image, shopping destination, or Supabase record may be created or published until Nick approves the exact candidate and the later Tool curation path exists. Prices are dated commercial snapshots, not fit criteria, and availability does not determine whether a product can perform a job.

## Shortlist outcome

- Qualify the existing BaByliss Hydro-Fusion D773DE as the first dryer example instead of adding another conventional dryer.
- Add eight exact candidates that fill distinct gaps: a foundational wide-tooth comb, heated rollers, one heatless band, three complementary Night Protection forms, one drying wrap, and one optional scalp-wash aid.
- Exclude two research leads from the active shortlist: these exact flexi rods lack route-critical use-state and diameter evidence, while the scalp-oil applicator attachment does not fit the confirmed product-type vocabulary. Neither product requires a decision now; a better-evidenced heatless volume/set form does remain required for complete neutral volume coverage.
- Do not add more straighteners, curling irons, ordinary dryers, day-use securing products, or similar brushes until the strongest existing Sheet rows have been qualified and deduplicated.

## Candidate fact records

### Existing dryer anchor — BaByliss D773DE

- Identity: `BaByliss Hydro-Fusion Hair Dryer`, manufacturer model `D773DE`.
- Attachments: included `diffuser` and included `concentrator`, each scoped to this exact product.
- Airflow facts: three heat settings, two speed settings, cool-shot control.
- Application protocol: use on towel-dried/damp hair; use the diffuser or concentrator at the manual's lowest heat and speed setting.
- Evidence: [official D773DE instruction manual](https://www.babyliss.de/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-master-fr/default/dw1d9c75a8/product-documents/Produits_2019/D773DE_IB.pdf).
- Commercial snapshot: [Müller exact D773DE product page](https://www.mueller.de/p/babyliss-haartrockner-hydro-fusion-d773de-2704608/), €59.99 on 2026-08-04.
- Migration action: enrich the existing Sheet identity; do not create a second dryer row.

### Foundational comb — Tangle Teezer Wide Tooth Comb

- Identity: `Tangle Teezer Wide Tooth Comb`; exact variant/colour must be fixed during image review.
- Brush/comb facts: `wide_tooth` geometry; dual-sided form.
- Application protocol: verified for wet, damp, or dry hair; can detangle and distribute product; keep away from heat.
- Evidence: [Tangle Teezer official product page](https://tangleteezer.com/products/wide-tooth-comb?variant=52172429525324).
- Commercial snapshot: [Rossmann exact product page](https://www.rossmann.de/de/pflege-und-duft-tangle-teezer-lockenkamm-wide-tooth-comb-for-curly-hair/p/5060630049874), €11.95 on 2026-08-04. Temporary unavailability is recorded but is not a fit signal.
- Route note: the manufacturer's audience language must not become a hard texture gate; the verified geometry, state, and jobs determine eligibility.

### Heated volume/set route — BaByliss RS035E

- Identity: `BaByliss Thermo Ceramic Rollers`, manufacturer model `RS035E`.
- Heated facts: adjustable 90–140 °C, 20 rollers, diameters 19/26/32 mm, 72-minute automatic shutoff.
- Included parts: 20 clips and 20 metal pins in the exact kit.
- Application protocol: verified on damp or dry hair; avoid direct scalp contact and allow the rollers to cool to a comfortable temperature before handling close to skin.
- Evidence: [official RS035E instruction manual](https://www.babyliss.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-ml-babyliss-Library/de_DE/v1745593301525/information-booklets/RS035E_IB.pdf).

- Route note: this fills a heated volume/curl/set form that is materially different from the Sheet's straighteners and single-barrel curlers.

### Heatless band route — Kitsch Charcoal Satin Heatless Curling Set

- Identity: exact Charcoal variant with one satin-covered rod and two satin scrunchies.
- Heatless facts: `band` geometry; manufacturer-stated rod length 34 in/86.36 cm and diameter 1.25 in/31.75 mm; satin contact material; scrunchie fastening.
- Application protocol: manufacturer directions cover damp or dry hair and day or overnight setting; guidance must still require comfortable, non-painful tension.
- Evidence and commercial snapshot: [Kitsch EU exact product page](https://www.mykitsch.com/en-ee/products/satin-heatless-curling-set-charcoal), €21.95 on 2026-08-04.

## Claims deliberately discarded

The research encountered `anti-frizz`, `damage-free`, `prevents breakage`, `repair`, `gentle`, `ionic`, `salon`, `for all hair`, and similar marketing language. None becomes a hard capability, fit gate, or guaranteed outcome. Only the narrower product form, included parts, controls, geometry, materials, use states, and explicit application protocol are retained.

## Do not add yet

- another conventional dryer when the existing D773DE can prove the diffuser and concentrator routes;
- another flat iron, curling iron, or generic multi-styler before exact existing candidates are qualified and deduplicated;
- multiple near-identical brushes, clips, colours, or Kitsch Night Protection variants;
- generic marketplace/no-brand tools without exact identity, evidence, and stable manufacturer-level facts;
- products whose only differentiator is an unsupported marketing outcome.
