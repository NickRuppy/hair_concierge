# Personal Plan launch evidence: Heat and Scalp

This tracked evidence slice contains the approved seven-product Heat cohort from
the `personal-plan-launch-v1` source batch. Five products are available and
recommended; two are unavailable and therefore nonrecommended.

It also contains the separately guarded eight-product Scalp Care cohort. All
eight Scalp products are reviewed and recommended, and their final image bytes
are tracked through exact allowlisted paths and SHA-256 values. The Heat and
Scalp cohorts have independent executors and release gates.

Wella is excluded. This evidence does not authorize catalog writes, uploads,
activation, or any production action.
