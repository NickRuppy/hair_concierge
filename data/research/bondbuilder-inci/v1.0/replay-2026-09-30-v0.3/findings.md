No custom-bottle ratio or effective active dose was guessed.

Both medium findings use the permitted empirical-technology route: relevant acid reinforcement research, compatible targeted source formulas and reasoned use bridges. They do not prove retail effect size, study/retail protocol equality, molecular restoration or that a cheaper product has a lighter effect. Undisclosed concentration alone is not a blocker; a demonstrated incompatible regimen would change the judgment. Kérastase's routine percentages were not attributed to its treatment alone.
