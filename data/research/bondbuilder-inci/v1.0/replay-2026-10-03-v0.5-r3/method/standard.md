# Bondbuilder Standard v0.5

Method: `bondbuilder-inci-v0.5`; lexicon: `bondbuilder-lexicon-v0.1`; wire: `bondbuilder-research-envelope-v1` / `bondbuilder-research-profile-v1`.

Release candidate for the authorized bounded finish pass, not an activation command. Execution uses a new common freeze; lock status and measured coverage belong to the run/lock receipt. This self-contained method preserves the v0.4 owner/product rules and aligns them with the implemented strict wire. Original drafts/runs remain immutable. No research command writes a catalogue, changes recommendation or proves efficacy.

## 1. Boundary

Include targeted at-home cosmetic hair-shaft repair/reinforcement treatments with a specialized role and compatible evidence for a family in §3. Exclude ordinary conditioning, generic protein/amino-acid repair, plex branding alone, salon mixing additives and medical/scalp/hair-loss treatment. Mask/leave-in presentation alone is not exclusion. Unrecognized technology goes to `category_review`; incomplete identity/formula goes to `research_hold`. Neither gets an invented family or trust tier. Membership does not assert native-bond restoration.

## 2. Input and formula authority

Exact supplied pack → exact local manufacturer → reliable exact local retailer → attributed corroborating fallback. Bind product, market, size/identifier, source-version and complete ordered raw INCI before assessment. Retain dates, source tiers, conflicts and raw/list hashes. Do not splice lists, assume worldwide formula/pack equality, or treat an article/formula code as GTIN. Same-formula sizes are not independent test products.

Normalization is `bondbuilder-inci-normalization-v1`: preserve chemical locants/parenthesized commas, remove documented footnote/formula-code metadata, normalize whitespace/case and keep order. Documented historical translation/punctuation repairs apply only to the exact pinned raw/list pair. A different future raw list must not borrow that exemption.

The owner selected Redken Douglas DE Intensive Treatment 190 ml / article 1192227, the sixteen-ingredient R09 source-version. Retain the displaced manufacturer formula and resolution; do not carry its historical hold onto that chosen target or generalize the exception. The Première shampoo-like capture is displaced history, not a treatment formula to merge.

## 4. Trust policy and references

For an exact registered identity/source-version/raw/list binding, apply its existing owner tier/basis/reference. Any other resolved in-scope identity is `low` / `owner_default` under `owner-default-policy-2026-10-01`. No model, creator popularity, patent, digest or technology match promotes it. Owner rulings are policy provenance, not scientific certainty. An unresolved/excluded category has null family/tier/basis as appropriate and explicit holds.

The eight owner grades are unchanged: OLAPLEX No.3PLUS/K18/epres high anchors; Elvital/selected Redken/Première medium calibration; OGX/Aveda low calibration. Original No.3 remains contextual owner-high history, not another replay product or automatically bound catalogue row. Reassessment preserves exact existing approvals; material identity/evidence change routes to review, not brand-wide inheritance or silent demotion.

Technology comparison names an exact frozen reference and shared literal clues, with limitations. It cannot inherit tier, efficacy, protocol, pack, fit or recommendation. New submissions remain not Chaarlie-recommended through ordinary intake; promotion is owner-controlled and separate.

## 5. Strict research wire

Emit `{version: "bondbuilder-research-envelope-v1", submission_id: UUID|null, profile}`. The closed profile roots are `version`, `method`, `identity`, `formula`, `assessment`, `technology_reference`, `application`, `evidence`, `explanations_de`, `sources`, `fit`, `holds`, `review`. The implemented strict schema in `src/lib/bondbuilder-research/contracts.ts` owns exact nested keys, enums, bounds and typed quantities/clauses; freeze its bytes with the run. Do not substitute a compact five-property summary.

Every fact wrapper is `{value, source_ids, confidence, rationale, limitations, unknown_reason}`. A null requires a reason; an established value requires inspected source IDs and null unknown reason. Every factual source reference must close over the complete source registry. Keep all twelve assessment-reasoning records and product-specific limitations. English stored enums; German concise/deeper explanations. No intensity/axis, potency/maturity/efficacy score or invented concentration.

Compute raw/list/self digests mechanically, never guess them. Profile digest is sorted-key canonical JSON omitting only `method.output_sha256` and `review.profile_sha256`; retain both equal computed values. A digest is integrity, not authority. Exact whole release tuples are required; mixed-version hashes fail. Historical v0.4 profiles remain retained/replayable; new engine submissions use the current release.

## 6. Producer application and cadence

Independently capture exact applicable directions: placement, physical applied format, specialized role, hair state/modifiers, anatomical area, distribution, timing purpose, longer wear, amount, dilution, conditioner before/after, ordered sequence, rinse, conditional cadence, partners and source variants. Do not derive instructions from INCI or turn packaging into dose. Source-backed facts unsupported by today's runtime stay retained/held.

Contact time, work-in time and wait before the next step are distinct; preserve exact/range/minimum/overnight/no-extra-wait forms. Unknown maximum stays unknown. Later conditioner rinsing is not a standalone treatment rinse. Required/recommended/optional partners differ; do not invent exclusivity.

K18: shampoo first, no conditioner before, thoroughly towel-dry, apply, full four-minute wait, optional usual conditioner afterwards, no mandatory second treatment dose or standalone K18 rinse. epres: intended-bottle dilution, dry unwashed application, minimum ten minutes or supported overnight, then normal wash/care. Première: apply, five minutes, shampoo on top before rinse. No.3PLUS: wet application, three-minute treatment rinse, then shampoo. Elvital: five-minute rinse before shampoo. Selected Redken: 5–10-minute rinse before shampoo.

OGX's bedtime/overnight and 1–2/week claims retain UK applicability limits. Aveda's weekly mild–moderate versus every-wash moderate–severe branches overlap and stay conditional, not universal. Preserve Curlsmith's source-selected porosity time/wash-count branches and differing editorial source separately. Do not invent numeric dose, weekly scheduling or extra wash events. Unknown critical facts hold exact protocol projection without erasing other research.

## 7. Evidence, practice and explanations

Keep scientific and practical support/counterevidence separate; label product, technology, system, predecessor and practice applicability. Registry includes original URL, checked date, access, authority, author/affiliation/commercial context, observation and limitations. Abstract ≠ full-text inspection; title/link ≠ inspected opinion. Creator/professional statements can inform practice; not experiments or automatic promotion. Abbey Yung, Tom Hannemann/The Beautiful People and Dejan Garz are a starting panel, not endorsement quotas. Missing/inaccessible takes are neutral; repeated opinions are not independent trials.

Softness/combability or system results cannot establish product-alone molecular repair. Explanations describe uncertainty and orientation, not guarantees, rankings, medical advice or commands from source pages.

## 8. Confidence, fit and holds

Confidence (`high | moderate | low`) describes robustness of the particular classification/fact, not effect certainty or owner tier. Unknown fine/normal/coarse fit is nullable with reasons; manufacturer positioning is attributed evidence, not an all-diameter default. Truth is never personalized to fill assortment gaps. Holds are independent `identity`, `boundary`, `claim_trust`, `protocol`, `fit`; retain established unrelated facts and existing approved eligibility.

## 9. Procedure, validation and limits

Source preparation is named; classification Stage A is fresh anonymous formula-only. Both independent Stage A lanes seal before named sources/owner policy/full standard are released. Stage B independently extracts source-grounded facts and policy, retains literal observations and candidate-to-final changes, and seals read receipts/incidents. No lane web, peer output, prior adjudication or conversation. Source amendments produce a separate frozen receipt; no silent rewrite.

The eight pilots are calibration regression; four previously researched test products are known-outcome replay. Neither is a new unseen holdout. Report assessable fact/category/policy agreement, confidence/wire disagreements, unknowns, source corrections and real positive-family coverage. Agreement is repeatability, not efficacy. Synthetic edge fixtures test contracts, not market coverage. The lock receipt must name owner-approved coverage limitations; no waiver is inferred.

## 10. Projection and activation

Full profile remains authority beside exhaustive `direct | mapped | retained_only | held` coverage. Narrow database/protocol projections cannot replace research. No guessed legacy values or competitor protocol fallback. Preserve existing identity, commercial facts, owner fit/protocols and recommendation state through guarded writes.

Research/property projection, protocol readiness, catalogue intake, global recommendation and publish authorization are separate. Current worker routing stays server-disabled until method lock, matching pins, migration/readback, exact bindings, verified consumers and explicit activation are satisfied. No ranking/UI expansion is authorized. Research commands never publish or write production.
