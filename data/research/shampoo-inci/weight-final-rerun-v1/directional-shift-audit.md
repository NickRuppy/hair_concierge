# Directional-shift shared-pattern audit

Status: method-conformance passed; research-only activation gate remains.

The final rerun moves 23 products upward, 2 downward and leaves 25 unchanged. This audit checks whether the upward movement is caused by the rejected automatic-polymer or route-count shortcut. It does not tune the distribution and does not prove consumer-perceived weight.

## Findings

- All 13 `low → moderate` calls contain at least two interacting deposition-relevant families or a repeated light-route architecture, plus explicit deposition, persistence and reset judgments. None is based on a lone polymer or lone refatter.
- `salthouse-anti-fett` is the control case: it remains `low` despite Guar at position 6 because that is the only deposition route and reset capacity is strong. This directly rejects an automatic polymer floor.
- All 9 `moderate → high` calls and the single `low → high` call contain multiple distinct, plausibly persistent systems with explicit reset context. Both blind lanes agree on all ten.
- No upward candidate violates the final method. No upward candidate required adjudication.
- Two upward boundary calls remain explicitly low-confidence: `dejan-garz-shampoo` because Quaternium-80 is unresolved, and `neqi-volume-victory` because the role of Juniperus Virginiana Oil remains unresolved.

## Conclusion

The 23/2 movement is explainable as correction of the earlier simplified baseline, not as evidence of a surviving one-ingredient shortcut. The final method distinguishes a single-polymer low case from formulas with interacting conditioning routes and reserves `high` for converging silicone, lipid, film-forming or polymer systems.

This is a repeatability and method-conformance conclusion only. INCI does not establish exact concentration, finished-product deposition or perceived heaviness, so the report remains a candidate research artifact and the directional-shift gate must stay visible before any future approval or activation.
