### 8. Reformulation / regulatory signal search
No dedicated press coverage of an Elvital Dream Length "neue Formel" relaunch was found. However, a directly relevant regulatory fact was confirmed: **EU Regulation (EU) 2023/1545** expanded mandatory individual fragrance-allergen labelling from ~24 to 82 substances, with a compliance deadline of **31 July 2026** for newly placed-on-market products (existing stock may be sold under the old labelling until 31 July 2028). Today (2026-09-12) is just over 6 weeks past that deadline. Several of Müller's "extra" ingredients not on the other three lists — Pinene, Carvone, Rose Ketones, Tetramethyl Acetyloctahydronaphthalenes — are consistent in kind with substances newly required to be individually declared under the expanded Annex III list, i.e. Müller's *longer* fragrance tail is not obviously "older" on regulatory grounds alone; it could reflect a more recently updated allergen declaration for the *same* perfume. This complicates a naive "longer ingredient list = newer" reading, and is flagged as a genuine limitation of this research (see below) — but it does not explain the Niacinamide/Panthenol presence-or-absence split, which is a functional-ingredient difference unrelated to allergen-labelling granularity.

## Verdict

**The codecheck.info capture (31 ingredients, `additional_inci_captures[0]` in the frozen u1 entry) matches the current German shelf formula.**

Three independently operated, live-fetched sources on 2026-09-12 — Rossmann.de (retailer, GTIN-keyed URL), parfumdreams.de (retailer, GTIN-keyed URL), and codecheck.info (independent aggregator) — return the **same 31-ingredient list, in the same order**, sharing the same `1184630 C` formula prefix and the same `F.I.L. C240136` fragrance-code family (differing only in a trailing revision digit, `/1` vs `/2`, between the two retailers). This list has the plain-DIMETHICONE silicone backbone and includes NIACINAMIDE and PANTHENOL; parfumdreams.de's own independent marketing prose corroborates the Niacinamide/Panthenol content outside the INCI field itself.

Against this 3-source convergence stand two outliers, each alone:
- Müller.de (38 ingredients: no Niacinamide/Panthenol, longer fragrance-allergen tail, no F.I.L. code shown) — re-verified live today and reproduces its frozen capture exactly, so this is a genuinely served, non-stale page, but it is contradicted by three other live sources for the same GTIN.
- loreal-paris.de (35 ingredients: different silicone system — Cyclopentasiloxane/Dimethiconol/PEG-PPG-17/18 Dimethicone — plus a unique protein ingredient and a different fragrance-code family, `C212631` vs `C240136`) — not re-verified live this pass, relying on the frozen 2026-09-12 capture; structurally the most different from every retailer-side source checked.

**Confidence: high** that the 31-ingredient codecheck.info list is what is actually sold under GTIN 3600523587841 today, on the strength of three-source, same-day convergence including an exact ingredient-order and near-identical fragrance-code match between two independent retailers.

**Confidence: medium** on *why* Müller.de and loreal-paris.de diverge — this packet's evidence does not establish whether Müller.de and loreal-paris.de are serving stale/mismatched product-data-management content (most likely explanation, given they are contradicted by three converging live sources) versus genuinely reflecting some other real formula variant (e.g. a different production batch, a market/language-mismatched manufacturer page, or an older declaration not yet refreshed post-relaunch). No direct pack-label photograph was obtained to physically confirm which backbone is printed on units currently on a German shelf — this is the strongest remaining gap.
