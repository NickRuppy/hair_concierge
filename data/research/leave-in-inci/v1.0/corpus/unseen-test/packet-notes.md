## Cross-cutting notes on method

- **"•"-delimited vs comma-delimited sources:** L'Oréal Paris's own pages and retail syndication (Müller.de, loreal-paris.de — slots u1/u2) display INCI lists with a bullet character ("•") as the visible separator rather than a comma. This packet preserves that delimiter verbatim in `raw_inci` (spacing around the bullet harmonized to a single " • " where the source page's own rendering had collapsed inter-token whitespace across a line-wrap — an extraction artifact, not a content change) and documents the resulting tokenization method in the packet's `fingerprint_method` block.
- **Trailing non-ingredient annotations** (fragrance formula codes, asterisk-meaning footnotes, "CLINICAL RESULTS: N/A" boilerplate) were generally excluded from `raw_inci` and quoted here instead, except loreal-paris.de's short trailing fragrance code on slot u1's manufacturer-source capture, which was kept inline in that capture's own `raw_inci` (and excluded only from its `normalized_ingredients` tokenization).
  - u4 (Briogeo/Douglas.de) full trailing annotation, excluded from raw_inci: "* Coconut, vegetable, or plant derived * Dérivé de la noix de coco, de légumes ou de végétaux. CLINICAL RESULTS: N/A"
  - u6 (amika/loveamika.com) full trailing annotation, excluded from both raw_inci captures: "*antistatic agent/agent antistatique"
- **Two products (u1, u2) are the same brand line (L'Oréal Elvital) fetched from the same two retailer/manufacturer pairing (Müller.de + loreal-paris.de)** — both independently produced multi-source INCI conflicts, which may indicate a systematic pattern in how this manufacturer's product data is syndicated across its own site vs. retailer feeds, rather than two isolated incidents. Not investigated further; flagged for lane-level awareness.
- **Sephora.de and Douglas.de both required JavaScript-level extraction** (reading the page's embedded framework data payload directly) to retrieve accordion-panel content (ingredients, composition, usage tips) that does not appear in a plain accessible-text render of the rendered page. This is noted per-source in the packet; it does not affect the verbatim-ness of the text obtained, only the method used to obtain it.
- **No classification, dimension scoring, or category judgment appears anywhere in this document or the companion packet.** Where a determination would require applying the standard's substantive rules (e.g., whether u6's cross-market heat claim is admissible under T14, or how to resolve any of the multi-source INCI conflicts), this document and the packet record the raw evidence and stop.
