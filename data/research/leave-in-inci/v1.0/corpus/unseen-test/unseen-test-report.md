## The one real disagreement — u1 formula-source conflict (all 9 fields)

The packet froze three genuinely conflicting INCI captures of the same nominal SKU (mueller.de 38 / codecheck 31 / loreal-paris.de 35, differing in kind — the C2 capture carries a cationized wheat protein the others lack). The standard contains **two applicable rules with no precedence**:

- **Lane A** applied §2.4-style conflict handling: affected dimensions (WT, PERS, R2) → `unknown`, cascading to unknown fits/care_direction, + review trigger. 7 uncertain fields.
- **Lane B** applied G5 (lower the smallest affected scope): classified from the frozen primary capture, marked `persistence` + `repair_surface_film` uncertain and routed. 2 uncertain fields, real values emitted.

Both are defensible readings; both route to a human; neither moves toward a recommendation (A ⊂ conservative of B). **Proposed rule (needs Nick's ruling, → candidate T18):** when independent captures of the same nominal SKU disagree in formula *set* (not order noise), affected dimensions take `unknown` + a named `formula_source_conflict` trigger (lane A's route) — classifying from one arbitrary source bakes in a source preference the evidence doesn't justify, and under AD-2 the product cannot commit either way until the conflict is resolved (e.g. a fresh C1 pack-shot capture).

One further consequence, recorded not applied: **gold slot 11** becomes vacuous under the widened set, with no value and no confidence step moving.

6. Confidence-band conventions under partial evidence differ (A holds `low` where B holds `moderate` with limitation notes) — same root as (2). — **RESOLVED 2026-09-13.** §4 gains the convention: partial, conflicted or incomplete evidence puts the field **one band below what the anchor would otherwise earn**, and the limitation must name the missing piece. **B's style — holding the anchor band with a limitation note — is the non-canonical reading**, because it makes a partially-evidenced field indistinguishable from a clean one in every machine comparison. Record-schema requirement for v1.0: lane records must emit a `basis`/prose field for `care_direction` (and every other separately-reviewable field) directly, so reasoning is never recovered from a notes file after the fact.
