## Cross-cutting conventions this lane had to adopt (standard is silent or self-conflicting)

These are stated up front because they apply to more than one product, and because a second lane reading the same packet could reasonably land elsewhere on each of them.

**A. Tail-marker vacuity — clause 6's strict enumeration vs its closing test (affects u4, u5).**
§3.1.1 clause 6 defines a vacuous marker two ways in one paragraph. The opening test is a closed enumeration: the tail consists "only of other concentration-capped materials (this section's own list), fragrance/allergen declarations, and colourants." The closing sentence is a substantive test: a marker is *not* covered by the clause when "at least one ingredient below the marker is real architecture (a real conditioning polymer, oil, or comparable species, **not merely a capped material, allergen or colourant**)."
The two disagree whenever a trace functional minor sits in the tail. u4's tail carries Sodium Hydroxide (pH adjuster) and Caprylhydroxamic Acid (a non-enumerated preservative); u5's carries Citric Acid. Under the strict enumeration neither marker is vacuous; under the closing test both are.
**I adopted the substantive (closing-sentence) reading**, for two reasons. (i) It is the clause's own stated purpose — "the tail it defines contains nothing the architecture read would have placed there anyway, so the test separates nothing" — and on both records the test genuinely separates nothing. (ii) §1.1 decides the tie: reading the enumeration strictly means a single trace pH adjuster rescues a marker from vacuity, which lets anchors be *credited* "present as architecture" on rank alone — a failure toward an upgrade, which §1.1 forbids. The substantive reading fails toward the coherence read plus human review, which is the permitted direction.
**Consequence check: on both records no value moved.** Every decisive anchor on u4 (r2–r8, r18) and u5 (r3, r6, r7, r9) is architecture on the ordinal/coherence read alone, so clause 6's "take the coherence read instead" instruction returns the same values. Only confidence discipline and the `tail_marker_vacuous` review route changed. This is the ambiguity most likely to produce a lane disagreement *in trigger lists* rather than in values.

**B. LAYER has no firing rule anywhere in the standard.**
§8.4 defines what LAYER means and §18 gives it a string, but no section states when it fires; §8.6 rule 1 only says it fires "when true". I adopted the narrowest defensible convention: **LAYER fires only when `persistence_removal_class` resolves to `permanent_cationic`** — the most substantive cationic deposit, the case the cationic/anionic complexation mechanism is actually about. A looser convention (any cationic deposit) would have fired LAYER on all four in-category records, which is the §10.3.1 overshoot shape the v0.3 repair pass exists to prevent. **LAYER fires on none of the six records** under this convention.

**C. SHN firing.** §8.1 emits `smoothing_shine_qualifier: present | absent` "as a qualifier on the smoothing route only", and §8.6 fires it at `present`. I read `present` where a **continuous film route** carries the alignment (the M3 optical consequence of the M1/M2 film has something to be a consequence *of*), and `absent` where the alignment route is an emollient package with no film former. Result: `present` on u1 and u6 (`smoothing_route: silicone_film`), `absent` on u4 and u5 (`smoothing_route: emollient`). No independent shine value was emitted anywhere (G3, FS-23), and no positioning („perfektes Glanzfinish" on u5, „Glanz" on u1) was allowed to set it.

**D. §18 has no string for `EXPO = aromatic_or_allergen_exposure`.** The table carries rows for `no_listed_fragrance_signal` and `fragrance_declared` only. I emitted „Enthält deklarierte Duftstoffe." as the nearest applicable string on u1, u5 and u6, since fragrance *is* declared on all three and the register is unchanged. Recorded as a §18 gap.

**E. §18's buildup row is keyed on the pre-T17 emission rule.** The row reads "PERS → `high` + buildup caution", but §8.5 clause 2 (T17) widened emission to the WT limb — `weight_potential: high` with PERS ≥ `neutral_non_volatile` — without updating §18. All four in-category records fire the caution on the **WT limb**, and three of them project `persistence: moderate`, so a literal §18 read would emit nothing. I emitted the existing buildup string in every case (its text is limb-agnostic) and recorded the emission basis in each `hinweise` note as T17 requires. Recorded as a §18 gap.

**F. Transfer has no §18 string.** §7.5 instructs "attach the transfer caution" and §8.6 lists `transfer` as a firing flag, but §18 defines no German string. Transfer is recorded in `hinweise` only and emits nothing user-facing on u1, u4 and u5.

**G. Isopropyl Alcohol (u1 r25, u5 r10).** §7.12's alcohol note keys on `Alcohol Denat.` / `Alcohol`; isopropyl alcohol is outside that enumeration and is named in open gap §17.21 as uncovered. The observation is recorded on both records and **no alcohol note and no §18 alcohol string is emitted**. Consistent with round 2's recorded handling of the same gap.

## u3 — Alcina Föhn Lotion

This is the record where I had to make the hardest call, and where a second lane could most plausibly diverge.

1. **Tail marker `none_visible`** (15 ingredients, no enumerated capped material; Alcohol Denat. r2 plausibly self-preserves; Linalool r11 / Citral r12 are allergens and not marker-eligible after H2). §3.1.1 mandatory limit 2 → fall back to the ordinal read, lower confidence one step, fire the `none_visible` trigger. On a 15-ingredient list the ordinal read is decisive anyway: **VP/VA Copolymer at r3 and Polyquaternium-11 at r4.**
2. **Architecture half of §2.3.2: established.** VP/VA Copolymer is an enumerated L5 fixative-class polymer, in a film-forming context (a hydroalcoholic solution sprayed onto damp hair and blow-dried), with **thin** conditioning behind it: the only conditioning species in the whole formula is one cationic polymer, ranked *below* the fixative. No silicone, no emollient, no lipid, no fatty alcohol, no protein, no humectant beyond Propylene Glycol r8. The rest is two solubilisers, a UV filter, a neutraliser, fragrance and a dye. HOLD = `meaningful_hold_route`.
   T15's adjudicated pair is the calibration: this is the **Maria Nila Curlicue** shape (fixative high, no conditioning architecture behind it), not the **Neqi** shape (a genuine substantive conditioning film — Silicone Quaternium-18 + Polysilicone-29 — with one supporting fixative at r8). T15's dividing line: "a fixative system with thin conditioning behind it is styling; a conditioning film with a supporting fixative is a leave-in."
3. **Positioning half: established at C2.** alcina.com is simultaneously the manufacturer's German-market page and the point of sale, so G13's tier is satisfied (§2.3.2 binds this half to C1/C2). „Die Föhn Lotion für alle klassischen Föhnfrisuren verleiht **Volumen und Elastizität**" plus the headline bullets „Sorgt für mehr **Schwung & Elastizität**… / Für alle **Föhnfrisuren** geeignet" lead on style and texture support; the heat protection is introduced as a secondary benefit („**Gleichzeitig** wird das Haar … geschützt"). The manufacturer's own category path (/haar/haarstyling-finish/fohnprodukte/) corroborates and decided nothing.
4. **The hard question: clause 1 (`excluded_styling_first`) or clause 2 (`provisional_boundary`)?** Clause 2's paradigm is "C1/C2 directions describing an explicitly *included* use — **a blow-dry primer**, a post-wash leave-in — for a product whose architecture reads styling-first", and the C2 directions here are literally „Ins feuchte Haar sprühen. Haare wie gewohnt föhnen." with a C2 heat-protection claim. On its face that is clause 2.
   **I ruled clause 1.** The reason is that §2.2's include row is conditional: "Blow-dry primers and heat-protective leave-ins **when conditioning is meaningful**." That condition fails on this formula — one cationic polymer ranked below the fixative, with no lipid, silicone or fatty-alcohol content of any kind, is not meaningful conditioning. A direction therefore does not establish "an explicitly included use" merely by describing a blow-dry step; it establishes a use that would be included *only if* conditioning were meaningful. Directions that are entirely consistent with a setting lotion do not **materially contradict** a styling-first architecture read, and §2.3.2 clause 4 reserves `provisional_boundary` for genuine evidence conflicts, "never the destination for a decision the reviewer merely found uncomfortable."
   A second check pointed the same way: if clause 2 fired on every blow-dry product with a styling architecture, `excluded_styling_first` would be unreachable for the whole Föhnprodukte shelf, which would make the state and its T15 worked example inoperative.
   **What would flip it:** any C2 statement positioning this as care/leave-in/Pflege/Kur, or a conditioning architecture the formula does not have. If a reviewer reads the blow-dry direction as itself satisfying §2.2's include row, the state becomes `provisional_boundary` — in-category, full record, every affected field uncertain. I have flagged this explicitly in `review_routing`.
5. **Emission contract** as for u2: identity block with `application_stage` (`towel_dry` + `pre_heat` — „Haare wie gewohnt föhnen" is the §7.13 `pre_heat` shape and here even names the tool), the inline architecture read (`aqueous_or_hydroalcoholic_solution` — the §3.1.2 threshold case of a solubiliser package with **no** real oil/silicone load behind it; Juniperus Virginiana Oil r14 is fragrance-tier), **no dimensions, no profile, no cautions**.
6. **Claim authority recorded though it moves nothing.** Both heat claims are C2 → **create**. No binary is emitted (excluded record). Recorded for the reviewer because the formula picture is instructive: **VP/VA Copolymer is an L5 fixative-class member but is NOT on the closed L9 list** (which carries VP/**Acrylates/Lauryl Methacrylate** Copolymer, PQ-55, PVM/MA + PQ-28, PVP/DMAPA Acrylates, Quaternium-70, hydrolyzed wheat protein). Even in-category this record could never have exceeded HEAT `claim_only` (G10) and would have routed under the standing "heat claim with no L9 member" trigger. It is also the only heat claim in the packet with **no** temperature figure attached — the one respect in which it is more modest than the others (FS-14).
7. **Capture gap flagged, not filled.** The packet's source note records that a "Flex/Hold styling-factor rating" was displayed on the page, but no value was frozen into `claims[]`. §7.7 rule 1 (T7) can only record a `manufacturer_hold_level` the frozen claims actually carry, so none is recorded and the T7 trigger does not fire. §2.4 rule 3 forbids this lane from fetching it; a re-freeze would be needed. A manufacturer-published hold number on a product read as `meaningful_hold_route` would be strong corroboration of the exclusion.

## u6 — amika The Shield Anti-Humidity Spray
