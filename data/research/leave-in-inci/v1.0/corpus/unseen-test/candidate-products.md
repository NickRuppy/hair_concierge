## Slot 1 — Long-ingredient-list "Repair" leave-in (stress-test late-preservative over-crediting)

### Candidate 1A (recommended): L'Oréal Elvital Leave-in Dream Length (No Spliss Milk)
- **Brand / format:** L'Oréal Paris Elvital, 200 ml leave-in milk
- **Retailer:** Müller.de — confirmed live on the "L'Oréal Paris Haarpflege" catalog page (47 items), listed as "Elvital Leave-in Dream Length", 4,55 € / 200 ml. https://www.mueller.de/b/loreal-paris/haarpflege/haarpflege/
- **Why it fits:** German-market SKU of L'Oréal's "Dream Lengths" anti-split-ends line, positioned squarely as mass-market repair. Full INCI (31 ingredients, confirmed via CodeCheck) ends in a typical late-list preservative/fragrance-allergen tail (Phenoxyethanol, Benzyl Alcohol, Citronellol, Coumarin, Hexyl Cinnamal, Parfum) after a long humectant/protein/film-former block — a good stress case for late-preservative over-crediting even though it falls short of 40.
- **INCI source (fetched):** https://www.codecheck.info/p/kosmetik/haarpflege/leave-in-pflege/l-oreal-paris-elvital-leave-in-kur-dream-length.html — 31 ingredients: Aqua, Isopropyl Myristate, Dimethicone, Amodimethicone, Triethanolamine, Niacinamide, Ricinus Communis Seed Oil, Hydroxypropyl Guar, Hydroxycitronellal, Hydrolyzed Wheat/Corn/Soy Protein, Phenoxyethanol, Behentrimonium Chloride, Trideceth-6, Polyquaternium-4, Limonene, Benzyl Salicylate, Linalool, Benzyl Alcohol, Panthenol, Isopropyl Alcohol, Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone, Carbomer, Geraniol, Potato Starch Modified, Cetrimonium Chloride, Citronellol, Coumarin, Hexyl Cinnamal, Parfum.
- **Fit rating:** Strong (best available German-market count; note below on the 40+ ceiling)

### Candidate 1B: Garnier Fructis Schadenlöscher Veganes Keratin 10-in-1-Leave-in
- **Brand / format:** Garnier Fructis, 150 ml leave-in spray, vegan "10-in-1" repair positioning
- **Retailer:** Rossmann.de — confirmed live, 5,99 € / 150 ml, "bis zu 230°C Hitzeschutz". https://www.rossmann.de/de/pflege-und-duft-garnier-fructis-schadenloescher-veganes-keratin-10-in-1-leave-in/p/3600542552981
- **Why it fits:** Explicit "10 signs of damage" marketing (maximal ingredient-story positioning), oil/protein/extract-heavy formula ending in a typical dye/fragrance-allergen tail — same over-crediting risk profile as 1A, slightly higher count.
- **INCI source:** CodeCheck listing for the equivalent EN-labelled "Goodbye Damage 10in1 Leave-In" SKU (fetched) — https://www.codecheck.info/p/kosmetik/haarpflege/leave-in-pflege/garnier-fructis-goodbye-damage-10in1-leave-in.html — 32 ingredients ending Linalool, Amyl Cinnamal, Benzyl Alcohol. (Ingredient order/count should match the German "Schadenlöscher" SKU; not independently re-confirmed under the German name — same brand/line/positioning, high confidence it's the same formula.)
- **Fit rating:** Strong

**Note on the "40+" target:** across every German-market leave-in cream/spray checked (Garnier Fructis, L'Oréal Elvital, Pantene Pro-V, ISANA Plex), counts topped out in the low-to-mid 30s. A 43-ingredient US "Elvive Dream Lengths No Haircut Cream" SKU exists (different regional formulation, cyclopentasiloxane-based) but could not be confirmed as sold in Germany under that formulation, so it's excluded. Recommend treating ~30-32 as the realistic ceiling for this slot unless the test accepts a non-EU import.

## Slot 2 — Dual-use directions ("im Haar lassen oder ausspülen")

### Candidate 2A (recommended): L'Oréal Elvital ÖL Magique Veredelndes Haaröl
- **Brand / format:** L'Oréal Paris Elvital, 100 ml hair oil/serum
- **Retailer:** Müller.de — confirmed live, listed multiple times ("Elvital Öl Magique Jojoba Haaröl", "Elvital Öl Magique Haaröl", "Elvital Öl Magique Midnight Serum") on the L'Oréal Paris Haarpflege catalog. https://www.mueller.de/b/loreal-paris/haarpflege/haarpflege/
- **Why it fits:** Official manufacturer page gives explicit dual directions — used **before** washing (rinsed out) and **after** washing (left in). Exact German wording (fetched from https://www.loreal-paris.de/elvital/oel-magique/veredelndes-haaroel-100-ml):
  - Pre-wash/rinse-out: *"Ein paar Tropfen ÖL Magique Veredelndes Haaröl 10 Minuten vor der Haarwäsche verwenden"*
  - Leave-in: *"ÖL Magique Veredelndes Haaröl in die feuchten Haarlängen (ab dem Ohr) einmassieren – für perfekt gepflegte Haare nach der Haarwäsche"*, plus an explicit leave-in claim: *"Das Elvital ÖL Magique Veredelnde Haaröl lässt sich einfach als Leave-In anwenden und muss nicht ausgespült werden."*
  - Note: this is a hair oil rather than a spray/cream/lotion, but it is explicitly marketed and used as a leave-in serum, so it should fit the task's "serum" category.
- **Fit rating:** Strong

### Candidate 2B (possible, weaker/contradictory — useful precisely because it's ambiguous): Moroccanoil Treatment Original
- **Brand / format:** Moroccanoil, 100 ml hair oil/treatment
- **Retailer:** Douglas.de, flaconi.de, notino.de (all list it; widely stocked)
- **Why it's interesting but weaker:** Third-party German beauty sites (perfecthair.ch, alfastore.de) describe using it either as a leave-in **or** as an overnight wash-out treatment ("über Nacht einwirken lassen und am nächsten Morgen auswaschen"). However, the brand's own official German FAQ (fetched, de.moroccanoil.com) states unambiguously: *"Die Moroccanoil Behandlung ist ein Leave-in-Produkt und kann bei Bedarf bedenkenlos erneut aufgetragen werden."* — no rinse-out option offered by the manufacturer. This is a real-world case of conflicting guidance (retailer/influencer content vs. brand copy) rather than a single product with official dual-use directions — good adversarial material, but not a clean citation of official "either/or" copy.
- **Fit rating:** Possible (flag the source conflict if used)

**Difficulty note:** Slot 2 was the hardest to fill. Extensive searching (30+ queries across German drugstore/retailer/brand sites) found that the overwhelming majority of German-market "leave-in" and "Sprüh-Conditioner" products are marketed as pure leave-in only, with no rinse-out alternative offered in the official directions — dual-use framing mostly appears in third-party advice content ("you could also rinse this out if you wanted"), not manufacturer copy. Pre-wash "Ölkur" / oil-serum products are the one product type where official dual-use directions text reliably exists.

## Slot 6 — Cross-market heat claim (positive T14 test)

**Requirement:** a global brand with no German-language manufacturer website, selling a leave-in product (spray/cream/serum/lotion) whose own manufacturer site makes an explicit heat-protection claim (ideally with a temperature), and which is stocked at a real German retailer — to positively exercise the "cross-market claim verification" rule.

### Candidate 6A (recommended): amika The Shield Anti-Humidity Spray

- **Brand / format:** amika (US, Brooklyn-founded haircare brand), 221 ml / 5.3 oz aerosol leave-in spray (also sold in a 103 ml / 2.5 oz size)
- **No German manufacturer site:** loveamika.com is the brand's only site — confirmed via live fetch: no country/language selector beyond a non-functional "United States" footer indicator, no `/de/` path, no `amika.de`. (amika does operate a separate `loveamika.co.uk` for the UK, but nothing for Germany/the EU.) A separate professional-channel site, loveamikapro.com, is also English-only.
- **Heat-protection claim (verbatim, from the manufacturer's own product page):** *"muggy weather, we're ready for you. this spray shields hair from frizz, humidity + 450° F heat."* — plus a standing product badge on the same page reading **"HEAT PROTECTS TO 450 °F"**. Source: https://loveamika.com/products/the-shield-anti-humidity-spray (live-fetched 2026-09-12).
- **German retailer (live-verified via browser, 2026-09-12):** Sephora.de, listed as "AMIKA — The Shield - Anti-Feuchtigkeits-Spray", 221 ml, 30,45 € (lowest recorded price 22,45 €). https://www.sephora.de/p/the-shield---anti-feuchtigkeits-spray-P1000205473.html — the German retail copy independently repeats the same claim in German: *"Die Anti-Feuchtigkeits-Spray schützt das Haar 24 Stunden lang vor Hitze bis zu 230 °C und Feuchtigkeit"* and lists "Wärmeschutzwirkung bis 230 °C" as a headline attribute — i.e. the German-market retail listing itself carries a translated heat-protection claim even though amika publishes no German-language site of its own. - **Leave-in confirmation:** aerosol spray applied to hair as a finishing/anti-humidity step, not rinsed out; Sephora.de directions and amika's own "how to use" copy both describe spray-and-style application.
- **Manufacturer INCI:** Yes — amika publishes a "full ingredients" accordion directly on the product page (loveamika.com), confirmed present via live fetch. Full list (cross-checked against incidecoder.com's mirror of the same product, https://incidecoder.com/products/amika-the-shield-anti-humidity-spray): Butane, Disiloxane, Water/Eau/Aqua, Propane, Hippophae Rhamnoides (Sea Buckthorn) Fruit/Seed Oil, Cetearyl Alcohol, Hydrolyzed Vegetable Protein, Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein, Behentrimonium Chloride, Cetrimonium Chloride, Cetyl PEG/PPG-10/1 Dimethicone, Phenyl Trimethicone, Cinnamidopropyltrimonium Chloride, Oleth-10, Glycerin, Ethylhexylglycerin, Ceresin, Polyquaternium-11, Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride, Citric Acid, Potassium Sorbate, Sodium Benzoate, Phenoxyethanol, Fragrance/Parfum, Benzyl Benzoate, Hydroxycitronellal, Limonene, Citronellol, Coumarin. A UPC of 840117813281 appears in a third-party retailer URL slug (beautybrands.com) associated with this product, but it was not independently cross-verified against the manufacturer or Sephora.de packaging data — treat as unconfirmed until checked against a barcode database or physical packaging.
- **Fit rating:** Strong — all four requirements independently live-verified (no German manufacturer site, leave-in spray, manufacturer's own explicit temperature-based heat claim, real German retailer with matching translated claim).
