## 6. Decisions the script made that the owner may want to change

1. **PH2-6 redaction applied.** In every S0 promise line, hold / finish / flexibility / duration words and hold numbers are replaced by `[CLAIM]` (rest verbatim, adjacent markers collapsed); the unredacted line and the redacted form (`s0_redacted_text`) are in cohort.json only. 75 S0 packets contain at least one redacted promise line (132 replacements). Residual hold/finish wording after redaction (review list): none. For round-1-style records with no designated promise line, the script derives promise lines from free claim lines and withholds every line with hold/finish/flexibility wording or numbers. Unseen records get C1 promise lines from `pack_text_observed` (header/brand/name echoes, scale notes and free-from words removed); no other new data was added to unseen records.
2. **Tier of the promise** = record `best_tier` of the recapture or F-file (a per-field promise tier was not captured). Hold and finish use their own recorded tiers; flexibility words use the hold tier. Records without a refresh file take the calibration `claim_tier`; 11 records had no stored `claim_tier` and were DERIVED (own-brand rule, manufacturer page, else retailer): `neqi-diamond-glass-ultimate-styling-spray` (C2), `got2b-hitzeschutzspray-schutzengel` (C3), `john-frieda-haarserum-frizz-ease-der-baendiger` (C3), `taft-x-gliss-hitzeschutzspray-lovely-long` (C3), `bali-curls-hydrating-curl-cream` (C3), `balea-haarserum-anti-frizz-glatt-und-glossy` (C2_house_brand), `got2b-trockenshampoo-extra-volumen` (C3), `redken-one-united-all-in-one-multi-benefit-treatment` (C2), `loreal-elvital-haaroel-oel-magique-alle-haartypen` (C3), `evo-head-mistress-cuticle-sealer` (C2), `garnier-fructis-sleek-and-stay-heat-activated-serum` (C3).
3. **Round-1 retailer claims are superseded** where a recapture or F-file exists (kept under `history`); hair-type and free-from claims always come from the round-1 capture at C3 (C2_house_brand for own-brand products).
4. **Unseen and gap-fill claim lines:** a `Pack:` line is C1; other lines are C2_house_brand for own-brand products, C2 when the record is a manufacturer-page capture, else C3. Hold strings that mix pack and retailer text were split by hand in the `HOLD_OVERRIDES` table (got2b Spruehkleber, Taft Forming Paste, Redken Beach Spray).
5. **Name overrides** (current unit on sale): Cantu "Firm Hold Edge Stay Gel", Syoss "Curl Cream Gel", Syoss "Max Hold Wax". Other manufacturer-name variants are kept in `identity.name_variants` only.
6. **Directions status** (coordinator rule: paraphrase is never presented as verbatim). 99 records take verbatim German directions (or `missing`) from `capture/directions-promise-r3.json`; the 8 unseen records and nothing else keep the earlier capture with the heuristic below. Each S0 and F/U packet carries `directions_status` = `verbatim` | `paraphrase` | `missing`. `verbatim` (102) = German capture text that needed no editing; this is a language heuristic, the text was not re-checked against the source page. `paraphrase` (1) = English or mixed-language capture summaries, composites of several sources ("Pack:/Web:") and texts from which capture annotations had to be removed; the packet block is `{directions_verbatim:false, paraphrase_text, tier, ...}`. `missing` (4) = no directions in the packet (`directions: null`): `cantu-shea-butter-extra-hold-edge-stay-gel`, `evo-head-mistress-cuticle-sealer`, `isana-professional-styling-creme-locken-traum`, `balea-styling-finishing-stick` (the Balea Styling Finishing Stick: dm states no directions exist; the capture paraphrase is withheld). Tiers are those of the underlying source. `inci.inci_item_count` in every packet is computed by the script from the split list; capture-file counts are never copied (mismatches with capture counts are flagged in `identity.inci_flags`).
7. **No lexicon ids.** `inci_normalised` carries rank and name as written (mechanically cleaned); lexicon normalisation (`lexicon_id`) belongs to the blind lanes and the lexicon, not to the freeze.
9. **Tier rules and flags (coordinator, 2026-10-12).** americancrew.com/de is the US shop in German: every claim, direction and INCI source on that host is C5 (`american-crew-fiber`, `american-crew-pomade`; both therefore have no C1/C2 promise). English-language pack text on products sold in Germany stays C1 with flag `non_german_wording` (`aussie-leave-in-creme-work-that-curl`, `cantu-lockenspray-comeback-curl-next-day`, `hask-lockencreme-curl-care`, `cantu-shea-butter-extra-hold-edge-stay-gel`, `cantu-lockencreme-kokos`). neqi-hair.com stays C2 with record flag `chf_pricing` (`neqi-diamond-glass-ultimate-styling-spray`). C3/C5 promise lines from directions-promise-r3 are recorded in cohort.json only (e.g. `american-crew-fiber`).
8. **Date of round-1 captures** is not stored in the sample files; round-1 fields carry `date: null`. Calibration-set-r2 was frozen 2026-10-09; F-file retrievals are 2026-10-09; recapture, gap-fill and unseen captures are 2026-10-11.
