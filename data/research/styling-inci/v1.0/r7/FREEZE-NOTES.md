## Scope tests

All **143** r6 cohort records and their packets were inspected; **69** selected. `scope-audit.json` records the inclusion/exclusion and captured directions for every record. Reason classes overlap.

- **Q1-owner:** always include the three explicit owner products. NEQI Ultimate remains FX-1 at r8 in the frozen formula: its owner route supersedes D3/R2-Q4, not its F evidence or ingredient class.
- **Q1-film:** aqueous (water above T_S, not propellant-led) spray/lotion; first non-solvent film former is a heat-activated smoothing coat (SI-1 Polysilicone-29/Silicone Quaternium-18 or comparable, supported by captured smoothing/heat-use evidence); no FX-1…FX-4 fixative; other care ingredients only at/after the first preservative/concentration-capped LI marker, not the perfume cutoff T_S. The leading smoothing film itself is not counted as disqualifying care. No public heat_activated value is assigned by this screen. Color Wow is included despite its C3-only promise: the evidence gate is preserved.
- **Q1-brand-line:** independently include all names containing Frizz Ease, Glow & Shine or Nutricurls, because R6-Q1 bars brand-line words as jobs, even where other product-type words keep the output unchanged.
- **T1/T2-directions:** main required application versus optional/conditional extra application, or required sequential multi-site use. Named Balea Volumenpuder/Redken Root Tease always included. Conservative screening also retains ambiguous repeat/dose cases and optional other-product combinations; these are reach probes, not assertions that a target zone changes. Equipment-only tips, nozzle cleaning, unconnected single-use alternatives and required same-site repeats alone do not qualify.
- **T3-result-phrase:** case-insensitive `definiert` stem in **any** C1/C2 captured claim line, including C2_house_brand and typed finish/flexibility/duration/other lines. A plain result phrase needs explicit curls/waves defining/shaping support; lanes decide this, not the builder. All **17** matches are included, not only alverde Beach Waves.
