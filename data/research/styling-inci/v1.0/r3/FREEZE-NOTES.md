## 1. How the cohort was built

1. The round-2 script `build/freeze-cohort-r2.js` is run unchanged in a sandbox whose writes go to memory (it rebuilds round 1 the same way). Its 420 round-2 outputs were compared with the files under `r2/` on disk: 420 of 420 byte-identical (the build stops on any difference). Its 139 records are the base.
2. The round-2 packet issues P2-1…P2-6, the post-recheck rulings R2-H1…R2-H6 and the re-check capture `capture/recheck-r6.json` are applied (section 3). 81 of the 81 re-check rows were read by a patch.
3. The 4 products of `capture/round3-smoothing-r5.json` are frozen with the round-2 builder (same record schema), role calibration.
4. P2-4 transcription repairs run over every record, then every list is renumbered (section 6).
5. Opaque ids: `sty-` + 10 hex of SHA-256(`styling-inci-v1.0-cohort-3/opaque-id/` + slug). No round-3 id equals a round-1 or round-2 id (checked). The slug ↔ id map (with the round-1 and round-2 ids) lives only in `r3/cohort.json` (`id_map`, `records[].source_slug`, `records[].history`).
