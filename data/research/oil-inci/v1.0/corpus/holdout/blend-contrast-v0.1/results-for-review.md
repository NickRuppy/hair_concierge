## What this supports

- A volatile carrier can coexist with a medium retained conditioning system; assess the complete carrier/film relationship.
- A coherent lighter botanical backbone can remain light with a medium co-carrier.
- Rich whole coconut participating in a blend does not automatically make that blend rich.
- Generic film-forming/conditioning functions are not quantity measurements or automatic weight floors. Fragrance-resin wording alone is not structural-film evidence.

These are applications of existing rules, not new thresholds, ingredients-to-weight shortcuts or rule changes.
