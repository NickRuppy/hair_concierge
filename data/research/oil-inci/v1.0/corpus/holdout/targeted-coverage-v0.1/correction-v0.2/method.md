## Canonical persistence definition
# Shared persistence potential v0.1

Local draft contract, approved architecture on 2026-10-07. Applies to the accompanying six-case pilot; category standards remain their existing authorities until explicitly integrated.

**Question:** how persistent a conditioning coating does the complete formula plausibly leave on hair under its intended application? This is a committed qualitative research judgment. It does not score sensory weight or establish accumulation across repeated applications and washes.

| Value | Working anchor |
| --- | --- |
| `low` | A less persistent coating is the better whole-formula reading: a mainly mobile/dispersible remainder with little affirmative support for a coherent persistent conditioning film. Consider all retained components; generic spreading or a volatile vehicle does not cancel a film. |
| `moderate` | A retained conditioning coating is the better reading, but its architecture favors ordinary fluid/lipid or removable conditioner residue over a particularly persistent cohesive/adherent film. This is a positive middle judgment, never uncertainty encoded as a class. |
| `high` | The whole conditioning architecture favors a more persistent coating through a coherent gum/polymer/adherent or structured conditioning-film route. Challenge whether a polymer is really conditioning architecture, merely rheology, an auxiliary or an insufficient isolated clue. Presence and rank alone do not establish high. |

## One decision, one value

For every complete source-scoped product, choose `low`, `moderate` or `high`. No confidence, unknown, indeterminate, abstention class or confidence-based computational gate. When evidence is close, compare the neighboring readings and make the best judgment; put the tipping assumption in one short research reason. Changing an answer later creates a new policy/decision revision.

Use the complete exact declaration and admitted material functions, including grade/derivative/plant-part distinctions. Rank is order only; no amount, top-N threshold, 1% boundary or point count. Exact constituent priors are clues, not blend answers. No automatic “all oils are low,” “all silicones are high,” weight-to-persistence rule or promotion from an unassessed auxiliary.

Application context is part of the research input: rinse-out, retained damp/dry hair care or shampooed pre-wash. A rinsed product's judgment concerns its likely post-rinse coating; a retained product's judgment concerns its applied conditioning remainder. Context does not provide a measured removal factor or guarantee. Classification should not speculate about individual washing schedules or scalp tolerance.

The common reference describes exact ingredient identity and relevant functions, not universal finished-product values. Category-specific material readings provide evidence; this shared definition owns the output meaning. Prior Conditioner cumulative residue risk and Leave-In mechanism scores remain their distinct source facts. No automatic conversion of indeterminate to moderate, binary elevated to high, or neutral-non-volatile to moderate.

## Meaning and evidence ceiling

Weight is the rough potential for greasy/coated feel and flattening on hair at a comparable modest leave-on dose. Every blend judgment is an **estimate with low confidence**. Ingredient order is a qualitative emphasis clue, never a known proportion, majority, trace cutoff or numerical weighting. Dispensing as a spray, a lightweight claim, penetration or a fine-hair label cannot establish weight. Application mode and thickness fit are separate.

The policy intentionally permits a working class from incomplete formulation information. Unknown is reserved for a decisive unidentified backbone or genuinely unresolved competing architectures; absent percentages or grades alone need not force it. Record the uncertainty and the strongest alternative rather than inventing quantities. Human-adjudicated examples are developmental choices, not accuracy targets or blind worked examples.

Quick reading: identify the base; check what conditioning material can remain on hair; choose the strongest light/medium/rich hypothesis; keep low confidence, an alternative and every deciding gap.

## Ordered interpretation

**B0 — exact identity.** Match only reviewed exact names/aliases. Keep plant parts, derivatives, macerates and grades distinct. A modified castor/coconut ingredient does not inherit the intact oil prior. An unspecified sunflower declaration does not inherit the seed-oil prior. Unknown preparation remains a gap; it blocks a class only if it could plausibly change the chosen backbone interpretation.

**B1 — identify the carrier and retained system.** Annotate all ingredients as principal, secondary, auxiliary or unresolved, with a reason. Identify the early carrier sequence using exact functions and ordered INCI together; do not mechanically make the first two ingredients principal, presume a 1% boundary, count oils, or assume all later ingredients are trace. Consider retained silicones, waxes, long-chain lipids, gums/resins and botanical oils alongside mobile carriers. Role assignments are themselves qualitative estimates where quantities are absent.

**B2 — single carrier or coherent oil base.** A sole admitted fixed oil applies its material prior with the original confidence ceiling. A coherent multi-oil backbone whose principal priors point to the same class supports that class. Auxiliaries do not automatically change it; any credible retained-film counter-signal must still be addressed.

**B3 — competing botanical carriers.** Use the leading identified fixed-oil tendency as the initial hypothesis, then evaluate the remaining principal carriers. A leading light oil plus a medium carrier supports light/low with medium alternative if no affirmative rich or structured-film signal overrides it. A leading medium base with lighter carriers supports medium/low with light alternative. A rich oil participating in an otherwise lighter principal oil base supplies a positive reason to lean medium rather than light; this is a qualitative burden hypothesis, not an average. A rich-leading botanical base supports rich unless a positive mobile-carrier/other architecture argument supports a lighter estimate. These are soft trial tie-breakers, not proof of dominant amount or universal rank rules.

**B4 — mobile ester/alkane base.** A coherent ester/alkane carrier architecture supports light/low when the retained system supplies no stronger structural/conditioning-film argument. Nonvolatile emollients and medium/rich botanicals remain counter-signals even when later. An early medium-weight plant oil counts against a light classification, but its presence or early position alone does not upgrade an otherwise lighter carrier blend to medium or establish a substantial retained contribution. Medium requires an affirmative whole-formula argument that the retained oil/conditioning system outweighs the lighter carrier interpretation; restating the oil's material prior or rank is insufficient. This does not require known percentages, ignore retained botanicals or force light when the competing architectures are balanced: retain the strongest alternative and apply B7 if no argument wins. Mobility/spreading is only a hypothesis about hair load; it does not measure evaporation or the retained fraction.

**B5 — silicone conditioning base.** When conditioning silicones form the principal carrier/film system, medium/low is the working interpretation of retained conditioning film, including mixed mobile hydrocarbon/ester carriers. Light remains an alternative when the mobile portion could produce a smaller residue. If the architecture is affirmatively mobile/volatile with only a secondary conditioning-film route, light may be selected with medium alternative. Silicone presence alone does not invoke this rule. Unspecified dimethicone grade stays a limitation; no grade-specific viscosity or volatility is invented.

**B6 — affirmative rich retained system.** Rich requires a positive whole-formula argument: a rich-leading oil base or a principal structured/persistent lipid, wax, gum or resin system that plausibly outweighs lighter distribution clues. One wax/gum, a rich-source name or several later oils is insufficient. When this argument is stronger than B3–B5's lighter hypothesis, choose rich/low and document why the lighter architecture loses. Otherwise retained materials supply a counter-signal and may support medium; they do not impose a universal rich floor.

**B7 — unresolved backbone.** Use unknown when the first-order carrier identity/function is missing, the declaration is materially incomplete/conflicted, or two incompatible backbone readings remain equally persuasive after the positive rules. Explain the missing deciding observation and the plausible classes. Do not assign medium just because evidence is missing. A known but unquantified later ingredient is not automatically decisive.

## Output and precedence

Apply B0/B1 first. Exact single-carrier B2 keeps its material confidence ceiling. For mixtures, evaluate B3–B6 as competing architecture arguments; B6 overrides a lighter class only with its affirmative whole-system reason. Select the best-supported soft interpretation and cite its rule IDs, positive signals, heavier/lighter counter-signals, alternative and remaining gaps. If none wins, B7 returns unknown. This is an expert judgment policy, not a concentration calculator or a deterministic runtime classifier.

Output values remain `light | medium | rich | unknown`; substantive mixtures have `estimate=true`, `confidence=low`. Unknown has `estimate=false`, `confidence=low`, an explicit gap and plausible alternatives. Two independent lanes test repeatability; neither agreement nor matching a human example raises confidence. No product eligibility, fit or production write follows.
