## Most useful challenge

L’Occitane contains **Hydrogenated Coco-Glycerides**, whose exact-INCI supplier example documents film-forming and viscosity-increasing capability. Both lanes treated this as a real high counter-signal, while choosing moderate from the complete fluid emollient architecture. They did not dismiss it as trace because of position, transfer the supplier grade/melting range, or assume every botanical system must be moderate. Root retained the same interpretation. The specific remaining assumption is whether that modified lipid organizes the finished coating; the ingredient list does not establish this experimentally.

## Independence and coverage limits

- All four are project-new exact product/formula candidates against this local oil corpus: 625 prior JSON files / 87 distinct raw declaration strings checked, no literal matches. Source agents separately checked names/variants. Literal case/whitespace comparison cannot prove chemical equivalence or model-level unfamiliarity. Oil Ultime Rose was already exposed; the Argan product is a distinct formula within that familiar family.
- **PC03 / Dr. Hauschka:** lane A disclosed a tentative unverified product-identity association and stated it was not pursued or used. Lane B reported no recognition. Retain the 4/4 declared-formula agreement, but do not present all four as clean model-unfamiliar blind validation. No sealed outputs were rewritten or rerun to conceal the incident.
- Coverage is **89 ordered ranks / 72 exact function identities**, including explicit unassessed functions and unresolved botanical preparation limits. Unknown function never means negligible contribution. No ingredient percentages, exact supplier grades, measured hours, wash counts or repeated-use build-up were inferred.
- The German Oil Ultime Argan declaration differs from encountered NL/Greek declarations; this trial uses only its matching DE producer/shop identity. Metal Detox uses the independently complete **NL** producer declaration, without German equivalence. Hauschka and L’Occitane have page/size/SKU identity but no verified GTIN in this pass. These are published-source packets, not physical-label batch verification.
- The four candidates still provide **no low example**. The earlier Mist/Vanishing/Two-Way Mirror search was bounded and disclosed, not an exhaustive shelf audit. Zero low is acceptable; it does not prove no low-persistence oil exists or validate that boundary.
