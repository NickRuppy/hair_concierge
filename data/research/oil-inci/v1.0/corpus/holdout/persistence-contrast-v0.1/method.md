# European oil persistence contrast — blind method v0.1

Persistence only. Derived from Oil standard v0.9 and persistence guidance v0.3, with the shared persistence endpoint unchanged. All product identities, marketing, prior product decisions and material-weight/build-up labels are withheld. No other direct property, fit, claim or intake readiness is assessed.

## Allowed inputs and independence

Read only this method, `neutral-packet.json` and `function-dictionary.json` from this trial. No browsing, repository searches, prior results, brand inference, peer reads, or nested reviewers. Root has admitted each complete source-scoped declaration and retained leave-on hair-fibre context. Exact identity and source binding remain outside the blind packet. Every source and ingredient function is evidence with limits, not a finished-product answer. Do not infer amounts, grades, a 1% boundary, a top-N threshold or a majority from order.

## Endpoint and boundaries

Question: how persistent a conditioning coating does the complete formula plausibly leave on hair in the admitted retained application? Commit exactly one scalar `low`, `moderate` or `high`. No confidence, estimate, unknown, indeterminate, abstention or computational alternatives. A reason and tipping assumption belong in the separate research trace. This is not sensory weight, cumulative build-up, wash resistance, duration in hours, repair, penetration or suitability.

- **P0 — exact declaration and functions.** Consider every ordered ingredient using only admitted exact names, derivatives and functions. A missing or unresolved function remains a visible gap, never negligible. The dictionary does not identify exact commercial grades or ingredient quantities. A generic dimethicone declaration can represent different viscosity/volatility ranges; do not invent its actual grade. Modified oils do not inherit whole-oil mechanisms.
- **P1 — complete retained architecture.** Distinguish vehicle/distribution functions, fluid emollient conditioning, affirmative cohesive/adherent/structured conditioning-film routes, rheology/texture functions and auxiliaries. Ingredient order helps interpret the system qualitatively, never as concentration. A mobile/volatile vehicle cannot by itself cancel a functionally meaningful retained coating. Nonvolatility alone is not proof of high persistence.
- **P2 — low.** Select low when a less persistent, mainly mobile/dispersible conditioning remainder is the stronger positive whole-system reading, with little affirmative support for a coherent persistent film. Low permits some residue. Spray format, light/dry/vanishing feel, evaporation of one carrier or penetration alone does not establish this reading.
- **P3 — moderate.** Select moderate when an ordinary retained fluid/lipid conditioning coat is the stronger positive whole-system reading, without a stronger particularly cohesive/adherent/structured-film interpretation. Moderate is not an uncertainty fallback or a default for natural oils.
- **P4 — high.** Select high when the complete conditioning system affirmatively supports a particularly cohesive/adherent/structured film. A gum with complementary conditioning fluids may support this reading if functionally substantive in the whole architecture; ingredient presence/rank alone does not. A polymer may merely structure product texture, assist delivery or supply an isolated clue. Natural systems can qualify through supported exact-material/whole-system mechanisms; absence of silicone/gum does not force moderate.
- **P5 — neighboring readings and commitment.** Challenge the strongest lower and higher interpretations, then choose the best class. Weight, viscosity, stickiness, plant-oil/silicone counts, base, nonvolatility, penetration and unknown grades alone do not decide it. Missing grade/proportion is not a fourth output or a mechanical reason for moderate. State the specific tipping assumption in ordinary prose.
- **P6 — evidence ceiling and coverage.** No desired class distribution or requirement to find low. No scientific validation, wash count, cleansing schedule, removal factor or fit inference. The trial measures independent repeatability only. Source/identity/context admission was performed outside your lane; do not infer a pre-wash or scalp scenario.

Those explanatory fields are research trace, not extra consumer properties. Do not emit weight or other product-property values.
