## Practical diagnosis and recommended next step

The failure is a **method gap for mixtures**, not four bad products or four missing ingredient lists. v0.7 forbids inferred majority, averaging, count votes, automatic rich floors and forced middle classes, but supplies no positive tie-breaker for common mixed-carrier architectures. Exact percentages and silicone grades are rarely disclosed in these inputs. More pure-oil lookup cases will not solve this.

Recommended next: prepare one conservative, explicitly low-confidence approximation policy using carrier architecture and ordered INCI as qualitative clues. It must state its assumptions, retain lighter/heavier alternatives and never report concentrations or measured feel. Keep unknown for unresolved backbone identity or genuinely incompatible interpretations. Reapply that reviewed policy to these **same four development blends**, retaining this round; do not call the rerun a holdout or tune rules solely to manufacture agreement. This is the next behavior choice before declaring the oil weight engine usable on arbitrary products.
