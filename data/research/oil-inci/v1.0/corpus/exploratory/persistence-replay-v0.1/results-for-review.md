## Interpretation

Moderate means ordinary retained coating, not medium weight or uncertainty. Plain coconut, jojoba and marula therefore receive moderate here without changing their different weight estimates. Fluid silicones do not automatically mean high: the Olaplex formula remains moderate, while coherent gum/conditioning-polymer/film-former architectures receive high after whole-system review. Retained oils and mobile emollients can support the middle anchor even when they feel light. No ingredient percentage, wash count, heat/UV efficacy, repair, scalp outcome or fit was inferred.

The harder structure-versus-film cases were reviewed explicitly: Living Proof's gelling copolymers, JVN's grade-dependent jojoba esters, Aveda's seed fat, Moroccanoil's film-former combination, and Color Wow's rheology system. These are current committed judgments, revisable with new evidence; computation does not branch on rationale language.
