# Provisional oil build-up guidance v0.1 — focused round 7

Scope: new formula-only build-up estimates for cosmetic hair/scalp oils and oil-labelled serums, including water-based oil-serums. No weight, penetration, fit, safety, repair, heat/UV or production inference. Outputs describe relative persistent conditioning-film/load potential under comparable small applications; they do not measure accumulation across washes or guarantee cleansing. This trial refines operational consistency, not scientific validation.

B0. Read the complete literal declaration and reviewed exact material functions. Preserve plant part, derivative and grade distinctions; do not chemically repair a spelling. A decisive unresolved identity or grade means unknown. Rank is order, not a percentage, top-N rule or 1% boundary.

B1. Judge the complete residual conditioning architecture. Plant/silicone origin, oil count, material weight, penetration, missing gum and manufacturer claims never decide build-up. An aqueous vehicle is not automatically low; a mobile/volatile carrier does not cancel a substantial retained conditioning film.

B2. Elevated needs affirmative support for a coherent substantial gum/resin/wax/polymer conditioning film. A structured or rich oil coating can contribute only with admitted material-function support, not merely coconut/castor presence or a botanical-only base. Challenge whether the film signal is secondary, whether the grade is unknown, and whether a thickener actually serves aqueous rheology rather than hair conditioning. One late gum is not sufficient by itself. Every elevated result is an estimate with low confidence, not measured accumulation.

B3. Low needs affirmative support for a coherent mobile fluid-emollient system and weak conditioning-film potential, with no substantial rival residual architecture. Examine the entire retained system and derivative/grade uncertainty. Generic emollient function, silicone-free positioning or a familiar light weight alone does not establish low. Low is not zero residue.

B4. Unknown remains valid when low and elevated residual architectures cannot be distinguished, decisive material grades are unestablished, or a plant-only backbone lacks affirmative weak/substantial-film support. State the exact gap and closest supported alternative; do not use medium as a fallback.

B5. Each result includes value (low/elevated/unknown), estimate (true for known new estimates, false for unknown), confidence low, exact ingredient rank/name evidence, counter-signals, nearest alternative, reason and unresolved gaps. Ingredient ledger covers every primary rank exactly once with roles copied from the frozen lexicon; contextual footnotes are retained separately, not converted into independently ranked ingredients. Do not infer parent fragrance percentages from its constituent sublist.

B6. All four neutral records were source-preflighted as in-scope under the unchanged category charter. Water-based oil-labelled serums are expressly included; no source identity or marketing claim is provided for classification. Do not exclude a record because water/glycols lead. Product directions are shown only after both lane outputs are sealed and cannot rewrite the blind result.

Previously accepted product decisions remain separate exact-formula precedents, not ingredient defaults or blind target answers. No automatic coconut/castor/ester/weight-to-build-up mapping is introduced. Any guidance revision after seeing this trial requires a new version/run; this method remains frozen.
