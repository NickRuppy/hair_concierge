## What the test changes

The guide is usable as a narrow provisional decision procedure, but it has not demonstrated a new low classification. Requiring affirmative weak-film support leaves pure jojoba and derivative-rich JVN unresolved. Agreement on unknown is repeatability, not successful classification coverage. Do not promote this to general intake readiness or silently weaken the frozen method.

The next recommendation is to review **pure jojoba first**, then JVN's derivative grades and Color Wow's film contribution, one concrete estimate at a time. For jojoba, low is the candidate both lanes identify, but adopting it would be an explicitly low-confidence practical product/material prior, rather than new measured accumulation evidence. The meaningful choice is whether to accept that narrow approximation; retain the frozen unknown either way. Do not reopen settled weight or add a thickness penalty.

Source-supported uses are preserved separately: Biopark has shampooed hair/scalp treatment plus retained ends; the other three establish retained fibre finishing. Shampooed use affects relevance, not the formula's build-up estimate or a removal guarantee. No repair, fit, weight, flag, heat/UV or production field is classified by this focused round.
