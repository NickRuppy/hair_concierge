## What the trial establishes

Both fresh castor-led systems (Sunny Isle R02 and Aroma-Zone R05) received rich in all three initial judgments. Both new silicone carrier/gum systems (Kérastase R01 and Oil Ultime Rose R08) received medium. The khadi botanical/ester matrix R04 received medium. This demonstrates that the current policy can express the three weight classes rather than assigning light to every oil; it does not validate the classes against hair measurements.

K18 R03 remained light in all three judgments after the exact shorter sunflower entry was admitted. Its accepted OE28 light/low decision is preserved. Wella R07 split medium/medium/light (main/A/B); the working medium retains the already accepted OE32 choice while preserving the light argument. No later botanical is treated as trace because of a fragrance boundary.

Evolve R06 is the sole unresolved new case: A light, B unknown, main medium. The leading ester is a positive light route; the intact botanical system and rich coconut are a medium counterargument. A coconut name or lack of proven evaporation cannot by itself upgrade an ester blend. Conversely a generic liquid ester does not quantify retained hair load. Reconciliation retains unknown under B7 because neither whole-system interpretation wins with these inputs. No class is forced and no policy is tuned.
