Two moderate, three high and one low; no distribution target was supplied.

## What changes relative to native labels

The pilot re-evaluates formulas against the shared endpoint. It does not numerically translate previous values:

- Both conditioners have native cumulative residue risk `indeterminate`; that asks a repeated-cycle question. Their new moderate/high persistence decisions come from their formulas and rinse-out contexts, not from mapping indeterminate into the middle band.
- Balea spray's native Leave-In persistence is moderate because of the mechanism-class projection. This shared complete-formula judgment is low; that is a deliberate distinction between mechanism presence and the complete conditioning system. GLISS remains high for a coherent combined film system.
- Pure jojoba's accepted native binary build-up estimate is low. It is moderate on this new three-band persistence scale because an ordinary retained lipid coating has its own middle band. Verb's native binary elevated estimate and the new high judgment share film evidence, but no universal elevated-to-high conversion is introduced.

The category integration step must replace/map affected fields explicitly and re-evaluate the remaining category records under this definition; it must not create two contradictory active matching inputs or silently reinterpret old artifacts. Weight remains a separate property.

## Checks and applicability

Six complete prior source declarations, 93 primary ingredient positions and 71 exact function identities. Two formula-only lanes, inputs frozen before dispatch and all four output files sealed before parent inspection. Both attest only permitted reads, with no recognized exact identity or access incident. Isolation was contractual. Value agreement is **6/6 repeatability**, not measured accuracy or validation of accumulation across washes.

This is a replay/compatibility pilot on existing captured declarations, not live shelf research or an unseen holdout. Some original source URLs were not preserved; their captured product/directions records remain the provenance. The source packet binds exact retained or rinse-out use; jojoba pre-wash/scalp use is outside this selected scenario. Material grades and deposition are ordinary research assumptions explained in the reasons, not extra output states. Source and all earlier category files are preserved.
