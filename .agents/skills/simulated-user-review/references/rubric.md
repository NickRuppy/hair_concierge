# Review Rubric

Use this rubric to turn observations into consistent judgments.

## Severity Levels

`Critical`
The flow is blocked, badly misleading, or meaningfully damages trust.

`Major`
The user can continue, but the issue is likely to confuse them, weaken recommendation trust, or make the output feel mismatched.

`Minor`
The issue causes friction, awkwardness, or polish problems but does not seriously undermine the journey.

`Note`
Useful observation or improvement opportunity without clear user harm.

## Dimensions

### 1. Language Clarity And Readability

Look for:

- Clear German sentence structure
- Limited jargon, or jargon that is explained
- Scannable phrasing and labels
- Consistent terminology across the flow

Escalate when:

- Text is hard to parse on first read
- The same concept is described with shifting terms
- Important labels or explanations are vague

### 2. Flow Coherence

Look for:

- Each step logically follows from the previous step
- Questions feel connected to the eventual result
- The interface makes it clear what changed and what comes next

Escalate when:

- The product jumps between ideas without enough context
- The result does not feel connected to the collected answers
- Navigation or state changes are confusing

### 3. Explanation Quality

Look for:

- Useful explanation of why a question matters
- Recommendation rationale that references user inputs
- Practical wording instead of filler language

Escalate when:

- The product relies on generic reassurance
- Explanations sound polished but say very little
- Advice is hard to act on

### 4. Recommendation Fit

Look for:

- Recommendations that clearly reflect the persona's answers
- Output that acknowledges the user's concerns and goals
- Internal consistency between profile, concerns, and advice

Escalate when:

- Recommendations seem generic
- Advice conflicts with the stated concerns
- The rationale does not connect back to what the user shared

### 5. Trust, Caveats, And Boundaries

Look for:

- Honest wording about uncertainty
- Clear boundary between cosmetic guidance and medically adjacent claims
- A tone that informs without overpromising

Escalate when:

- The product sounds more certain than the evidence shown
- Caveats are missing where a user would expect them
- Cosmetic guidance slips into diagnostic language

### 6. Usability Friction

Look for:

- Clear buttons, labels, and progression
- Predictable interactions
- Errors or blockers explained in plain language

Escalate when:

- The user is blocked or lost
- Important UI states are easy to miss
- The product requires guesswork to continue

## Evidence Standard

Each finding should point to a specific moment in the journey:

- screen, step, or URL state
- short quoted or paraphrased text
- user-visible impact

Lead with what the user experienced. Root-cause guesses are optional and should be labeled as tentative.
