# Round 1 ingredient supplement

Version `oil-round-1-supplement-0.1.0-draft`, reviewed 2026-10-01. This extends exact generic ingredient coverage only. No cohort capture, product identity or classification records were accessed; no product-label queries were used. Ingredient searches occasionally returned irrelevant product results, which were not used as evidence. Existing seed files were not modified.

## Use and limits

- Merge entries by canonical identity, retaining stronger seed evidence and prior limits. Duplicate canonical names here add explicit submitted aliases; they must not downgrade seed penetration evidence to not_assessed.
- Roles marked not_assessed are real evidence gaps. A glossary establishes nomenclature, not material function, approval, product performance or concentration. Ingredient family descriptions preserve exact preparation. No rank, amount, weight, penetration or residue label is assigned.
- Exactly enumerated bilingual brackets, slashes and parenthetical common names preserve species/part/preparation. They are scoped editorial wrappers, not chemical synonyms. Do not generalize punctuation removal, replace species, add SEED, or change a slash compound.
- The parent retains input-scoped repair of Helianthus Annus and Coco Caprylate/Caprate. Neither typo is a universal alias here. The Alzo primary supplier supports the latter spelling; parent owns correspondence and provenance.
- The five flags remain independent closed identity tests. Terpineol, Tetramethyl Acetyloctahydronaphthalenes, Juniperus Virginiana Oil and Citrus Aurantium Peel Oil establish fragrance exposure. They do not join the agreed scalp-active list. Mentha arvensis leaf oil remains fragrance/scalp-positive. Botanical extracts and fixed oils do not establish undeclared constituent allergens.
- No entry adds a recognized Annex VI filter. Colourants, antioxidant esters and peptides do not establish UV. Do not infer drying alcohol from diol/polyether names or mineral oil from hydrocarbon-polymer systems.
- Fixed seed/fruit oils have no positive hair-penetration admission here. Fermented oil extracts, plant-oil copolymers and Castor Isostearate Succinate do not inherit source-oil data. Broad alkane names have no automatic volatility assignment.
- Helianthus Annuus Oil remains pending: full glossary did not establish that exact no-SEED form. Resolve intended material identity before consuming it. Unknown roles can matter to the two-function auxiliary suffix; never silently treat all extracts/peptides as antioxidants or preservation markers.
