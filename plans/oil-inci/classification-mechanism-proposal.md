## Ordered classification mechanism

### Stage 0 — identity and evidence capture

Capture exact product/market/variant/size, ordered complete ingredient declaration, directions, pack mechanism and relevant claim excerpts. GTIN is useful corroboration, not an arbitrary requirement when the exact package is otherwise unambiguous. Source hierarchy: exact label; current exact local manufacturer; exact local retailer; fallback with explicit confidence/limits. Foreign-market lists do not become local lists without equivalence evidence. Do not merge divergent declarations.

Resolve identity/formula conflicts before a completed record. Missing permitted property observations can yield `unknown`; this is different from an incomplete formula. An unreviewed claim/directions/hero section cannot silently yield a completed negative or empty list. Preserve source date/excerpts and raw and normalized INCI hashes, identity/package fingerprint, method and lexicon revisions.

### Stage 1 — blind ingredient evidence ledger

Classifier sees a neutral slot, complete ordered ingredient list and allowed physical technical observations, with sources redacted so they do not disclose brand/selection rationale. Identity resolution remains auditable outside the blind packet. Classifier sees no hero claims, marketing, old labels, fit output, other lane or source-preflight selection descriptions.

For each decision-relevant ingredient retain exact form, normalized name, original rank, recognized functional roles, source/lexicon entry and ambiguity. Normalization does not erase plant part, oil/extract distinctions, hydrogenation, PEG modifications or polymer/grade uncertainty. Grade-specific supplier data is not proof of the retail ingredient grade.

### Stage 2 — blind formula properties

| Property | Ordered emission method | Required challenge / abstention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `base` | Identify actual carrier/vehicle roles across the full list. Water-leading credible aqueous architecture → water_based. No competing carrier and one intact botanical oil → pure_plant_oil; two or more → plant_oil_blend. Otherwise choose silicone_led or ester_alkane_led only when that family explains the backbone. Hybrid requires affirmative joint backbone evidence. | Identify strongest rival and explain why it loses. Ingredient counts/ranks are not mass shares. Unknown carrier family or equally supported led/mixed interpretations → unknown; hybrid is not uncertainty shorthand. Stabilizer convention is pending above. |
| `weight` | Judge supported volatile vehicle, fast-spreading but nonvolatile emollients, residual film/structural lipids, aqueous conditioning matrix and interactions. Light = coherent low residual/sensory-load architecture; medium = affirmative intermediate mobile-emollient/film architecture; rich = coherent substantial nonvolatile/structured-film architecture. | Challenge lighter and richer alternatives. Base never maps directly to weight; unspecified Dimethicone grade, unresolved residual load or competing interpretations → unknown. No one-ingredient rich floor and no medium default. These are calibration conventions, not scientifically measured cutoffs. |
| `penetration_potential` | Relevant exact-formula experiment wins within its conditions. Otherwise estimate penetration_potential when admitted penetrating oil(s), initially exact coconut/camellia/safflower, form a principal plant-conditioning backbone; estimate surface_led when a coherent film/emollient backbone predominates and studied oils are secondary. Pure mineral oil supplies an affirmative surface anchor. | Secondary positive oil never automatically wins; no absent-study negative or “silicones never enter” claim. Challenge the opposing architecture; choose a preferable anchor at low confidence, unknown for balanced/decisive delivery ambiguity or plant-only systems without an admitted relevant anchor. |
| `build_up_potential` | Estimate elevated for a coherent substantial gum/resin/wax/rich persistent-coating architecture; estimate low for a coherent fluid/spreading weak-film system without substantial residual-film counter-signals. Consider the full residual conditioning system under comparable small-application assumptions. | Volatile carrier does not cancel a prominent gum/film; lone late gum does not impose elevated. Plant/silicone origin never decides it. Exact-formula removal evidence can challenge the estimate. Unknown for genuinely balanced residual-film readings; no measured accumulation/cleanser claim follows. |
| Five ingredient flags | Exact recognized name/form match in complete INCI → true plus matched identities. Reviewed complete list with no recognized match → false. Keep all five keys. | Candidate lexicon and alias/form coverage must be versioned and resolved before each run; unresolved material recognition is a review issue, never a fabricated false. No silicone flag implies a weight/build-up/penetration class. |

All inferred base/weight/penetration/residue and non-absent prominence values carry `estimate: true`, confidence moderate or low, anchor, positive evidence, strongest counter-signal and nearest-alternative rationale. Exact identities/ranks, observed pack/use facts and claim-gated booleans carry their actual observation/claim basis, not an efficacy estimate. A missing ingredient grade lowers confidence when the remaining architecture still supports a winner; it does not require automatic unknown. Unknown remains when identity is unresolved or the competing interpretation is genuinely as good. Do not use a heuristic on an incomplete input or turn an estimate into measured scientific evidence.

**Main-session disposition of the agent proposals:** retain ordered whole-formula subjudgments and the two-function auxiliary-context heuristic. Reject the proposed automatic “first two carriers differ → hybrid” shortcut: it can turn a minor co-carrier into a defining backbone despite contradictory whole-list evidence. Hybrid instead requires affirmative co-backbone interpretation, clearly marked as estimated. Count every intact botanical oil when distinguishing one-oil from plant-blend identity; the tail-context heuristic must not erase oils from that inventory.

Freeze Stage 2 outputs before unblinding. Correcting a formula/lexicon/rule later creates a new derivation; positioning cannot rewrite the blind formula assessment.

### Stage 3 — controlled unblinding of product facts

| Property / fact | Mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Named oils | Assess each exact advertised hero after unblinding. Absent requires resolved identity + no exact recognized match. Leading requires frozen principal-carrier role. Supporting requires affirmative secondary role. Trace is a low-confidence estimate only under the auxiliary-context rule below, never an amount. Reviewed no hero → empty list. |
| Dispenser | Exact pack/photo/directions observation → approved mechanism. Other names mechanism. Missing mechanism → unknown, not a guessed pour. Jar/semisolid handling is a pending form mapping if encountered. |
| Heat | Accepted exact-product manufacturer/pack heat claim → true; completed review without accepted claim → false. No ingredient AND test, temperatures or inferred grade. |
| UV | Recognized exact INCI filter **AND** accepted manufacturer hair-UV claim → true. Filter-only, claim-only and neither → false after completed review. Generic sun-aftercare, antioxidant, colour positioning or skin SPF does not establish the hair claim. |
| Supported uses | Capture coherent repeatable exact-direction scenarios. Pre-wash fibre role needs fibre site + subsequent wash context; scalp-only instructions do not add fibre role. Damp/general retained fibre application establishes leave_on_fibre_conditioning; explicit dry retained post-wash/styling application establishes dry_finish. Both damp/dry retained alternatives establish both with coherent scenarios. Literal “conditioning”/“finish” wording is unnecessary. Dry pre-wash does not establish dry_finish. Scalp needs explicit scalp application. Do not infer overnight, dose or rinse behavior. |

Each scenario retains role, site, damp/dry stage, dose and actual unit, contact time, rinse/shampoo requirement, restrictions and source, with unestablished facts visible. Multiple scenarios per role are allowed. One scenario may establish more than one role only when the same exact instructions explicitly support them; cross-scenario facts are never recombined. Application and hero unblinding cannot change blind formula truth.

**Auxiliary-context trace heuristic:** in the blind role ledger, identify the earliest suffix containing at least two independent recognized auxiliary functions in different ingredients (e.g. fragrance and preservation/antioxidant), with an established carrier earlier and no subsequent competing vehicle, synthetic emollient, structurant or unresolved ingredient. Only recognized auxiliaries and intact botanical oils can occur in that suffix. Several fragrance allergens count as one function; a dual-purpose ingredient cannot supply both; colourants cannot establish the boundary because of their ordering exemption. A present advertised oil assigned only to that suffix can be estimated trace, with the two markers and actual rank retained. An already established principal/secondary role takes precedence; use unknown for conflicting roles. This explicitly approximate context rule never estimates percentage or proves negligible effects. With one marker or an interrupted suffix, no tail rule exists.

**Flag/claim integration details:** keep mineral_oil as the exact liquid-mineral-oil identity/aliases in the seed; petroleum waxes/other hydrocarbons require an explicit future scope extension. Fragrance denotes declared fragrance/allergen exposure, so Benzyl Alcohol can match even without intentional perfuming; it does not match drying_alcohol. Current-modern rosemary oil aliases and other encountered unmatched preparations need identity validation before freeze. Ethylhexyl Salicylate is a separately confirmed filter seed (Annex VI/20, CAS 118-60-5; [official identity row](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32009R1223)), alongside the filter identities in the flag report. Regulatory recognition is function/identity evidence, not product compliance or hair efficacy. Under the existing exact hair-UV-claim rule, explicit protection from UV-caused hair-colour fading qualifies within that retained scope; general colour/antioxidant/sun wording does not.

### Stage 4 — separate fit and completion

Preserve every requested thickness, texture and damage row. Derived fit consumes direct properties and the relevant role/scenario only through a separately approved policy. Initially unresolved fit policy produces unknown with a policy-gap reason; it does not manufacture universal suitability or production thickness eligibility.

Completed record validation: required property keys/all five flags, unique use members, reviewed empty semantics, no null/omission defaults, true flag matches, exact claim provenance, conditional fit conditions, other-dispenser mechanism, no matched rank for absent hero and immutable method/input/version references. Unknowns name their missing evidence. Separate record-completion status from substantive property resolution and production-projectability.

## Logic probes before freezing a method

These are specified acceptance cases, not tests already run or product labels.

1. Four UV combinations produce true only for filter+hair claim; incomplete formula blocks completion separately.
2. Heat claim is true even with no admitted ingredient heat mechanism; unsupported generic heat wording is false after claim review.
3. Fatty alcohol is not drying alcohol; rosemary leaf extract is not rosemary essential oil; fragrance allergen recognition does not prove perfuming intent.
4. Water-leading structured matrix does not automatically become light; silicone flag does not force elevated or surface-led.
5. Unspecified dimethicone grade can leave weight unknown while base is sufficiently supported.
6. Mixed carriers with no clear leader do not default hybrid or medium.
7. Hero oil last in INCI does not establish trace percentage; a two-function auxiliary suffix may establish contextual estimated trace, while one marker/several allergens/colour alone cannot. Unreviewed positioning does not create an empty hero list.
8. Scalp-before-shampoo and overnight-ends instructions remain separate; a scalp-only scenario does not establish pre-wash fibre use.
9. Missing dispenser produces allowed unknown; inconsistent exact formulas block rather than fill absent-match booleans.
10. Same formula/different pack can share formula truth while dispenser/dose/package fingerprints differ.
11. Damp-or-dry leave-in directions yield both appropriate fibre roles; dry pre-wash yields only the wash-out role; absent stage/context is not invented.
