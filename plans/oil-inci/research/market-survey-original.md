## C. Coverage check
- **Shine:** no property, deliberately dropped. Type (b): shine is claimed by nearly every product regardless of base.
- **Heat protection:** covered by property 6.
- **Anti-frizz/humidity:** partly covered by Base, since silicone or film-former carriers are what sit on the fibre, and by Build-up. The "blocks humidity" effect itself is type (b).
- **Repair/bond/split ends:** type (b). K18's first eight INCI entries (caprylyl caprylate, squalane, avocado, sunflower, alkanes) show no peptide. Isana Plex's INCI shows a silicone/isoalkane carrier plus plant extracts. The "repair" rests on proprietary actives.
- **Scalp/growth:** scalp use is covered by property 7. The growth claim is type (c), and I recommend not modelling it. The formula-readable part is a gap: scalp oils contain rosemary, menthol, camphor, peppermint and eucalyptus. Type (a), partly covered by flag 9.
- **Pre-wash/overnight:** covered by property 7. Overnight is a duration variant of pre-wash or leave-on. Fable & Mane's "not a leave-in" is expressed by the absence of leave-on uses.
- **Weightless/fine hair:** property 3 (weight) plus derived thickness.
- **Porosity:** Nanoil sells three formulas by porosity, with different oil sets by design. This is not a derived fit axis in your set. It is a genuine difference (type a) that can be read from the named oils, related to properties 3 and 5.
- **Curl definition:** derived curl pattern. The rest is type (b).
- **Blonde/toning:** "for bleached hair" is type (b). An anti-brass toning oil would presumably contain a colourant (not verified), which would be readable from INCI, type (a), and rare.
- **UV/sun:** type (b). Nanoil's low-porosity page lists a UV filter, so there is a small INCI-readable element.
- **Multi-use body/face/hair:** a product-scope difference (type a, readable from pack). Not captured by your properties.
- **Natural/vegan/anti-ageing/scent:** flag 9, Base, or type (b).

## D. Verdict on dropping "main strength"
The evidence supports dropping it.

- **Silicone-serum base.** Garnier Wunderöl (nourish dry, dull hair; three uses), Olaplex No.7 (shine, 72h frizz, 450°F) and Moroccanoil Original (shine, faster drying) are all silicone-carrier oils with a plant oil. Their headline claims differ in wording, not in formula class.
- **Evaporating "dry oil" carrier.** Living Proof Vanishing Oil (isoalkane carrier, humidity block), Isana Plex (isododecane plus dimethiconol, "repair and seal") and K18 (squalane/esters plus C13-15 alkane, "repair") share a carrier type. Their effects are sold as different, but the differences rest on proprietary actives that classic INCI reading cannot verify.
- **Same brand, same claims, different weight.** Schwarzkopf Oil Ultime Rose versus Argan, and Moroccanoil Original versus Light, share the same claims. They differ only in weight and target hair, which property 3 and the derived thickness already cover.
- **Real differences track use-case, not effect.** Scalp oils (Neqi, Mielle, Fable & Mane) versus finishing oils (Moroccanoil, Wella) are a genuine split, but it is use and application site (property 7) plus ingredients (flag 9), not a different "strength". The other real split is Nanoil's oil chemistry by porosity.

## E. Top 3 recommended changes
1. **Split flag 9's essential-oil part into a "scalp-active/cooling botanicals" flag.** The flag would cover menthol, camphor, peppermint, eucalyptus, rosemary and tea tree. Evidence: Neqi and Mielle INCI lists, and six scalp products in the set. It is formula-readable and relevant to scalp application. It keeps growth claims out (type c).
2. **Add porosity as a derived fit, alongside damage level.** Nanoil is the clearest case of a brand selling different plant-oil sets by porosity. Also make property 5 (penetrates or coats) depend on the oil classes present in a plant-oil blend: coconut, babassu and coconut alkanes in the low-porosity Nanoil versus omega-rich oils in its high-porosity variant. I have not verified the INCI of the normal- and high-porosity Nanoil variants.
3. **Add low-priority scope tags, not properties: "hair-specific vs multi-use (face/body)" and "formulated for bleached/toned hair".** Evidence: Nuxe and the pure oils for the first; Kérastase Blond Absolu and Moroccanoil Purple for the second. The second is pack-claim-led, so keep it a tag.

I found no missing marketing direction that would justify reinstating "main strength" or adding protein/moisture or repair properties.
