## Why the gap changes this decision

The first ingredient is Calendula flower oil and the second is decolorized Aloe leaf oil. Their names do not disclose their preparation media. The approved natural reference deliberately gives Calendula no standalone weight. Different plausible carriers have different hair-weight tendencies; identifying one as sunflower, coconut or another carrier would be invention.

The known formula contains retained botanical-oil signals, including Morete, and exact Sacha Inchi has a lighter material tendency. Neither ingredient order nor the coexistence of these tendencies establishes an intermediate blend. This is why the current B0/B7 decision remains unknown. The interpretation is not blocked merely by absent percentages: it is blocked by the identities of the first-order preparations.

**Conditional recommendation for that explicit exception: medium, with rich as the nearest alternative.** This is a feasible bounded product judgment, although the evidence cannot establish a formula-derived winner under B0/B7. The affirmative reason to avoid light is the retained botanical conditioning system, including declared coconut-family material, Morete and Sacha Inchi, without a declared mobile ester/alkane or volatile-silicone carrier architecture. Those conditional possibilities are not assertions about the real carriers. This ranking is practical and weak rather than experimentally established; equal-dose hair experience with the exact product would be more useful now than repeating broad supplier searches.
