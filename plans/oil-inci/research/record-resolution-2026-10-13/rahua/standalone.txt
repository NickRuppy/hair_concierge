Product details
A fast-absorbing finishing oil that smooths frizz, boosts shine, and protects hair from daily environmental stressors. Rahua oil strengthens strands from within, antioxidant-rich morete oil helps defend against UV damage while preserving moisture, and omega-rich sacha inchi deeply hydrates to improve softness and elasticity. Just a few drops leave hair sleek, luminous, and healthy-looking.

How to use
Warm 1-3 drops in palms, work through dry hair. Start small and work your way up. Good for daily use.
Key Ingredients
Rahua Oil
Amazonian tree nut oil with superfine molecules that fortify strands, help maintain scalp health, and lock in color. Sustainably harvested through our Symbiotic® partnerships.
Morete Oil
Rich in vitamins A, C, and E, this Amazonian fruit oil protects hair from damaging UVA rays while enhancing moisture.
Sacha Inchi
Exceptionally rich in omega-3 fatty acids, this ancient seed oil locks in moisture while soothing the scalp.

Our community says this hair oil makes hair soft, shiny, and healthy-looking without weighing it down. Users appreciate that a small amount goes a long way, with many using just one pump on wet or damp hair, particularly on ends and mid-shaft. The oil effectively controls frizz and flyaways while adding shine and smoothness.
