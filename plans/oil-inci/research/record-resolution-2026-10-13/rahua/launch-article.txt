The Legend Lives On: Legendary Amazon Oil™
December 7, 2018
We are thrilled to announce that
Rahua Legendary Amazon Oil™
has launched on rahua.com!  We honor the legend of the miracle hair oil by partnering with indigenous families in the Amazon, drawing on their ancient wisdom.  We bring this new product to life through sustainable methods that preserve special, cultural rituals. The oils are produced in the same complex and sustainable fashion they have been for generations.
With an airy, fresh floral aroma, one or two pumps of this quick-absorbing, lightweight oil immediately replenishes hair, restores vitality and smooths cuticles. Formulated especially for daily use and for all hair types, this versatile treatment can be applied daily on wet or dry hair. This exclusive blend of plant and flower oils delivers a beautifully smooth, shiny, anti-frizz finish.
Key Benefits
• Prevents Frizz and Flyaways
• Nourishes & Strengthens Hair
• Prevents Split Ends
• Boosts Shine
Star Ingredients
• Rainforest Grown Rahua, Morete and Sacha Inchi Oils
• Organic Calendula Flower Oil
• Aloe Leaf Oil
• Gardenia Enfleurage Oil
THE LEGEND
The Amazon Rainforest is a majestic and mystical place in its culture and ancient wisdom. While growing up in Ecuador in a family of hair dressers, Fabian Lliguin, our founder, always heard of the mysterious legend of a powerful, nutritious oil from the Amazon that transforms hair. It wasn’t until the 1990’s when Fabian was visiting the rainforest, educating indigenous people about their rights, that he discovered this legend to be true. Surrounded by women with fantastically long, lustrous hair that cascaded past their waists, he discovered their secret to be Rahua oil. For centuries, the women had relied on this oil to nourish their hair and skin.
