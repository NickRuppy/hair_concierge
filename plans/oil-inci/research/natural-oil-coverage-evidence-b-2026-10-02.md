# Natural fixed-oil coverage evidence — slice B

2026-10-02. Advisory research: 16 exact identities and 26 primary/excluded source rows. All weight confidence is low; no standardized hair-heaviness validation. Main persisted the compact handback after worker filesystem became inaccessible, and will validate it locally. See [source/material ledger](natural-oil-coverage-evidence-b-2026-10-02.json).

Proposals: 13 light, two medium (buriti, coffee), one rich (Inophyllum tamanu). Buriti medium is an explicit rough compromise across conflicting light/rich skin descriptors; unknown is defensible. Coffee is selected within the directly reported light-to-medium range, with green/roasted processing unresolved. Raspberry and cranberry rely on low-grease descriptions, weaker than explicit light texture.

The [meadowfoam study](https://meadowfoam.com/wp-content/uploads/MSO_assessment_hair_effect_LTR_ENG_03282022.pdf) compares 5% emollient in conditioners in a 15-person half-head design and assesses conditioning, not heaviness. Nonsignificance does not prove equivalence. Three roses have separate exact identity evidence. Incarnata passionfruit, Tacamahaca tamanu and Oleifera camellia sources are excluded from the other species' positive evidence. Kukui name variants need reconciliation. The Sinensis page body/URL processing discrepancy remains visible.

Supplier medical, growth and UV claims are not adopted. Density, fatty-acid composition, viscosity thresholds and penetration cannot decide weight. Grade, dose and formula architecture can change practical feel. No files outside the two assigned evidence files, runs, standards, database or runtime were changed by this research handback.
