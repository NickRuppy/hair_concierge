# Exploratory penetration and residue estimates

Date: 2026-10-01. **Proposed operational rules for the first eight exploratory products; not calibrated.** Nick authorized approximate rules clearly marked as estimates. This permits engineering judgments beyond direct experiments; it does not strengthen the scientific evidence. The [evidence review](penetration-residue-evidence.md) remains unchanged. No actual product labels are assigned here.

## Shared interpretation and record

Read the complete INCI as a formula, distinguishing the spreading/evaporative carrier from the conditioning material expected to remain. Use ingredient order as qualitative evidence of architecture, alongside ingredient function and the coherence of the surrounding ingredients. Do not convert order into percentages, assume a fixed top-N boundary, or let a single ingredient decide the result.

“Principal backbone” means the most defensible structural reading of the formula, not a claim that a class exceeds 50%. A coherent early sequence of comparable emollients is stronger evidence than an isolated ingredient. Check the entire list for a competing film system. A carrier at the head of the list does not necessarily define the residual conditioning backbone.

For each approximated judgment record **`estimate: true`**, `confidence: moderate|low`, the selected anchor, positive evidence, counterevidence and a one-sentence nearest-alternative rationale. These are review metadata, not new customer properties. Moderate means one clear architecture with limited conflicting evidence; low means a plausible best interpretation with an important unresolved counter-signal. Use `unknown` only when competing interpretations remain genuinely balanced or missing identity/delivery information could reasonably reverse the choice. Incomplete formula identity still blocks the record upstream.

Keep penetration and residue independent: a penetrating oil can leave residue, and a surface-led product can have a light residual film. Packaging, luxury positioning, “weightless,” “deep nourishment” and “silicone-free” do not supply the answer.

## Penetration: ordered approximation

Meaning: **credible penetrating-oil potential is structurally relevant to this formula** versus **expected conditioning action is predominantly at the surface**. Neither label measures the fraction entering hair or efficacy at the printed dose/contact time.

1. **Resolve an applicable exact-formula experiment first**, if available, and retain its conditions. Do not override relevant direct findings with this heuristic. A measurable trace of internal material alone does not settle the formula's predominant expected action.
2. **Choose `penetration_potential` when admitted penetrating oil(s) form the principal plant-conditioning backbone.** Initial strongest candidates are exact coconut, Camellia oleifera and safflower oils. They may occur in a pure oil, plant blend, or an ester/silicone hybrid. Secondary other oils or spreading esters do not automatically defeat the judgment. Positive signals: an admitted oil leads a coherent plant-oil conditioning sequence; other constituents support delivery/spreading without a comparably prominent persistent film system. Counter-signals: the studied oil looks like a secondary addition within a substantial silicone/ester/alkane conditioning architecture; short-contact delivery differs materially from the studies. The latter lowers transfer confidence rather than proving no potential.
3. **Choose `surface_led` when a coherent surface-film/emollient architecture is the principal conditioning system and admitted penetrating oils are absent or secondary.** Positive examples are mineral-oil-led conditioning or a silicone gum/fluid/ester serum whose structural reading is film deposition. This is an affirmative architecture estimate, not “the ingredient was not studied.” Silicones and esters do not receive universal nonpenetration tags. Counter-signals: admitted oils also form a coherent principal backbone, relevant direct internal-delivery evidence, or uncertain emulsion delivery that materially changes the interpretation.
4. **Choose the nearest anchor with low confidence for a hybrid when one reading is still better supported.** Say why the other reading loses. Use `unknown` for a genuinely balanced plant/film backbone, material delivery uncertainty, or a plant-only blend with no admitted or separately accepted comparable penetration anchor. Absence of an admitted oil alone never supplies a surface-led label.

**Boundary:** extension of the admitted ingredient set remains a lexicon decision. Argan/avocado evidence identified in the science note can support a later explicit admission; this estimate policy does not silently admit every plant triglyceride or derivative. Fractionated/hydrogenated materials do not inherit coconut's entry by name association.

## Build-up: ordered approximation

Meaning: **relative propensity for a persistent conditioning film/load**, not measured repeated accumulation or harm. Compare formulas under the same hypothetical small leave-on application mass per hair mass, distribution, hair condition, elapsed time and washing context. This is an analytical comparison assumption, not a dose recommendation. Do not compare the amount from one product's pump with another's drops. Record a materially different supported-use scenario, such as shampooed-out pre-wash use, as a transfer limitation; it does not change the INCI's residue architecture.

The relative anchors are **a fluid spreading formula with a weak residual film (`low`)** and **a gum/resin/wax-rich or otherwise substantial durable conditioning film (`elevated`)**. “Low” does not mean residue-free; “elevated” does not mean difficult to clean, unsuitable, or that a particular cleanser is required.

1. **Prioritize relevant exact-formula deposition/removal evidence**, if available. Retain its comparator and conditions. A high stable plateau can still be a substantial persistent film; no growth across cycles alone does not establish low load.
2. **Choose `elevated` for a coherent substantial persistent-film architecture.** Positive signals: gum/resin/film-former constituents occupy a structurally important position in the conditioning sequence; several durable conditioning components reinforce that reading; or a rich viscous oil/wax system supports a substantial residual coating. A volatile carrier does not cancel the material left behind. Counter-signals: the film former is an isolated secondary component within a fluid system, relevant removal/low-load data, or evidence that the apparent viscosity comes from the carrier rather than a persistent film. Do not require exact commercial grade unless the distinction could reverse the judgment.
3. **Choose `low` for a coherent fluid/weak-film architecture without a substantial persistent-film counter-signal.** Positive signals: a spreading volatile/fluid carrier with a modest-looking residual emollient system, or a simple freely spreading plant/ester oil blend without a gum/resin/wax or rich-coating architecture. These are qualitative estimates, never inferred low percentages. Counter-signals: important nonvolatile gum/resin/wax components, several rich persistent conditioners, or a sticky/viscous material architecture supported by ingredient evidence. Plant origin and silicone absence are not positive evidence by themselves.
4. **Challenge hybrids against both anchors.** An early volatile ingredient with a structurally important gum system usually stays nearer `elevated`; a fluid ester/silicone system with only an isolated secondary film component may stay nearer `low`. Document the decisive positive and counter-signal. If one anchor is preferable despite uncertainty, use it with low confidence; use `unknown` when residual-film strength is genuinely balanced or a missing grade/delivery fact is decisive.

These residue rules are explicitly an engineering approximation. Related-formula experiments show that composition and delivery change deposition and removal; they do not validate these exact class boundaries. A matched ingredient's existence alone never proves a substantial film. [Primary repeated-deposition study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11382849/), [supplier carrier/gum documentation](https://www.dow.com/en-us/market/mkt-beauty-personal-care/sub-beauty-hair-care/dowsil-silicone-gum-blends.html).

## Recommendation to the owning session

Adopt these ordered estimates for the exploratory round and review low-confidence/boundary judgments explicitly. Keep the original evidence note as the scientific record. Do not tune the rules to make the eight products cover every class. Record recurring disagreements before changing an anchor; no current accuracy, reliability or finished-performance claim is justified. No application code, tests, database work, fit policy or consumer wording is introduced by this note.
