# Hazelnut seed oil: proposed hair-weight tendency

Prepared 2026-10-03. **Proposal for Nick's next one-at-a-time decision; not an admitted material prior.** v0.7 and all completed trials remain unchanged.

## Identity and coverage gap

`CORYLUS AVELLANA SEED OIL` is the fixed hazelnut kernel/seed oil, not an extract, essential oil or hazelnut-named finished blend. Round-4 NUXE Q01 contains its source-scoped display declaration at rank six, but the 65-material v0.7 reference has no matching weight prior. The frozen run names this gap; the proposed addition cannot retrospectively fill that input.

Exact identity evidence: [COSMILE Europe](https://cosmileeurope.eu/inci/detail/3867/corylus-avellana-seed-oil/) and [Landema's own material page](https://landema.com/nos-extraits/huile-vegetale-noisette-bio), which declares 100% Corylus avellana seed oil. [Aroma-Zone's material specification](https://www.aroma-zone.com/info/fiche-technique/huile-vegetale-noisette-bio-aroma-zone) identifies pure virgin cold-pressed oil from Corylus avellana nuts. These sources establish identity/grade rather than a hair-weight class.

## Hair-specific evidence

- [Natural Heroes' own organic cold-pressed hazelnut oil page](https://www.naturalheroes.nl/en/products/hazelnootolie-biologisch-koudgeperst), read directly 2026-10-03, identifies Corylus avellana seed oil. Its hair-care section describes a light application that avoids weighing down hair and explicitly positions it for fine hair. This is supplier commercial hair-use guidance without a disclosed comparative test. The page's separate skin-absorption discussion does not determine the proposed class. Web reader timed out; the relevant page text was confirmed by a direct HTTP read.
- [Aroma-Zone's hair-oil selection guide](https://www.aroma-zone.com/products/huiles-capillaires/), read 2026-10-03, groups hazelnut with jojoba as lighter oils for hair prone to oiliness and describes avoiding heaviness. This is commercial hair-use guidance, not a controlled sensory comparison; oily hair is not equivalent to fine fiber diameter. Its nearby sebum, growth, penetration and repair claims are not adopted.

## Recommendation and competing interpretation

Propose **light / low confidence**, with **medium as the alternative**, for neat, unmodified hazelnut seed oil. Two primary commercial sources give affirmative hair-specific lightness guidance; one explicitly mentions fine hair. That is a practical reason to lean light under OE14's authorized approximation approach. It does not establish a precise position relative to jojoba, grapeseed or almond.

Medium remains plausible because neither source supplies a standardized retained-film or hair-heaviness comparison at equal application dose. The bounded search did not find such a controlled comparison. Dose, processing/grade and hair substrate could affect the result; no quantified effect or automatic thickness eligibility is established. Skin absorption, food density and generic fatty-acid-family similarity do not choose the class.
