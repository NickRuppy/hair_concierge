## Important boundaries

- The corn supplier cross-lists Oil and Germ Oil for one grade; the historical inventory defines distinct declarations. Keep them separate.
- Oat Lipid e is low-viscosity and non-greasy on skin, despite rich-lipid wording. Generic oat stays unknown; that exact grade is a light candidate.
- Refined linseed and soybean have explicit supplier hair-lightness descriptions. Rice bran has a use claim about not weighing hair down. None provides a blinded hair panel.
- Borage and evening primrose have conflicting residual-feel descriptions. Medium would be an unsupported compromise.
- Blackcurrant heavy/oily wording concerns scent. The only clear light wording found is repeated supplier boilerplate. Keep unknown unless the parent intentionally accepts that weak evidence.
- Both ordinary and high-oleic sunflower grades are called fairly light by Heess. The Hybrid Oil declaration still lacks an exact-grade sensory bridge.
- Macadamia Ternifolia has its own Oleon lightweight descriptor. The Integrifolia/Tetraphylla declaration has identity and low-viscosity evidence only.
- Rejected shortcuts: linoleic means light; penetrates means light; colour/density means weight; rich in nutrients means rich weight; any generic liquid oil means medium.
