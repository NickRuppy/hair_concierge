## Settled decisions

- Persistence is one `low | moderate | high` scalar for retained leave-on hair-fibre application. The reason and tipping assumption remain in a separate trace.
- Low needs an affirmative less-persistent mobile/dispersible remainder interpretation. Some residue is compatible with low; no class quota or requirement to find a low oil.
- Moderate positively means an ordinary retained fluid/lipid coating, never an uncertainty fallback.
- High needs an affirmative cohesive/adherent/structured conditioning-film architecture. Silicone/polymer presence, thickness, viscosity, stickiness, nonvolatility, penetration, base, marketing and spray application alone do not determine it.
- Exact conditioning-gum architecture can support high; a texture polymer is not automatically a conditioning film. Do not invent dimethicone grade or constituent quantities.
- Previously discussed product/material decisions are disclosed developmental anchors, withheld from assessors. They are neither measured persistence benchmarks nor unseen validation.
