# Oil build-up evidence and thickness-fit proposal — 2026-10-05

Status: completed research and proposed policy, awaiting the material fit decision. Nick authorized investigating the four remaining build-up cases and preparing thickness-fit guidance. OE14 already authorizes clearly marked approximate judgments; it does not require forcing a value without a useful evidential distinction. This document does not adopt new anchors, change existing classifications, or activate recommendations.

## Outcome

The targeted hair-evidence search did not find a controlled repeated-application/shampoo experiment that supplies a comparative build-up ranking for these four formulas. Some studies support surface-film redistribution and ingredient penetration. Those endpoints do not establish accumulation or cleanser requirements. The absence of a suitable study in this search is not proof that no study exists.

Recommendation: retain build-up as a distinct research property under the current standard, preserve these four unknowns, and do not use unknown build-up as a negative thickness-fit verdict. Proceed with explicit low-confidence, use-specific fit estimates from the separately established formula weight. This retains useful approximation without inventing a second weight scale.

## External hair evidence, independent of engine conventions

| Evidence | Actual endpoint and relevant conditions | Bounded interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keis et al., 2005, [Investigation of penetration abilities of various oils into human hair fibers](https://1.oliveoiltimes.com/library/olive-oil-hair.pdf) | Oil application to Indian hair, followed by interfiber withdrawal-force and optical surface-film observations immediately, after 24 hours, and with heat. Coconut surface film thinned; mustard retained visible cuticle-masking film; olive findings varied with treatment. | Single-treatment surface behavior can inform a weak film-persistence estimate. It does not measure successive application/shampoo cycles. Penetration does not mean disappearance or easy washout. |
| Marsh et al., 2024, [Penetration of oils into hair](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/ics.12978) | Differential extraction and labelled-triglyceride NanoSIMS; coconut, safflower and camellia oils. Experimental dose/time/temperature differ substantially from a few drops of ordinary leave-on oil. | Many triglycerides and the tested oils can penetrate. A blanket polyunsaturated-oil/nonpenetrating/high-build-up shortcut is unsound. Penetration does not establish repeated surface accumulation. |
| Lourenco et al., 2024, [primary Raman/mechanical study](https://doi.org/10.3390/cosmetics11020064) | Argan, avocado and coconut oil treatments on virgin and bleached hair; Raman and mechanical endpoints. This is supporting evidence, not necessary for the proposed decision. |
| Kim and Ahn, 2023, [coconut/MCT treatment study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40691-023-00332-0) | Daily application of a cream containing oil to severely bleached hair for 21 days, followed by water rinsing; microscopy, lipid and mechanical/protein endpoints. | Repeated treatment alone is not a progressive surface-residue endpoint. Vehicle, damage and water rinsing limit transfer to neat oils and shampoo removal. |
| Supplier-authored [avocado-oil/isoamyl-laurate hair study](https://sofw.com/en/sofw-journal/articles-en/48-personal-care/4816-natural-oil-and-ester-mixture-as-a-high-performing-sustainable-alternative-to-silicone-oils-in-hair-care) | Blend used in shampoo formulations; combing, sensory and damaged-hair hydrophobicity measurements. | Hair evidence exists, but does not isolate isoamyl laurate or establish low build-up for an anhydrous leave-on. |
| Mamada and Nakamura, 2007, [hair volume/bounce and bending elasticity study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17938824/) | Hair diameter and bending mechanics measured in volunteer hair. Hair volume/bounce also depended on intrinsic mechanical changes and density. | Diameter matters to hair behavior but cannot alone validate a product-specific fit matrix. The matrix below is a practical policy proposal, not an experimentally demonstrated threshold. |

No suitable direct comparative build-up data were located for castor, almond, baobab, jojoba or broccoli oils. Castor viscosity/stickiness is a plausible handling and sensory signal; it does not provide a measured accumulation coefficient. A case report is insufficient to turn this into a universal castor exclusion. Skin absorption, dry-touch marketing and cosmetic density tables were not used as hair build-up evidence.

## Internal reconciliation

Current [oil standard](../../../docs/research/oil-inci/v1.0/02_classification-standard-v0.1.md) distinguishes expected sensory load (`weight`) from relative persistent conditioning-film/load propensity (`build_up_potential`). Build-up is explicitly not measured repeated accumulation or a cleanser requirement. It needs coherent whole-formula affirmative signals; plant/silicone origin, volatility, an oil's presence or one late gum cannot decide it.

The four current unknowns are consistent with that rule. The research does not justify converting a rich-weight class, coconut penetration, or castor rank into an automatic build-up result. An elevated castor/mustard-rich prior could be proposed as a separate weak mechanistic rule, but would need explicit anchors, counter-signals and review; it is not adopted here.

Conditioner and Leave-In offer transferable separation principles: silicone alone does not determine heaviness, formula truth is distinct from fit, and route/dose matter. Their residue anchors and thickness matrices belong to their own category contracts; they are not inherited as oil-engine rules.

## Thickness-fit policy proposal

First decision: use weight as a soft preference for an established fibre application, rather than a universal product exclusion by diameter. Recommendation: conditional fit for richer formulas on fine hair, with exact-use/dose context retained. Alternative: label rich retained formulas `not_suitable` for fine hair to prioritize volume protection. That is simpler and more selective, but overstates what diameter and approximate weight can prove and hides usable small-end applications.

Proposed ordinary retained-fibre matrix:

| Formula weight | Fine | Normal | Coarse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| light | suitable | suitable | suitable |
| medium | conditional | suitable | suitable |
| rich | conditional | conditional | suitable |
| unknown | unknown | unknown | unknown |

`Suitable` means no thickness/weight mismatch established for the supported scenario; it does not guarantee adequate conditioning, individual satisfaction or compatibility on the other fit axes. `Conditional` means extra load/flattening concern: keep to a small, controlled application on the source-supported site, using the published dose where available; do not invent a numerical dose or application permission. Fit confidence cannot exceed the robustness of its approximate weight input.

Additional proposed scenario handling, still pending:

- Explicit fibre pre-wash followed by shampoo: do not exclude any thickness solely because the oil is rich. This is not a washout guarantee; direct use restrictions still apply.
- Scalp-only use: fibre thickness fit remains unknown with a reason that the scenario does not establish a fibre application. No scalp safety, tolerance or growth verdict follows.
- Mixed-use products: retain a verdict for each supported scenario. No single global fit may conceal a retained-finish caveat behind wash-out permission.
- Unknown build-up: retain the research gap alongside fit; it neither becomes low nor automatically makes a product unsuitable.
- Positive estimated build-up: show the residual-film caveat separately. Do not apply a second automatic thickness penalty merely duplicating weight.
- Curl and damage fit remain pending separate policy; no inference from diameter and no repair efficacy.

If Nick selects the soft-preference direction, the next decision is the exact matrix and application-scenario conditions. Then version the normative policy and its consuming guidance, and derive a separate reviewed application overlay. Do not edit frozen round-6 records, compare the proposed map as observed sensory truth, or convert lane agreement into validity.
