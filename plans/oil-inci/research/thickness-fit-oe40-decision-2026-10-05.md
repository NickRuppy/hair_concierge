# OE40 — rich leave-on oils and fine hair

Nick replied **“okokok”** to the recommendation: **conditional fit for rich oils left on fine hair**, with small, controlled application to source-supported ends and a flattening/load caveat. The approved direction is a soft preference, rather than excluding a product solely because its estimated weight is rich.

This decision settles that cell and direction only. It does not approve every cell of the complete thickness matrix, new numerical doses, pre-wash/scalp-fit rules, curl/damage fit, or a production eligibility mapping. Existing formula weights and build-up values remain unchanged. A condition must retain the exact supported scenario and available manufacturer directions; it cannot invent permission to apply to the ends.

All remaining cells are proposed. `Suitable` means no weight/thickness mismatch identified for the supported scenario, not guaranteed performance. `Conditional` flags load/flattening concern and requires source-supported placement and controlled application. Fit robustness cannot exceed its approximate weight input. Unknown build-up is not automatically a poor-fit result.

Proposed route rules for the same next review: an explicitly supported fibre pre-wash followed by shampoo is not excluded by diameter/weight alone; a scalp-only instruction does not establish fibre-thickness fit; mixed-use products retain separate scenario verdicts.
