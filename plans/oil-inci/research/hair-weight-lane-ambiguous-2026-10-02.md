# Hair-weight lane: ambiguous oils — 2026-10-02

Proposed completion: **12 LIGHT, 3 MEDIUM, 1 RICH; all low confidence.** These are approximate tendencies on hair after comparable modest leave-on application. No weight class is measured or validated. Exact INCI and aliases remain separate.

The physical inference is narrow: mobile oils can distribute into thinner, less localized films; more viscous oils may leave more localized coating. This does not establish less residual mass, penetration, or zero limpness. Temperature and grade remain important. The [hair formulation technical discussion](https://www.cosmeticsandtoiletries.com/magazine/article/22862759/cosmetics-toiletries-magazine-choosing-the-right-plant-oil-for-hair-and-skin-benefits) links viscosity, spreading, surface coverage and hair lubrication.

## Decisions and evidence

### rapeseed_napus

Neat rapeseed in a same-method comparison flows close to sunflower. At equal modest application this supports a readily distributed thin hair coating and LIGHT tendency; no skin-absorption transfer. Exact Napus identity is separately confirmed.

- [Primary source](https://ota-oil.co.jp/business/cosmetic-materials/brassica/): Supplier specifies Brassica Napus Seed Oil, expressed then refined.
- [Primary source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.5402/2011/531510): Balafoutis et al. 2011 Table 3: measured neat sunflower 40.96, rapeseed 42.38, cottonseed 41.67 cSt at 35 C; seeds screw-expelled and filtered. Species and cosmetic grade unspecified. Only neat-oil viscosity used; fuel blends and engine outcomes excluded.

Excluded: Supplier lightweight skin copy; Engine and fuel-blend performance.

Limit: Study botanical species/grade not specified; cosmetic Napus and high-erucic grades may differ.

### canola_declaration

RBD canola has a documented fluid liquid profile rather than castor-like thick flow. A thin distributed hair film at modest dose is a reasonable LIGHT engineering estimate, with MEDIUM credible; not a generic synonym of Napus/Campestris.

- [Primary source](https://ss3-production-public.s3.amazonaws.com/seller-document/1/28D9568BD1FBC0C6D73719F272893D13.pdf): LDC canola specification revision 7 June 2024: RBD canola with at most 0.0015% citric acid, typical viscosity 46.2 cP at 30 C. Food-grade physical anchor; not exact cosmetic batch measurement.
- [Primary source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.5402/2011/531510): Balafoutis et al. 2011 Table 3: measured neat sunflower 40.96, rapeseed 42.38, cottonseed 41.67 cSt at 35 C; seeds screw-expelled and filtered. Species and cosmetic grade unspecified. Only neat-oil viscosity used; fuel blends and engine outcomes excluded.

Excluded: AAK skin sensory; Diluted canola hair blends.

Limit: 30 C food-grade viscosity is not a room-temperature cosmetic hair test; ordinary intact canola only.

### flax_linseed

Exact refined Linum oil supplier explicitly describes lightweight hair moisturization. Retain LIGHT as weak practical guidance for fresh unmodified cosmetic oil.

- [Primary source](https://www.oand3.com/product/linseed-oil-refined/): Linum Usitatissimum Seed Oil grade has explicit lightweight hair moisturization wording.

Excluded: Skin lightness; Flax gel; Boiled/polymerized drying oils; Oxidation used as a weight ranking.

Limit: No dose, hair type or comparative panel disclosed; cold-pressed grades may differ.

### evening_primrose

Cold-pressed exact-species oil measured 54.6 mPa.s at 20 C: fluid enough for thin distribution over hair at equal modest dose. This is positive physical support for LIGHT, not averaging conflicting skin claims into MEDIUM.

- [Primary source](https://www.ippt.pan.pl/repository/open/o10344.pdf): Krysiak et al. 2020 ACS doi:10.1021/acsabm.0c00854, sections 2.6 and 3.3/Table 1: cold-pressed OlVita Oenothera biennis 54.6 +/-0.1 and Etja Borago officinalis 18.7 +/-0.1 mPa.s at 20 C. Neat rheology measured in triplicate; Newtonian. PVB wetting and skin tests are not hair results.

Excluded: Oily skin residue; Low-percentage blended hair claims; PVB/skin wetting as hair evidence.

Limit: Single supplier batch; remaining surface oil and strand clumping not measured; MEDIUM plausible.

### borage

Measured exact-species cold-pressed oil is low-viscosity (18.7 mPa.s at 20 C), favouring easy distribution and less concentrated film on hair. LIGHT is more defensible than RICH inferred from skin oiliness.

- [Primary source](https://www.ippt.pan.pl/repository/open/o10344.pdf): Krysiak et al. 2020 ACS doi:10.1021/acsabm.0c00854, sections 2.6 and 3.3/Table 1: cold-pressed OlVita Oenothera biennis 54.6 +/-0.1 and Etja Borago officinalis 18.7 +/-0.1 mPa.s at 20 C. Neat rheology measured in triplicate; Newtonian. PVB wetting and skin tests are not hair results.

Excluded: Oily versus lightweight skin descriptors; GLA content as heaviness; PVB spreading as hair measurement.

Limit: Unusually low measured value is one supplier sample; do not claim all borage grades share it or infer evaporation/absorption.

### camelina

Manufacturer explicitly describes light nourishment without weighing down the hair fibre and suitability even for fine hair; small amounts on lengths support LIGHT practical tendency.

- [Primary source](https://www.aroma-zone.com/page/bienfaits-huile-cameline): Manufacturer describes camelina as light nourishment of the hair fibre, small amounts on lengths/ends, suitable even for fine hair without weighing down. Nearby passage also mentions mixing oils: useful guidance, not a neat controlled test.

Excluded: Dry skin feel; Scalp/growth benefits; Mixed-oil prewash results.

Limit: Commercial advice includes mixing and very small dosage, so no equal-dose neat-oil validation.

### chia

Unmodified chia oil measured about 43 mPa.s at 20 C supplies a direct fluidity anchor. Thin distribution over strands is plausible at modest dose, favouring LIGHT without claiming rapid hair absorption.

- [Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1002/ppap.202300224): Bellmann et al. 2024: unmodified chia oil 46.7 mm2/s and density 0.930 at 293.15 K, calculated dynamic viscosity 43.4 mPa.s; plasma coating results not used.

Excluded: Light skin feel; Mousse/conditioner volume claims; Chia gel; Epoxidized or plasma-polymerized oil results.

Limit: Material from an engineering study rather than cosmetic hair test; spreading on hair remains inferred.

### blackcurrant

Primary physical study finds a mobile blackcurrant seed oil (~27 cSt at 40 C). Compared at the same temperature with typical seed oils, it supports a thin-film LIGHT tendency instead of interpreting its heavy odour as rich coating.

- [Primary source](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/10/24/9086): Olszak et al. 2020: supercritical-CO2-extracted blackcurrant seed oil in Fig 1 approximately 27 cSt at 40 C, visually read from PDF plot; lower than strawberry/chokeberry samples. This is bulk flow, not hair lubrication or cosmetic sensory.

Excluded: Heavy/oily odour; Skin non-greasy template; Hair-growth claims; Hydraulic performance as hair performance.

Limit: Supercritical extraction and 40 C are imperfect proxies for cold-pressed/refined room-temperature cosmetic oil; MEDIUM plausible.

### sunflower_hybrid

Exact Heess high-oleic grade may use either Seed Oil or Hybrid Oil declaration. Retain approved sunflower LIGHT tendency through this limited grade bridge and fluid triglyceride film rationale, with no universal alias or claim that every hybrid is identical.

- [Primary source](https://chemecosmetics.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Sunflower-oil-high-oleic-refined-organic_Product-Dossier_22.03.pdf): Gustav Heess product 220131: 100% refined organic high-oleic sunflower oil, expression from seeds, may carry Seed Oil OR Hybrid Oil INCI. Grade-specific bridge, not universal alias.
- [Primary source](https://www.cosmeticsandtoiletries.com/magazine/article/22862759/cosmetics-toiletries-magazine-choosing-the-right-plant-oil-for-hair-and-skin-benefits): Marsh, Knowles and Simmonds 2023: viscosity affects spreading/heaviness; hair lubrication depends on level and surface coverage. This supports physical inference, not measured classes.

Excluded: Skin fairly-light descriptor as proof; Oxidative stability as weight; Universal Seed Oil=Hybrid Oil alias.

Limit: Hybrid cultivar and grade variation can shift residual coating; approved ordinary sunflower remains a separate entry.

### macadamia_ternifolia

Suggest MEDIUM coherently with the macadamia family: producer physical anchor of 80 mPa.s at 20 C predicts somewhat more persistent localized coating than lower-viscosity seeds, yet far below castor. Exact ternifolia hair styling use supports a manageable film. Species transfer is explicit, weak, and not synonymy.

- [Primary source](https://www.mayomacs.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2025-Product-SPEC-Macadamia-Oil-Product-Specification-Non-Food-Grade-1.pdf): Mayo Macs mechanical-pressed integrifolia and/or tetraphylla kernel oil: viscosity specification 80 mPa.s at 20 C, 40 at 38 C. Family physical anchor, not proof for exact ternifolia or hybrid cosmetic declaration; method unspecified.
- [Primary source](https://naissance.com/products/macadamia-nut-oil): Refined/deodorized Macadamia ternifolia product is suggested on damp hair before styling for shine/frizz. Lightweight wording is predominantly skin/general, and separate mask is washed out; neither supplies a hair heaviness measurement.

Excluded: Oleon lightweight skin descriptor; Naissance skin absorption; Wash-out mask result; MakingCosmetics skin-rich/heavy-cream wording.

Limit: 80 mPa.s is integrifolia/tetraphylla family data, not measured ternifolia; refined ternifolia could be LIGHT. Difference from integrifolia is not established.

### macadamia_hybrid

MEDIUM is a material-led macadamia-family estimate: mechanically pressed integrifolia/tetraphylla oil has ~80 mPa.s at 20 C, while the exact hybrid supplier calls its refined oil low-viscosity. Together suggest a modest persistent lubricating film, not RICH. LIGHT remains close for refined grades.

- [Primary source](https://www.mayomacs.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2025-Product-SPEC-Macadamia-Oil-Product-Specification-Non-Food-Grade-1.pdf): Mayo Macs mechanical-pressed integrifolia and/or tetraphylla kernel oil: viscosity specification 80 mPa.s at 20 C, 40 at 38 C. Family physical anchor, not proof for exact ternifolia or hybrid cosmetic declaration; method unspecified.
- [Primary source](https://www.interchimie.fr/produits/huiles-vegetales/huile-de-noix-de-macadamia-raffinee/): Macadamia integrifolia tetraphylla seed oil is pressed/refined and low-viscosity.

Excluded: Withdrawn MakingCosmetics skin/heavy-cream rationale; Silicone/macadamia blends; Prewash non-weighing claims.

Limit: Producer says integrifolia and/or tetraphylla, not certified botanical hybrid; family inference only, and qualitative low viscosity conflicts with a strict boundary.

### cottonseed_hirsutum

Measured neat cottonseed flows almost identically to sunflower under the same method (41.67 vs40.96 cSt at35 C). This supports sunflower-like thin distribution on hair and a LIGHT estimate, with exact Hirsutum identity kept separate.

- [Primary source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.5402/2011/531510): Balafoutis et al. 2011 Table 3: measured neat sunflower 40.96, rapeseed 42.38, cottonseed 41.67 cSt at 35 C; seeds screw-expelled and filtered. Species and cosmetic grade unspecified. Only neat-oil viscosity used; fuel blends and engine outcomes excluded.
- [Primary source](https://raasaoils.in/products/cottonseed-oil): Exact Gossypium Hirsutum Seed Oil is described as a light refined carrier for skin and hair.

Excluded: Generic supplier light skin carrier copy; Engine/blended-fuel results; Silent Herbaceum alias.

Limit: Study lacks species declaration; Hirsutum transfer is family-level inference and depends on refining/winterization.

### buriti

Supplier hair-specific guidance recommends blending buriti with lighter jojoba to lighten the finish. That positive relative coating signal supports a RICH tendency for neat fruit-pulp oil at equal dose; MEDIUM is the nearest alternative, not a skin-richness conclusion.

- [Primary source](https://www.aroma-zone.com/en/page/hair-and-the-sun-5-plant-based-oils-that-really-do-protect-your-hair-from-uv-rays): Manufacturer hair-use article specifically says buriti can be used alone or mixed with a lighter oil such as jojoba for a lighter finish. Relative hair-finish guidance; its UV/antioxidant efficacy assertions are excluded.
- [Primary source](https://www.aroma-zone.com/en/product/buriti-oil): 100% virgin cold-pressed Mauritia flexuosa fruit-pulp oil; clear oily liquid may crystallize in cold. Greasy skin feel and dilution due to orange colour are not weight proof.

Excluded: Beraca rich skin finish; Skin absorption; Orange colour or low-use dilution as weight proof; UV protection and antioxidant claims.

Limit: Uncontrolled supplier advice and grade variation; comparison establishes heavier than jojoba, not a validated RICH cutoff.

### meadowfoam

Manufacturer viscosity of49.6 cSt at40 C supports a more persistent lubricant film than lower-viscosity seed anchors at the same temperature. MEDIUM is a positive material inference; replacing current LIGHT based only on skin slip. Silky feel can coexist with residual coating.

- [Primary source](https://meadowfoam.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/SDS-GHS-Format-MSO-ENGLISH-v1.5-2022.02.01.pdf): Manufacturer NPP SDS: meadowfoam seed oil kinematic viscosity 49.6 cSt at 40 C. This is higher than the cited blackcurrant sample and many ordinary seed-oil values at 40 C; no direct hair heaviness endpoint.
- [Primary source](https://meadowfoam.com/wp-content/uploads/MSO_assessment_hair_effect_LTR_ENG_03282022.pdf): 15-person half-head comparison, 5% oil versus 5% 1000cSt dimethicone conditioner; shine/combing/manageability outcomes.

Excluded: Non-greasy skin descriptor; 5% rinse-out conditioner versus silicone equivalence; Shine/combing as weight.

Limit: No neat equal-dose hair sensory test; LIGHT remains plausible for well-distributed modest application.

### rosehip_canina_fruit

Rubiginosa (Rosehip) Seed Oil declarations. Supports supplied-grade/family inference; no universal species or part alias.

Confirms this exact declaration can refer to seed-derived oil, without making all fruit and seed declarations equivalent.

## Review notes

- Macadamia ternifolia LIGHT was selected on skin feel. MEDIUM is now proposed consistently with integrifolia and hybrid using qualified physical family evidence. A grade-specific LIGHT result remains entirely credible; species names are never added as aliases.
- Buriti RICH now rests on relative hair-finish guidance. Its skin feel, colour and UV claims do not choose this class. MEDIUM is credible because no rich cutoff exists.
- Borage and evening primrose: the ACS paper contains separate neat-oil rheology. Its PVB fibres and skin tests are excluded. The cold-pressed brands and methods were verified from full PDF section2.6; borage18.7 and evening primrose54.6mPa.s are20 C values.
- Blackcurrant graph was inspected visually: approximately27cSt at40 C, not an invented precision figure or viscosity-index confusion. Extraction is supercritical CO2.
- Sunflower Hybrid uses the Heess supplied-grade bridge. It does not change approved ordinary sunflower or introduce a universal INCI alias.
- Rosehip Fruit Oil sometimes labels seed-pressed grades. The producer-level bridge supports weak flow inference; generic species and plant-part distinctions remain intact.
