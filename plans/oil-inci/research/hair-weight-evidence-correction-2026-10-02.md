# Hair-weight evidence correction — OE21

## Correction

The latest recommendation for macadamia hybrid relied on skin feel and heavy-cream suitability. That evidence does not establish heaviness on hair. The proposal is withdrawn and stored v0.5 remains unknown. The broader 40-light count reflects provisional, mostly low-confidence priors and requires review of applicability to hair. No class quota, blanket medium fallback or silent override of approved tendencies is introduced.

## Evidence target

Relative burden on hair after comparable modest leave-on application: perceived oiliness/heaviness, residual coating, limpness/volume reduction and suitable direct sensory observations. Comparative hair testing is preferred; credible material-specific descriptions of hair behavior may inform weak estimates. Clinical studies are not a prerequisite for the already authorized rough tendencies. Formula, applied dose, hair substrate, timing and whether oil is washed out must be retained where known. A finished blend's lightweight claim cannot establish its named plant oil's class.

Skin absorption/cream suitability cannot select a hair-weight class alone. Hair penetration, protein loss, shine, detangling and tensile strength are separate endpoints. Physical/composition information supplies context only unless its connection to hair weight is explicitly reasoned and supported.
