# Hair-weight evidence lane: mid-list oils

Scope: equal, modest, neat leave-on dose. This is a collected provisional list for the oil-INCI ledger, not a validated rank or a product-level conclusion. No source supplied a controlled comparison of neat oils on hair volume, greasy residue, or heaviness.

Physical inference is deliberately narrow: an author article explains that viscosity can affect spreading/heavy feel and that hair lubrication depends on dose, surface coverage, and spreading ([Cosmetics & Toiletries](https://www.cosmeticsandtoiletries.com/magazine/article/22862759/cosmetics-toiletries-magazine-choosing-the-right-plant-oil-for-hair-and-skin-benefits)). It does not validate a class; similar long-chain triglyceride oils cannot be decisively sorted from fatty-acid composition alone.
