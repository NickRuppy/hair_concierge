[Nikko's supplier hair-oil example](https://in.nikkolgroup.com/nikko-wp/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Hair_Cuticle_Repair_Oil.pdf) links the exact Hydrogenated Farnesene name to hemisqualane HF and carrier/emollient use. [Givaudan's material-family page](https://uxbeauty.givaudan.com/concepts/permalink/givaudan-neossance-hemisqualane) describes spreading properties and multiple grades. Those are functional clues, not evidence of JVN's exact grade, concentration or washout. No example percentages are transferred.

## Why unknown is defensible

[Jojoba Esters liquid grade 15](https://www.cargill.com/doc/1432223227839/beauty-floraesters-15-product-data-sheet.pdf) and [hard-wax grade 70](https://www.cargill.com/doc/1432224610669/beauty-floraesters-70-product-data-sheet.pdf) share the exact name. JVN's rank 3 grade is undisclosed. A dissolved wax can still occur in a liquid oil. Supplier skin-feel/hydration and lipstick tests do not rank residue on hair, and whole-jojoba's accepted prior does not apply to these derivatives.

[Elementis Meadowfoam Estolide](https://www.elementis.com/products/meadowestolide/) is a separate conditioning derivative. Its cached supplier TDS describes liquid appearance at 35°C, not guaranteed room-temperature fluidity or low build-up. Its hair-friction claims do not measure repeated residual accumulation. Squalane and botanical oils also remain after application. Neither missing gum nor a light feel alone proves low.

The fresh check therefore supports a practical low **candidate**, while leaving the exact grade uncertainty unresolved. It does not turn either derivative into a generic low-build-up material. The conservative alternative is to retain unknown; elevated lacks affirmative evidence that this formula has a substantial structured coating.
