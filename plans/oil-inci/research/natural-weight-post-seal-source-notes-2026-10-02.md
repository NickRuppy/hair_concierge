# Additional weak source observation after the v0.2 seal

Main's focused source check found [Raasa's cosmetic sunflower carrier page](https://raasaoils.in/products/sunflower-seed-oil), describing a lighter feel in skin/hair applications. The page ties the carrier to Helianthus annuus seeds but does not disclose a comparative method, commercial INCI-grade specification or uncertainty. This is weaker descriptive supplier evidence and supports the already-retained **light alternative** to v0.2's low-confidence medium sunflower prior; it does not validate either class.

This new observation was not a blind-run input and did not change sealed material priors or lane outputs. No selected single-material case was sunflower. The operative reference's particularly weak olive/sunflower/black-seed priors remain explicitly provisional; new grade/comparative evidence should create a separate reference revision and re-derivation, rather than silently fit a class to a vendor claim.
