## Why this gap exists

The finite coverage audit encountered literal `COCOS NUCIFERA FRUIT OIL` in the Biopark Organic Coconut Oil capture. It kept the declaration outside generic matching rather than silently converting it to `COCOS NUCIFERA OIL`. An Eden Botanicals coconut-pulp CO2 SDS uses Fruit Oil as a common chemical name while declaring Fruit Extract as its INCI. That source does not establish a generic Oil/Extract alias, nor does it resolve Biopark's separately documented pressed-oil product.

Biopark was already rich in the first product round under its frozen method. This follow-up concerns coverage of the exact ingredient declaration in future material lookups; it is not a new first-round result or sensory measurement.

## Primary identity observations checked 2026-10-03

- [Biopark Cosmetics Organic Coconut Oil 100 ml](https://biopark-cosmetics.com/products/organic-coconut-oil-100ml) declares Cocos Nucifera Fruit Oil as its sole oil composition. The maker describes cold-pressed, unrefined coconut oil, semisolid when cool and liquid when warmed, and provides hair-use directions. These facts support a source-scoped ordinary coconut fixed-oil interpretation. Its skin absorption, repair, mineral and scalp/dandruff claims do not determine weight or efficacy.
- [6biologico REMEDY Coconut Oil](https://www.6biologico.com/prodotto/809/REMEDY/COCONUT-OIL.html), the maker's own product page, describes pure cold-pressed coconut oil and lists Cocos nucifera fruit oil as the INCI. This independently corroborates commercial use of the exact declaration for pressed coconut fixed oil. Neither source supplies a controlled hair-heaviness comparison or establishes the behavior of every supplier grade.

Preserve `COCOS NUCIFERA OIL` as its own identity and all earlier reference bytes. The extra specificity in the literal name is retained rather than erased by a normalization rule.

The affirmative identity evidence is stronger than the earlier general SDS caution: two own-maker product declarations pair this exact Oil name with pure pressed coconut, and Biopark supplies grade/physical-form context. The rich tendency still comes from the existing approximate coconut hair-weight reference and supplied practical sheet, not from skin absorption, semisolid state alone or these makers' efficacy claims.
