## Status vocabulary

- **Code readiness** means an exact category adapter is implemented and has fail-closed `known`, `pending`, `unknown`, and `unsupported` outcomes; it does not make a customer-facing assertion when facts are missing.
- **Data readiness** means the active recommended cohort, required category specs, and exact Stage-5 application protocol/guidance evidence agree for the category.
- **Known / pending / unknown / unsupported behavior:** adapters preserve known fit or mismatch only when their loaded facts support it; pending intake stays pending; missing facts stay unknown and non-executable; an unsupported category stays an honest gap, never a user-specific no-match or silent fallback.
- Every category is safe as a Stage-1 need card. None is currently launchable as a complete production Stage 1–5 category.
