Key whole-formula calls:

- SLES alone did not create a `strong` label. Four products remain `moderate` because amphoteric/nonionic buffering and care routes materially change the net chassis. H&S is `strong` because a second anionic route remains after helper ingredients are excluded.
- Syoss reaches `high` weight from early silicone + Amodimethicone + cationic guar + waxy/lipid routes; the other four retain enough reset or lack that persistent stack and remain `moderate`.
- Hydration has no direct v1.4 focus enum. Elvital therefore remains `general`, with `shine` only as a separately claimed and formula-supported secondary.
- Piroctone Olamine makes H&S dandruff support `supported`; no other pilot formula contains Piroctone Olamine or Climbazole.
