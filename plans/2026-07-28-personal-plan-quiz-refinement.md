## Chosen direction so far

### Pre-payment diagnostic contract

The new funnel collects these diagnostic inputs before showing the result:

1. hair texture / pattern;
2. strand thickness;
3. hair density;
4. hair length;
5. surface / finger test;
6. elasticity / pull test;
7. chemical treatment;
8. scalp type;
9. conditional scalp complaint;
10. reframed current problems;
11. unrestricted goals.

Density remains in the quiz even though it costs one additional screen. Avoiding that screen does not justify changing intake routing, resume behavior, legacy density backfills, and the otherwise established paid onboarding.

### Paid refinement contract

After payment, the current onboarding continues unchanged to collect products, product frequency, heat behavior, drying, brushing, and night habits. This refinement does not add, remove, reorder, or restyle paid-onboarding screens.

### Quiz persistence and compatibility contract

- Continue using `public.leads`; each completed quiz submission owns a separate lead row.
- Add one queryable `quiz_kind` discriminator with initial values `legacy` and `personal_plan`.
- Continue using `leads.quiz_answers` JSONB. Personal-plan submissions store a `schema_version` and a structured answer payload rather than adding one SQL column per question.
- Do not add a separate submissions table unless retakes, comparison, or multiple assessments per lead become a confirmed product requirement.
- Persist diagnostic answers, the complete current-problem and goal arrays, routine/result context, previous attempts, blockers, plan style, and meaningful moment.
- Do not persist the four problem-admission confirmations (`recurrence`, `conflicting needs`, `practical cost`, `emotional relevance`), the separate daily-time conversion commitment, or the three loading commitments. They may affect only the immediate in-session presentation.
- Do not map persuasion or commitment answers into `hair_profiles`.
- Do not collect a name in the personal-plan acquisition funnel. Email is the only required contact field; customer-facing result and service-email copy must work without first-name personalization.
- While the offer remains a placeholder, legacy result, result-email, profile-linking, analytics, and email-fallback consumers must explicitly reject or exclude `personal_plan` leads. A full personal-plan-to-canonical adapter is deferred until the offer/payment flow can actually reach profile linking.
- Deduplication is scoped by `quiz_kind`; within the current 15-minute window, personal-plan deduplication reuses only a lead with the same normalized email and complete normalized persisted V2 payload, and never reuses a legacy lead. Changed answers or a later retake create a new lead.
- Funnel and campaign attribution remains in `funnel_sessions`, not inside quiz answers.

### Conversion psychology retained before payment

- early proof and social proof;
- recognition of uncertainty and inconsistent results;
- encouraging transitions and non-blaming reframe;
- visible product education;
- aspiration and meaningful-moment framing;
- unrestricted goal selection;
- three micro-commitments;
- three preparation stages;
- testimonials during preparation;
- email completion assistance;
- optional marketing consent in a second state of the same email-capture shell.

### Locked section spine

1. **Hair profile opening:** promise, texture, thickness, and density.
2. **Early proof:** first-party survey proof and an approved testimonial.
3. **Goals:** unrestricted, texture-adapted goal selection.
4. **Current situation:** routine structure, result reliability, adaptation confidence, and texture-adapted current problems.
5. **Analysis bridge:** a dedicated reassuring transition explaining why the factual observations are needed.
6. **Factual analysis Part A:** length, surface, and elasticity.
7. **Midpoint recognition:** a short automatically advancing animation showing that the profile is taking shape.
8. **Factual analysis Part B:** chemical treatment, scalp type, and conditional scalp complaint.
9. **Problem admission:** a personalized recognition followed by four visual confirmation questions covering recurrence, conflicting needs, practical cost, and emotional relevance.
10. **Positive reframe:** the hair is not the problem; the missing element is a suitable coordinated plan.
11. **Previous attempts and blockers:** establish why earlier efforts did not become a reliable system.
12. **Desired future:** plan feeling and meaningful moment.
13. **Positive profile receipt:** a compact `VIEL POTENZIAL` summary reflects recognizable diagnostic and preference answers without revealing the paid routine or products.
14. **Daily-time commitment:** a separate full-screen conversion question asks how much time the user wants to take for their hair on a normal day; the answer does not alter the plan and is not persisted.
15. **Plan preparation:** three personalized processing stages, three commitments, and one testimonial card per stage. The reviewed V1 copy is placeholder content and must be replaced with attributable customer proof before production launch.
16. **Result delivery:** an email-entry state with provider completion suggestions is followed by the current Chaarlie-style same-shell consent card with explicit `Ja` and `Nein` actions. Either action saves the V2 lead and continues to the placeholder offer route; no name or second opt-out confirmation is requested.

Texture is collected on the first screen and is then reused throughout the funnel. Later question wording, applicable examples, option ordering, silhouettes, profile visuals, and loading states should acknowledge the selected texture wherever it improves recognition. This personalization must remain grounded in the selected and subsequently collected answers; it must not invent appearance, condition, or need.

### Locked preparation-stage testimonial placeholders

The three cards follow the psychological sequence `Verstehen → Personalisieren → Umsetzen`. They are prototype fixtures, not attributable customer statements:

1. **Understanding:** `Ich hatte schon so viele Tipps ausprobiert. Durch Chaarlie habe ich zum ersten Mal verstanden, was meine Haare wirklich brauchen.`
2. **Personalization:** `Mein Haarpflegeplan fühlt sich wirklich auf meine Haare abgestimmt an – nicht wie eine allgemeine Routine, die für alle gleich ist.`
3. **Clarity and ease:** `Endlich weiß ich, welche Schritte für meine Haare wichtig sind und in welcher Reihenfolge. Das macht meine Haarpflege so viel einfacher.`

The prototype may use these cards to validate hierarchy, pacing, and transitions. Production must not present them as real Chaarlie customer quotes until each statement has an attributable source and explicit approval. Replacement proof should preserve the three distinct jobs above rather than reverting to Chat-centric or generic product-recommendation testimonials.

### Locked current-problem taxonomy

The current-problems page shows eight meaningful cards with unrestricted multi-selection. Six are shared across all textures:

1. `Trockene oder raue Längen`
2. `Frizz oder viele abstehende Haare`
3. `Wenig Glanz`
4. `Haarbruch oder Spliss`
5. `Schnelles Verknoten`
6. `Meine Kopfhaut gerät schnell aus dem Gleichgewicht`

The final two cards adapt to texture:

| Texture  | Form or definition                                  | Volume or weight                                               |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Straight | `Mein Haar verliert schnell Form und Halt`          | `Mein Haar wirkt schnell platt oder beschwert`                 |
| Wavy     | `Meine Wellen verlieren schnell ihre Form`          | `Mein Ansatz wirkt flach oder meine Längen schnell beschwert`  |
| Curly    | `Meine Locken verlieren schnell ihre Definition`    | `Form oder Volumen wirken schnell ungleichmäßig`               |
| Coily    | `Meine Definition hält nicht so, wie ich es möchte` | `Form oder Volumen verteilen sich nicht so, wie ich es möchte` |

`Nichts davon` is a separate mutually exclusive action beneath the cards rather than one of the eight meaningful options.

### Locked goal taxonomy

The goal page shows eight texture-adapted domains with unrestricted multi-selection and no primary-goal follow-up. Healthy, beautiful hair remains the overarching promise rather than one selectable card.

| Domain             | Straight                                         | Wavy                           | Curly                              | Coily                                           |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Moisture           | `Feuchtigkeit ohne Beschweren`                   | `Feuchtigkeit ohne Beschweren` | `Intensive Feuchtigkeit`           | `Feuchtigkeit länger bewahren`                  |
| Frizz or surface   | `Mehr Geschmeidigkeit, weniger abstehende Haare` | `Weniger Frizz`                | `Weniger Frizz`                    | `Mehr Geschmeidigkeit, weniger Frizz`           |
| Shine              | `Mehr Glanz`                                     | `Mehr Glanz`                   | `Mehr Glanz`                       | `Mehr Glanz`                                    |
| Strength           | `Weniger Haarbruch und Spliss`                   | `Weniger Haarbruch und Spliss` | `Weniger Haarbruch und Spliss`     | `Weniger Haarbruch und bessere Längenretention` |
| Scalp              | `Ausgeglichene Kopfhaut`                         | `Ausgeglichene Kopfhaut`       | `Ausgeglichene Kopfhaut`           | `Ausgeglichene Kopfhaut`                        |
| Manageability      | `Leichteres Styling`                             | `Leichteres Styling`           | `Leichteres Entwirren und Styling` | `Leichteres Entwirren und Styling`              |
| Form or definition | `Mehr Form und Halt`                             | `Mehr Wellen-Definition`       | `Mehr Locken-Definition`           | `Mehr Definition`                               |

Priority is derived deterministically from safety and scalp signals, damage and treatment, texture, current problems, and selected goals. The interface does not ask the user to identify a single highest-priority goal.

## Scope and non-goals

### In scope

- Reorder, combine, remove, or restyle screens in the new funnel.
- Replace temporary V1 copy and visual placeholders.
- Preserve enough diagnostic fidelity for the future result/offer adapter without implementing that adapter in this refinement.
- Define V2 persistence, result handoff, draft, consent, and analytics contracts.
- Preserve medically responsible boundaries without turning the conversion result into an alarming medical interstitial.

### Out of scope

- A new offer-page information architecture or pricing model.
- The exact layout and content of the V2 transactional result email; that email is designed alongside the stable combined result/offer destination.
- A complete rewrite of onboarding.
- Changes to the current live `/quiz` journey.
- Production activation, deployment, or experiment rollout.

## Designed user journey

Status: approved by Nick on 2026-07-28.

Current direction:

1. A paid-ad visitor lands directly on the texture image-card question.
2. One selection auto-advances into the first diagnostic section.
3. The funnel alternates effortful diagnostic inputs with proof, recognition, reassurance, and aspiration rather than presenting one long administrative block.
4. Problems and goals visibly affect the eventual diagnosis and remain broadly selectable.
5. A positive reframe explains that the user’s hair is not the problem; the missing piece is a plan adapted to it.
6. The user receives a positive `VIEL POTENZIAL` profile receipt based on recognizable answers.
7. A separate daily-time conversion commitment follows; its answer changes neither plan computation nor persistence.
8. The funnel prepares the result through three stages with three commitments and background testimonials.
9. The user enters only an email, helped by provider completion suggestions. `Weiter` replaces the form within the same shell with the existing Chaarlie-style consent card. `Ja, weiter zu meiner Auswertung` and `Nein, nur meine Auswertung schicken` both save and continue; no name or follow-up rejection screen appears.
10. The V2 lead is saved and the user reaches the placeholder offer route owned by the separate offer-page workstream.
11. After the future offer and payment, the existing onboarding gathers current products and routine details without any flow changes from this refinement.
12. Completion leads to the paid personal plan/routine destination defined by the separate post-payment plan.

Error, recovery, and compatibility requirements:

- Back navigation restores prior selections.
- A stored draft resumes at the correct semantic screen.
- Invalid or stale draft values fail safely.
- Density is collected and restored inside the quiz before the saved result is generated.
- Safety-relevant scalp answers receive responsible boundaries without replacing the conversion result with an alarm state.
