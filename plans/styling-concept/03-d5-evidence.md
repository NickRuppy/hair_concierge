Collected 2026-10-09 from two read-only lanes:
- an external evidence lane (hair-care-expert);
- a scan of Chaarlie's own knowledge base: Tom's book chapters, course transcripts and live calls (`data/markdown-cleaned/`, gitignored, root checkout only), plus the repo's agent guidance.

Ruling: D5 option A (see `02-rulings.md`).

Evidence tags:
- **[A]** measured data
- **[B]** convergent practitioner or brand convention
- **[C]** mechanism inference
- **[D]** none found, or myth

## Measured data (thin; no product-count study exists)

| Finding | Tag | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 87 % of German styling users deliberately keep styling simple; many spend < 5 min/day. | [A] | Mintel DE Haarstyling 2025, n = 2,000 |
| The "repertoire" (number of products) figures are behind Mintel's paywall. Men and textured-hair users use more products. | [A] | Mintel DE Haarstyling 2025 |
| About 15 % of Germans 14+ used gel or other non-spray styling products in the last 7 days (11.09 M). | [A] | AWA 2025 via Statista |
| Hairspray is the most popular styling product in Germany. | [A] | Mintel |
| 26–33 % of curly/wavy women use no regular hair-care or styling product. Mousse 13–17 %, flexible hairspray 11–16 %. | [A] | YouGov US/GB 2022 |
| 46 % of German women wash their hair only 1–2× per week; 9 % wash daily. | [A] | YouGov DE 2025, n = 2,027 |

## Practice

| Finding | Tag | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The salon/pro blow-dry sequence is prep (Föhnlotion / Schaumfestiger + heat protection) → blow-dry → hairspray: about 2 products. A sleek look adds an anti-frizz or shine spray. | [B] | Wella tutorials; friseur.com and other German media; Schwarzkopf Session Label Prep / Style / Finish |
| For straight/wavy hair, use one hold product only: „es macht keinen Sinn, mehrere Haltprodukte miteinander zu kombinieren". Hairspray follows as a separate final step. | [B] | Tom, book ch. 14 |
| Curls: one-styler camp (leave-in → gel). | [B] | Curlsmith 4-step; Ouidad; Tom's baseline, book ch. 12 |
| Curls: cream → gel camp. | [B] | Curlsmith layering guide; German curl guides; Tom permits it for thick or dry curls; repo `curl-definition/assembly-rules.md:36` permits it for coarse/coily hair |
| Day-2 refresh: water + the same gel. A separate refresh spray is not part of it. | [B] | Tom course module „Lockenrefresh"; Curlsmith (water + one light mist) |
| No data on how many people refresh. | [D] | — |

## Avoid

| Finding | Tag | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Never use two products of the same type (two gels). | [B]/[C] | Practitioner convention; polymer-compatibility science is plausible but there is no on-hair study |
| Don't apply a heavy cream over gel. | [B] | Curlsmith and others |
| Fine hair: many layers weigh it down. This is mostly a dose effect. | [B] | — |
| "Alcohol hairspray ruins curls" | [D] | No controlled study found |
