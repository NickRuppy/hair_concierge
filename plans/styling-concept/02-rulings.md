# Styling concept: owner rulings ledger

These are Nick's rulings, given in this session on 2026-10-09. Where this ledger and `01-styling-concept.md` (Rev. 1) differ, **the ledger governs**. Rev. 2 of the concept will fold these in.

## First round (before the concept)

| ID | Ruling |
|---|---|
| SD1 | Scope: every styling family, plus the overlap types at the edges of Leave-in, Heat Protectant, Oil and Dry Shampoo. |
| SD2 | One category key `styling` with subtypes. |
| SD3 | Shine and finish products with no oil go to Styling, not Oil. Narrowed by D2 below. |
| SD4 | Styling is optional by default and actively suggested when there is a matching problem or goal. Refined by D4 below. |
| SD5 | Chaarlie builds its own hold scale. |

## Concept decisions

| ID | Ruling | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Accept all three merges → 12 subtypes.** | `blowdry_lotion` absorbs Föhnlotion, Föhnspray and root lift (`target_zone: roots`). One `molding` subtype covers wax, paste, clay, fiber, pomade and men's cream, with `form` kept as a display label. Edge control becomes `target_zone: hairline` under `gel`. |
| D2 | **Retailer way: the name decides the Oil ↔ Styling boundary** for shine and anti-frizz products. | Anything sold as „Öl" or „Serum" stays in **Oil**, whatever the formula: Elvital Öl Magique, Frizz Ease, and the pure-silicone Garnier Sleek & Stay serum. Shine and finish products sold under other names go to **Styling**: Glanzspray and Brilliantine → `shine_finish`. *(Example corrected 2026-10-09: Color Wow Dream Coat is also Styling under D2, but its subtype is `smoothing_styler` by job, because it is applied damp before blow-drying.)* Oil keeps "immediate dry shine" in `oil/decision.md`, with no Oil catalog re-sort. SD3 is narrowed to products not named oil or serum. Water-based "serums" that are really leave-in conditioners still follow Leave-in G0. **Replaces the Rev. 1 lipid-vs-silicone rank test.** |
| D3 | **Keep the September rule (T15) as the Leave-in ↔ Styling border.** | A conditioning film with a supporting fixative is Leave-in; a fixative with thin conditioning is Styling. NEQI Diamond Glass stays Leave-in, Maria Nila Curlicue goes to Styling. Both engines share one border. |
| D4 | **A matching problem *or* goal makes Styling recommended (basis).** In addition, Styling is suggested as **optional based on the profile**, with a considerate note, even without a stated problem. | This replaces "goal only → optional". Hair type can now trigger an *optional* suggestion, but never a recommended one. Proposed profile triggers (Nick may still adjust): |
| | | • fine hair → volume helper: „Für Tage, an denen das Volumen nicht ganz mitspielt." |
| | | • wavy, curly or coily hair that air-dries → definition helper: „Für Tage, an denen deine Wellen/Locken etwas mehr Halt brauchen." |
| | | • short hair → molding: „Falls du deiner Frisur mal mehr Form geben willst." |
| D5 | **One product per job (option A).** | One shaping styler. **Hairspray is the default finisher for blow-dried and heat-styled looks**; for air-dried curls and short-hair molding it appears only on `lost_shape`. **Curly/coily hair with normal–coarse or dry ends may get `curl_cream` + `gel`**: two stylers with different jobs, never two of the same type. **Refresh is a day-2 instruction** reusing the existing gel („Wasser + etwas Gel“); a `refresher` product is an optional extra only. **Ceiling: 3 styling products.** Leave-in and heat protection do not count (separate categories). Evidence: `03-d5-evidence.md`. |
| D6 | **Sprays with neither real care nor real hold that promise a styling look → Styling** (refresh mists, beach sprays). | This adds one exception to Leave-in G0. T15 is untouched. *Clarification (wording only, 2026-10-09):* "real hold" means a fixative-class polymer (Leave-in L5 / HOLD sense). Salt, starch and particle mechanisms do not count, so beach sprays and starch-only curl sprays qualify, as intended. Products with real conditioning stay Leave-in: Bali Hydrating Curl Cream, Balea Anti-Frizz Serum, Redken One United, EVO. |
| D7 | **Curl-cream middle band: the name decides.** T15 stays for clear cases. When a product has **both** a fixative-class polymer above the tail **and** a substantive conditioning system, being sold as a curl/styling cream or balm („Lockencreme“, „Curl Cream“, „Styling Cream/Balm … Locken“) routes it to **Styling** `curl_cream`. | Scope is the middle band only. Curl creams **without** a real fixative stay Leave-in per T15, even when named „Lockencreme“ (Cantu Kokos, HASK, Bali Hydrating). Moves to Styling: Taft Styling Balm Locken, Balea Pro Styling Cream Traumlocken, John Frieda Lockencreme Traumlocken. NEQI stays Leave-in (not a curl cream, care film + small fixative). |
| D8 | **Hold is used only where brands state it; Chaarlie never guesses and never excludes (option A, "for now").** | Texture, salt, powder, shine, smoothing and refresh products: hold plays no part in selection (finish, weight and application decide). Gel, curl cream, mousse, Föhnlotion, hairspray and molding: a stated, fitting level ranks first; no level = still eligible, ranked after, shown as „Halt: keine Herstellerangabe“. Gaps are filled over time from pack photos (intake/scanner). Nick asked for parallel research on community experience and ingredient-based estimation (`round2/hold-research-community.md`, `round2/hold-research-ingredients.md`) and may revisit. |
| D8b | **After the hold research: keep A, plus an internal plausibility flag and a neutral ingredient fact; user hold ratings later.** | Flag (internal, never changes a level): a strength claim of ≥ stark, or any hold claim on shine/smoothing/refresh products, with no hold mechanism above the tail → review. Today this catches Cantu "Extra Hold". Same brand + identical INCI + different level → verify against the pack. Product card: when no level is stated, show the neutral fact „Enthält Stylingpolymere (z. B. PVP)“ next to „Halt: keine Herstellerangabe“. This is never a rating. Later, separate feature idea: in-app user hold ratings (Halt · wie lange · Knusper/Beweglichkeit · bei Feuchtigkeit), grouped by hair profile, shown only above a minimum n. Evidence: `round2/hold-research-community.md` (Stiftung Warentest: „Jeder Anbieter erfindet eigene Skalen“; no app has crowd hold ratings) and `round2/hold-research-ingredients.md` (best formula rule 3/6 precise). |

## Phase 1 research-engine rulings (2026-10-09)

Source: the charter (`docs/research/styling-inci/v1.0/00_category_charter.md`, proposals P1–P4), the property set (`01_property-set-v0.md`, PS1–PS6) and `parked-border-questions.md` (Q2). Nick approved the bundle "for now", except the two items marked open.

| ID | Ruling |
|---|---|
| PH1-1 (PS1) | **Tail cutoff (trace line):** the first preservative **or** perfume, whichever comes first, for Styling's own properties (Mask rule). The Leave-in G0 border check keeps its own rule. |
| PH1-2 (PS3) | **Weight:** judged from the ingredients the way Leave-in does it, never from product form. |
| PH1-3 (PS4) | **Alcohol: reuse the Leave-in alcohol note** („Enthält Alkohol – bei empfindlicher Kopfhaut lieber nur in die Längen geben.“) with a clear trigger: Alcohol / Alcohol Denat. / SD Alcohol / Ethanol / **Isopropyl Alcohol** among the first 3 ingredients. Fatty alcohols and benzyl alcohol never count. It is a note only, never an exclusion. *(Ruled 2026-10-09 after the buildup research.)* |
| PH1-4 (PS5) | **No new "hard to wash" field. Reuse what exists.** (1) Styling `weight` uses the shared production scale **light · medium · rich** (CANONICAL_WEIGHTS; research `low/moderate/high`) and the Leave-in WT rule. (2) The Leave-in **buildup note** (§8.5 Hinweise, copy „Bleibt lange im Haar …“) is reused unchanged as a note, not a score. (3) **Deferred to the Personal-Plan decision:** whether a rich Styling product used weekly adds +1 to the Deep-Cleansing reset-load (today Styling is not an input). *(Ruled 2026-10-09; evidence: buildup survey of Leave-in/Mask/Conditioner/Shampoo/Deep-Cleansing.)* |
| PH1-5 (PS6) | **Word rules:** „ohne zu verkleben“ is not a flexibility word; „Crunch“ = a cast to knead out; „natürlicher Glanz“ = natürlich finish; brand flex/shine numbers are recorded and the words decide. |
| PH1-6 (PS2 part) | **PQ-11/-16/-46 alone** (no other fixative-class polymer) → hold_route review, not real hold. |
| PH1-7 (P2) | **One product = one route**; no split by use (e.g. Balea MEN Pomade Haar & Bart = Styling `molding`, beard use only recorded). |
| PH1-8 (P3) | Leave-in identity, claim-authority (rules 1–7) and same-market INCI-conflict (T18) rules are reused unchanged. **A pack-vs-manufacturer-page hold conflict → „keine Herstellerangabe“.** |
| PH1-9 (P4) | **„Enthält Stylingpolymere“** shows only for a fixative-class polymer above the tail; starch, wax, salt and particles don't trigger it. |
| PH1-10 (Q2) | **Oils count as conditioning** for D6, as in Leave-in. Balea Definier Spray stays Leave-in; its "24 h" claim is flagged. |
| PH1-11 (P1) | **v1.0 is finished when:** every subtype has ≥ 5 products; two sealed blind lanes agree on all 90 routes and properties (no unresolved disagreements); two independent claim normalisations give identical hold levels; ≥ 6 unseen products (≥ 2 traps) match pre-registered expectations; and a Leave-in amendment for D6/D7 exists. |
| PH1-12 | **D7 runs before the S0 "mixed promise" stop, for the curl-cream middle band only.** If the middle-band test fires (fixative-class polymer above the tail + substantive conditioning + sold as a curl/styling cream or balm), the product is Styling `curl_cream` even when it also promises care or heat protection (e.g. Balea Pro Styling Cream Traumlocken). The mixed-promise stop still applies to every other product. |
| PH1-13 (Q1) | **„Effective amount“ rule (R2, class-aware dose test) for the D6 border only.** Oils, esters, plain silicones and fatty alcohols count as care only **above the first preservative**. Cationic conditioners, cationic polymers and amino silicones may sit below it, but not below the first pH adjuster listed after it (fallback: Parfum; no yardstick → review). Humectants, panthenol, proteins, extracts, starch and volatile carriers never count. Anything decided below the preservative carries a review flag. "Real care" = any care, not COND ≥ moderate (a detangling spray counts). It is not to be used as the T15 "thin" test. Outcomes: alverde Locken Refresh → Styling `refresher` (review flag); Syoss Curl Control → Leave-in (still blocked on DE identity); no ruled/reference outcome flips (40 checked). The preservative-count rule (option C) is rejected. Evidence: `concept-v0/round2/care-vs-trace-rule.md`. |
| PH1-14 | **Elnett Stylingspray „3 Tage Glatt“ → Styling `smoothing_styler`.** The pack front leads with the styling result („Hitze Styling-Spray, glatt 3 Tage, starker Halt“). Its pack „Starker Halt“ = level 3, carrying the D8b plausibility flag (no fixative). |
| PH1-15 | **New property: heat protection for Styling products, using the same rule as Leave-in** (`provides_heat_protection`, binary). It is true only with a C1/C2 heat claim **and** a pass on the Leave-in heat check (HEAT §7.8/§13: closed L9 list above the tail). Only then may a Styling product fill the Personal-Plan heat-protection step (e.g. a Föhnspray covering both jobs). Otherwise the claim is shown („Hitzeschutz laut Hersteller“) but never fills the role. **This amends `heat-protectant/decision.md`** (Styling was excluded from the heat role in V1) and supersedes the Rev. 2 "Styling never fills the heat role" line. About 18 of 70 Styling products mention heat protection in our data, some only at C3. |
| PH1-16 | **Leave-in amendment waits until Styling locks (option B).** The locked Leave-in standard stays unchanged for now. D6, D7 and the four missing vinyl fixative families (e.g. VA/Crotonates in Elnett) live in the Styling standard's own fixative list and border rules. Right after the Styling v1.0 lock, a Leave-in addendum (the T20-overlay pattern) is written; every already-ruled Leave-in product that would move goes to Nick first. **This adjusts PH1-11:** the lock criterion is now "Leave-in addendum drafted and queued for right after lock", not "addendum exists". Until then, a product both engines could claim is held as `provisional_boundary` on the Styling side. |

| ID | Ruling |
|---|---|
| PH2-1 (P-S1) | **The Leave-in border check applies only where the promise overlaps Leave-in's own definition** (care, detangling, smoothing/anti-frizz, curl support, blow-dry/heat support; LI §2.1). | PH2-3 (P-S3) | **D6 applies to any format**, not only sprays (format never routes). E.g. Balea Locken Crunchgel → Styling. |

## Phase 3 round-1 calibration rulings (2026-10-12)

| ID | Ruling |
|---|---|

| R1-Q2 | **Anti-frizz / flyaway control on dry, finished hair is part of the shine-finish job** (Styling `shine_finish`, `li_function` no, recommendable now). The product's **main** directions decide („nach dem Styling … auf das trockene Haar"); a side tip (e.g. „bei krausem Haar vorher ins handtuchtrockene Haar") does not. Anti-frizz promised for damp hair, or sold as care, keeps the Leave-in border check. This closes the gap between D2 / the `shine_finish` definition („shine, flyaway control on dry hair") and the PH2-1 Leave-in job list. Outcomes: Balea Glow & Shine Finishing Spray and Feschi Smooth & Shine → `shine_finish`, not held; Balea Finishing Stick still stops at the evidence gate (no directions).
