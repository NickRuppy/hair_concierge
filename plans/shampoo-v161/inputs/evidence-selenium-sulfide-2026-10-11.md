# Evidence: selenium sulfide vs piroctone olamine vs climbazole (anti-dandruff), 2026-10-11

Research agent (hair-care-expert, Sonnet), read-only; orchestrator spot-verified 21 CFR 358.710(a) (eCFR API) and Danby 1993 (Europe PMC).

Bottom line: SeS2 ≤1% is at least as well supported as PO and climbazole and stronger on regulatory status. Grades: SeS2 ≤1% moderate; PO limited–moderate; climbazole limited. No placebo-controlled monotherapy RCT at ≤1% for any of the three; ≤1% SeS2 data mostly Vichy/L'Oréal-funded; PO mostly P&G/Beiersdorf.
