## Boundary evidence

- src/lib/personal-plan/products/production-persistence-gateway.ts owns current-draft loading, owner/refined-source checks, mutation dispatch, CAS and persistence; route callers instantiate it at src/app/api/personal-plan/stage-3/route.ts:571 and complete/route.ts:214.
- The authority suite owns deterministic authority/category/candidate decisions. API mocks cannot establish those concrete calculations, and gateway fixtures do not substitute for production authority.
- tests/personal-plan-api-stage3.test.ts owns the real route handler contract: request parsing, server owner derivation, rate limits, typed status mapping and prohibited client payloads. Its injected gateway deliberately cannot prove persistence/CAS.
- tests/personal-plan-stage3-gateway.test.ts owns the client transport parser and fixture lifecycle: stale/conflict/retry behavior, request shapes, recovery and idempotency. Route tests do not exercise those response-decoding/client transitions.

CI discovers root Node tests through package.json test:node; nested personal-plan tests have their own recursive runner. History/previous Stage3 cutover evidence shows gateway role replacement already has its former pure state-machine duplicate transferred into the production-gateway keeper; that transfer is retained here and not counted again.
