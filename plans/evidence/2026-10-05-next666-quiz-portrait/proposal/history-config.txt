feat(offer): introduce guided-story result journey (#238)

## Why

The previous offer page did not tell a sufficiently personal,
concern-led story from quiz result to routine and Chaarlie support. This
draft publishes the reviewed guided-story direction so the founders can
evaluate the complete experience in a hosted preview before merge.

## What changed

- adds a hard-gated four-chapter quiz-result journey
- derives three concern-led analysis priorities and a personalized hair
portrait
- selects one approved generated portrait from the user's hair texture
and length, with simple perm/straightening treatment mapping and a
generic fallback
- preserves three accessible analysis markers and adds a protected
21-state portrait review gallery
- builds a shampoo/conditioner foundation plus an eligible targeted
third step
- demonstrates Chaarlie through a precomputed chat exchange and
comparable tracker preview
- restyles customer proof as one centered editorial section without
decorative quote icons
- preserves the existing pricing and checkout boundary while warming
Stripe before the final gate
- adds stable guided-story analytics, focus routing, and incumbent
rollback registration
- switches active organic and Meta packages to the guided-story variant
in this branch
- exposes `/labs/offer-page?variant=guided-story` and `/labs/portrait`
in local development and Vercel Preview while keeping production access
blocked
