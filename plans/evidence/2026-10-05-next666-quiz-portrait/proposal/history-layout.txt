Add restrained motion to the personal-plan quiz and offer (#280)

## Problem

The personal-plan quiz and offer changed state abruptly on mobile, with
limited response feedback and no coordinated motion across pricing or
FAQ interactions.

## Behavior changes

- Adds direction-aware, inert two-layer quiz transitions plus option,
milestone, analysis, and profile-summary feedback.
- Adds restrained membership selector motion and a fixed-size sticky CTA
that preserves pricing-navigation versus checkout-intent semantics
across membership and one-time arms.
- Animates the existing native FAQ disclosure while retaining keyboard
access, open/toggle state, analytics, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Preserves the approved plan and mockups with the implementation.
- Keeps Slice B checkout-dismissal behavior out of this PR.
