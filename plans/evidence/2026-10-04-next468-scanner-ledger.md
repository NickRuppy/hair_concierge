## Rejected shortcuts and limits

No wholesale reducer retirement: component filters stale saves/resolves before dispatch, so UI can pass with missing reducer guards. Preserve same-product reveal-token race, request-kind collisions, cancellation interleaving, exact persisted budget identity, omitted searchReason default, and repeat/reset state. HTTP200 unavailable and429 enter different search branches; neither retires the other. Both catalog and retailer identity branches stay. Source-port binding tests retain actual delivered port/flag contracts; they are not mere source-text grep. No source cleanup is unlocked by17 Cs; helper imports remain used. Null fatigue action is a separately recorded stale reachability/documentation F; no source type contract is narrowed in this batch.

F repairs other than the transferred wishlist observation are recommendations only, do not imply tests executed red/green, and earn zero quota. The two remount tests remain valuable against fatigue faults despite incomplete positive controls. Their unproven negative premises do not authorize deletion.
