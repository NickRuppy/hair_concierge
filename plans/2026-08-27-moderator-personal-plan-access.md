## Designed user journey

1. Before invitations are sent, these accounts are temporarily unavailable during a verified maintenance window. The operator separately clears all old application data for the approved account: quiz/profile properties, plans, routines, products, chats, memory and tracker history. Only login identity/credentials and billing/audit evidence remain; old sessions and resumable links cannot restore old state. Once the reset is verified, Nick sends the restricted invitation. No invite is distributed while reset/billing state is unresolved.
2. The moderator opens the invitation. The familiar Chaarlie account card asks them to sign in with their invited email. They can use a password or “Login-Link per E-Mail senden”; no new account or password is required.
3. An email login link returns to the moderator entry on the device where it is opened. An expired login link can be requested again without losing eligibility. A wrong account gets a switch-account prompt with no other recipients disclosed.
4. The authenticated entry explains the fresh quiz and 90-day complimentary period. “Quiz starten” enters the current Personal Plan landing/quiz with a fresh draft; old quiz/plan state is not offered as resumable content.
5. They answer the current quiz, review the personalized result, and see a 0 € offer: “Keine Zahlungsdaten · kein Abo · 90 Tage Testzugang”. Their plan is explicitly saved to their signed-in account. No Stripe/PayPal or purchase claims appear.
6. “Kostenlos mit meinem Plan fortfahren” starts activation. Loading is visible, duplicate presses are disabled, and a failure preserves the exact submitted answers/result with a retry. Success opens existing `/plan-bereit` and then the normal five-stage plan journey.
7. They use Bedarf, Feinschliff, Produkte, Routine and Anwendung with the same current feature/ownership gates as eligible users. Their account shows the exact access-end date; the period does not restart on another login.
8. Later, ordinary Chaarlie login on the same or another device opens the saved plan at its current stage. Reopening the invite while already activated resumes; it never clears data or reruns the reset.
9. After 90 days, the complimentary period ends; the account and new plan data stay stored. The screen explains that no charge was made and how to contact the team. Another valid paid entitlement is not revoked. Database outages show retry/unavailable, not a false expiry or checkout.
