## Usage type is a direct product property; preference is a separate user fact

Nick explicitly requested that research capture **where the treatment fits into the routine**, and that a user's preferred placement help choose among suitable high-trust options. Similar practical trust does not establish identical efficacy.

Reuse the existing product fields rather than introduce a duplicate `usage_type` enum:

- `application_mode`: broad routine position, currently `pre_shampoo` or `post_wash_leave_in`.
- `product_format`: cream treatment, spray treatment, leave-in mask, etc.; keep separate from position.
- `treatment_mode`: whether it is rinsed or left in; not a substitute for routine position.
- Exact source-backed protocol: hair state, order, contact/wait time, rinsing, dilution, conditioner interaction, cadence and required versus recommended partners.

Nick also confirmed that **leave-on/contact time must be an explicit researched fact**, as with masks. Capture the minimum and recommended contact time, any source-supported longer-use window, whether to rinse, and any waiting time before the next product or step. Keep these distinct: a leave-in remaining on the hair is not the same as a required wait before styling. Missing timing stays unknown; do not invent a maximum or assume overnight use is supported.

The frozen pilot already contains application facts. The amendment makes their research/projection coverage and preference consumer explicit; it does not authorize deriving instructions from chemistry or brand familiarity. Additional placements encountered in the cohort must be reviewed against the application compiler rather than forced into the two current modes.
