## What belongs here

A targeted at-home cosmetic repair/reinforcement treatment, in the German/EU market, using one of the specific approaches in Nick's candidate report:

| Recognized approach | Report references | What the explanation must preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dimaleate-based sulfur-targeting chemistry | Olaplex | Proposed chemistry is distinguishable from proof of native disulfide restoration. No.3PLUS evidence is not automatically legacy No.3 evidence. |
| Designed peptide | K18 | Peptide interaction/reinforcement differs from simply adding amino acids or generic hydrolyzed proteins. |
| Maleate-ester approach | epres | Distinct active/formulation from Olaplex; do not transfer mechanism or finished-product evidence between them. |
| Citric-acid reinforcement / calcium management | Elvital, Redken, Kérastase | A specific targeted acid-treatment architecture/role is required; citric acid used as a pH adjuster in ordinary care is insufficient. |
| Gluconamide/gluconate reinforcement | report's OGX and Aveda treatments | Identify the paired technology markers, with a targeted treatment role; supplier-proposed interactions remain identified as proposed where appropriate. |

Generic repair marketing, bond/plex branding, ordinary conditioning, and generic protein/amino-acid claims alone do not qualify. A newly discovered technology outside this list routes for category review; the engine does not silently broaden the category or assign it low trust merely for being unfamiliar.

K18 shows that a mask/leave-in presentation alone is not an exclusion. Distinguish a specialized treatment from an adjacent care product by intended role and compatible technology evidence. The optional Aveda leave-in is not a mandatory first-wave addition and does not justify duplicating records or adding a cross-category framework.

Consumer at-home products are in scope. Salon mixing/service additives, medical or hair-loss claims, and unsourced promises of permanent restoration are outside this program. Exact pack/formula/market identity is required; separate formula versions remain separate evidence targets.

## Three trust levels

- **High / well supported:** converging, relevant support for the defined repair benefit, with enough exact-product/formula/protocol applicability to justify confidence. This does not require unanimous reviews or independent replication as an absolute universal rule. A patent or popularity alone is insufficient.
- **Medium / promising:** a credible recognized approach and relevant supporting research, but limited exact-product validation or a narrower demonstrated benefit. It can qualify for a category addition when the evidence rationale and identity/protocol checks are complete.
- **Low / weakly substantiated:** a recognized approach with insufficient or materially unconvincing support for the product's repair claim. Preserve the assessment for user feedback; new curated category admission fails.

The standard will provide neighboring-level anchors and counterevidence rules using calibration. Nick has explicitly selected original OLAPLEX No.3 and No.3PLUS as high-trust reference anchors in the post-pilot amendment below. This is not permission to preassign other favourite products to high or first-wave candidates to medium. An unfinished search, identity conflict, or incomplete packet is a research hold, not a trust grade. A high-confidence classification can conclude that claim trust is low.

Relevant contradictory research is judged by quality and applicability. Mixed consumer feedback is expected and is not an automatic grade ceiling. Experience may help explain usability and variability; it does not by itself prove molecular repair.

## Smallest property set and evidence ownership

| Property / fact | Meaning and input | Consumer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology family and exact marker(s) | Formula-led identification, followed by compatible targeted-use/claim confirmation; no efficacy inference from presence alone | Category boundary, technology explanation |
| Claim trust: high / medium / low | Bounded research judgment from product/technology support and limitations; completed evidence record required | New-category admission and chat explanation; automatic shortlist ordering stays as currently approved |
| Evidence summary and sources | Which repair outcome was supported, whether evidence concerns the exact product, technology, or a multi-product system; provenance and limitations | Operator review, chat depth, future product surfaces |
| Technology explanation: concise and deeper | Plain German account of the approach, separating established findings and proposed mechanisms | Chat now; stored for future Routine Plan depth |
| Application/treatment mode and format | Usage type: exact routine position, rinse/leave-in status and product format from local directions/presentation; neither mechanism nor benefit magnitude follows from rinse status | Existing Routine Plan placement/instructions and explicit application/format preference matching among otherwise ideal products |
| Exact protocol | Damp/dry hair, order, time, rinse/layering, cadence, partners; independently source-transcribed | Existing application system; no named-product guessing |
| Intended treatment role / intensity | Intended ongoing support or targeted damage treatment; not a scientifically measured potency rating | Descriptive compatibility fact; the approved Personal Plan does not use it as a person-side ranking axis |
| Thickness suitability | Separate fit assessment from formula/application/deposition and relevant product evidence | Existing global recommendation gate; uncertainty must remain visible |
| Method/provenance | Formula hash, standard/lexicon version, artifact reference, review date, classification confidence and limiting factor | Reproducibility, review, future reassessment |

Do not add a maturity score, effect-size score, per-endpoint public ratings, or comparison page. Price is sourced intake metadata, not an efficacy property. No automatic cheaper/weaker or premium/stronger assumption is allowed.
