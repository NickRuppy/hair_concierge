## Extraction contract for the bounded opinion sweep

For each accessible original statement, record source URL/date, exact product/formula/market where known, statement context, recommendation versus personal/professional use, evidence of sustained use, stated benefit and limitations, and disclosed/verifiable commercial context. Distinguish subjective experience, mechanism commentary, reported empirical evidence and brand-hosted endorsements.

No source commenting on a product is neutral missing evidence, not a negative opinion. Inaccessible content remains a lead, not an assessed statement. Fan summaries, mirrors, generated image descriptions and automatic transcripts are not verified first-person opinions. Whole-system praise cannot be attributed to one bottle. Creator agreement does not count as independent replication of brand research.

This practical-experience lane follows the sealed blind formula stage. It cannot create a compatible technology marker, resolve a conflicting INCI/market identity or replace exact usage directions. Its trust contribution is governed by the next reviewed method version; the frozen v0.1 pilot remains unchanged.
