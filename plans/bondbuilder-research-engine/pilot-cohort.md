## Sequence

1. Write the provisional method, technology lexicon, evidence schema, source hierarchy, neighboring trust anchors, and review-hold rules from the approved charter. Label this method v0.1; do not claim it is a calibrated standard.
2. Freeze exact cohort/source/formula packets and the run procedure. Keep raw sources and hashes; product names/claims/previous grades are hidden in the formula stage. Product evidence is a subsequent explicitly unblinded stage. Decide metrics and holdout reservations before seeing validation labels.
3. Research the eight products through independent lanes using the same frozen packets. Reuse the shared versioned technology dossiers, but assess current finished-product evidence, directions, and limitations separately.
4. Bring Nick the product-by-product findings: technology, proposed trust with rationale, actual supported outcome, limits, protocol, identity/source gaps, fit readiness, and candidate admission status. Show every low/medium disagreement and unsupported high-trust claim; do not produce a league table of invented effect sizes.
5. Recalibrate the general rules from the observed traps, not desired brand grades. Record each amendment and reason; synchronize standard/prompt/runbook before replay. Preserve original results and show the exact deltas under the revised method.
6. Review the revised engine and run its preregistered independent validation on unused identities/packets. Keep pilot replay and genuinely unseen holdout results distinct. If a feasible positive holdout cannot be sourced within the permitted technology types, disclose and resolve the limitation before lock.
7. Lock the method only after validation succeeds. Build the deterministic adapter/persistence/consumer path, then prepare actual catalog additions from the reviewed results through the usual guarded process.

## Review deliverables

- One eight-row findings table, with unresolved rows left unresolved.
- Shared technology dossiers and exact product evidence/protocol records.
- Independent-lane disagreements and adjudication record.
- Proposed rule changes, why they generalize, and before/after replay deltas.
- Explicit high/medium/low examples and admission-boundary cases.
- Unused-holdout receipt, results, and disclosed coverage limits.
- A clear verdict: ready to lock, needs another calibration pass, or specific missing evidence.

No pilot result promotes, deactivates, or writes a product automatically. Existing approved products keep their prior state during review. Research findings, admission decisions, global recommendation readiness, and actual publication remain separate.
