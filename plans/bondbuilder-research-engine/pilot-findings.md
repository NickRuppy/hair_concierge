## Pilot review before the next method revision

Proposed clarification for Nick: **medium can be supported by relevant empirical technology research plus a verified compatible targeted formula and a reasoned application bridge, even when the exact retail active concentration or an exact-bottle study is unpublished.** Record those limits. A marker plus branding is insufficient; incompatible conditions, inaccessible pivotal results or a balanced low/medium call still yield a hold. High needs stronger product-applicable support. This does not waive exact identity, usage/dilution instructions, fit or protocol readiness.

The general technology-to-product route was already approved. The concrete treatment of unknown active concentration now needs a plain-language ruling because it could change admission outcomes. The property-specific hold correction follows the already separate boundary/trust/admission fields; Nick's pilot review should see it, not a silently activated revised engine.

Zero yield remains possible. Default: preserve the medium minimum and the agreed cohort; if it still cannot supply useful calibrated examples, report that result and ask before broader product sourcing. More products do not guarantee better evidence. No medium/high positive anchor exists yet, so upper-tier calibration is explicitly incomplete.
