## Relevant implications

- The shortlist spans targeted structural reinforcement, including non-covalent interactions and mineral management. A category requiring proven native-disulfide restoration would not faithfully admit the entire shortlist.
- Proposed curation: K18/No.3PLUS as core premium choices; Elvital as the first affordable entry option after verification; other candidates as format/suitability alternatives. This is a report proposal aligned with Nick's direction, not an activated ranking or evidence result.
- Cheaper price does not establish smaller benefit or restrict an option to mild damage. A satisfactory lower-cost result need not be followed by a premium upgrade.
- Patents and old-product research cannot automatically substantiate a current reformulated retail product. Record the evidence bridge and its limitations.
- Report confidence labels are appraisals from a focused public review, not proof that every relevant study was found.
- Its observed prices are 2026-09-29 snapshots. They have not been refreshed or adopted as current catalog values in this planning turn.
