## Internal authority versus new external research

The existing approved [Personal Plan decision](../../docs/personal-plan/categories/bondbuilder/decision.md) already records the active top three as all-thickness suitable. This follow-up does not overwrite or demote those reviewed existing facts because a new controlled diameter study was not found. It also does not grant all-thickness suitability to a new product by analogy.

The current Plan deliberately does not rank Bondbuilders by product weight or formula richness. Weigh-down information can inform application/explanation, not become an unapproved fine-hair penalty. Diameter (fine/normal/coarse), density, length and pattern remain distinct. Manufacturer “all types” positioning, regional individual reviews and a controlled exact-formula fit finding are different evidence types.

For the three medium candidates, new `suitableThicknesses` assignments remain unfinalized. No controlled exact-current-formula thickness comparison was identified in this bounded pass. This is a global-recommendation fit gap, not proof of unsuitable products, a category/trust downgrade or a reason to ask Nick to invent empirical findings. Further defensible product-specific fit assessment may use relevant formula/application and practice evidence; absence of a controlled trial is not a universal veto.

Intended restorative role is supported for all six; precise legacy `maintenance | intensive` assignments remain source-judgment metadata for projection review, not effect rankings or an automatic conversion from frequency/trust. A product can have initial and maintenance usage phases without changing its technology or efficacy grade.
