## Approved owner choice: K18 Conditioner guidance

Established source facts: no Conditioner **before** K18; full four-minute wait; subsequent Conditioner allowed. The explicit rinse-out branch comes from the local DE distributor and is corroborated at the general permission level by the global manufacturer. This is not “rinse K18 out after four minutes.”

**Approved by Nick on 2026-09-30:** retain a user's already established Conditioner step on a K18 wash, move it after the full wait, and let the user omit it when it is not needed. Do not add a compulsory Conditioner purchase or require Conditioner for K18 to work. The alternative of defaulting to K18 alone and mentioning Conditioner only if asked was not selected.

Approved German guidance direction for the existing application surface (not yet implemented):

> „Wasche dein Haar mit Shampoo und verwende zunächst keinen Conditioner. Trockne es gründlich mit dem Handtuch, verteile K18 und warte volle 4 Minuten. Danach kannst du deinen üblichen Conditioner verwenden und diesen wie gewohnt ausspülen. Wenn du keine zusätzliche Pflege brauchst, lässt du diesen Schritt weg. Anschließend wie gewohnt stylen.“

Quantity and course accompany this copy only after exact pack/source binding; this example does not settle the conflicting cadence variants. If a rinse-out Conditioner is chosen, subsequent drying and styling follow that Conditioner/styling protocol; do not automatically apply another K18 dose or Shampoo again. If a leave-in care product is chosen, do not give it a rinse instruction. No automatic extra Mask + Conditioner stack is introduced.

### User journey and recovery

Entry: user has a verified, in-hand K18 primary and an existing Conditioner preference/care need. Guidance shows Shampoo first, explicitly delays Conditioner, then a four-minute wait. The user either continues with their normal Conditioner after the wait or skips extra conditioning. Completion: normal styling after the chosen care sequence; K18 is not assigned a standalone rinse step.

If the user has already conditioned before K18, explain the intended order for the next K18 application; do not generate an extra compulsory wash to redo today's routine.
