# Trial notices and privacy compatibility — final research

Research date: 13 September 2026. Scope: Germany-facing Chaarlie consumer membership, 7-day card/PayPal-authorized trial, then EUR 9.99/month or EUR 69.99 first annual year and EUR 99.99/year thereafter. This is implementation evidence, not legal advice or a tax determination. It deliberately separates legal/network obligations from product choice.

## Result: the minimum communication set

Nick's decision is **no optional trial-reminder campaign**. That remains possible, but it does **not** remove the following transactional notifications. They must be sent outside marketing consent and recorded as attempted/delivered; a provider-hosted receipt can count only after its content, timing, sender, and durable-copy properties are verified.

| Event | Minimum content and timing | Why it is required / status | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Capture affirmative acceptance without a hidden/expand-only disclosure. | German distance-contract information duties require total price including taxes, subscription-period total/monthly total, term/cancellation conditions and withdrawal information. Mastercard requires the trial, later price/frequency and affirmative acceptance at the credential request and summary. | Stripe card and PayPal. |

### Seven-day conclusion — corrected after later primary-source verification

Visa’s **20 February 2020 clarification** explicitly covers trials of **seven days or less**: put the trial expiry and simple cancellation details in the initial confirmation. This clarifies the older 2019 document’s narrower wording. The earlier exact-seven-day timing blocker was a false inference from the superseded wording and is withdrawn. Verify complete initial confirmation and delivery; no invented seven-day timing workaround or optional reminder campaign. [Later Visa clarification](https://usa.visa.com/dam/VCOM/global/support-legal/documents/recurring-vbn-public.pdf), page 2.

## Provider and network findings

### Visa card via Stripe

- Visa requires disclosure and simple cancellation for trial subscriptions. For seven days or less, the later clarification specifies those details in the initial confirmation. The first clearing transaction must carry a trial-related enhanced descriptor. Stripe's ability to pass that descriptor for this account is **unverified**; implementation must obtain written/provider evidence before launch rather than assuming that an invoice description changes a Visa clearing descriptor.
- The selected offer must visibly state the later charge, renewal frequency, and cancellation terms. For this seven-day trial, verify the initial confirmation contains the required expiry/cancellation information under the later clarification.

### Mastercard card via Stripe

- Mastercard requires terms alongside card credentials and order summary, affirmative acceptance, and prompt electronic confirmation after the subscription order, including cancellation/account-management instructions.
- For a digital trial of seven days or less, Mastercard's FAQ says no additional 3–7 day pre-trial-end reminder is needed. The confirmation still is required.
- The public FAQ is not sufficient to set renewal timing: Stripe's current merchant guidance says subscriptions with billing periods **longer than 180 days** need an upcoming-renewal event 7–30 days ahead. Thus the annual plan, not monthly, is the relevant renewal-notice case. Preserve payment receipts for every successful collection.
- Stripe cannot reliably identify card network to the product UI before payment. The safe implementation is a provider-neutral transactional confirmation and payment receipt for every card subscriber, plus an annual 7–30 day transactional renewal event for card subscribers unless Stripe proves a compliant network-specific alternative.

### PayPal

- PayPal's current Subscriptions documentation supports trial billing cycles and says the regular billing period begins after the trial. Its Braintree recurring-billing guidance says a payment method is required at subscription start even for a trial and says users are not alerted by default before the first billing cycle; it recommends the merchant alert them.
- No official PayPal direct-Subscriptions rule was found that replaces the German contract confirmation, Visa/Mastercard rules for card rails, or § 312k cancellation requirements. Treat PayPal’s on-screen approval/receipt as **insufficient until verified** as a durable confirmation that includes all required terms and a Chaarlie cancellation path.

### Provider option, not a decision yet

Stripe and PayPal may be able to issue receipts/invoices or show subscriber-management/cancellation paths. They can reduce duplication only if a prelaunch proof records: which message is sent; its complete content; exact send point; durable medium; language; branded sender; inclusion of the correct 69.99→99.99 annual progression; and its behavior after retry/out-of-order webhooks. No provider email should be enabled as marketing.

## German contract and cancellation floor

- Before the binding click/authorization, [Art. 246a § 1 EGBGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgbeg/art_246a__1.html) requires the total price including taxes, subscription-period total/monthly total, delivery/payment terms, contract term/cancellation conditions, and where applicable withdrawal information. This supports the approved compact offer copy, but it must be complete at the credential/summary step; hiding the material terms behind a link or disclosure is not enough under Mastercard's rule.
- [§ 312f BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__312f.html) requires distance-contract confirmation on a durable medium within a reasonable time, no later than before service performance. If this is digital content and withdrawal is to lapse early, that confirmation must record the express consent and acknowledgement.
- [§ 312k BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__312k.html) requires a readily accessible `Verträge hier kündigen`-equivalent button, a confirmation page ending in `jetzt kündigen`-equivalent, a saveable declaration with timestamp, and immediate text-form confirmation. The current profile control does not evidence that complete statutory sequence: PayPal directly POSTs cancellation and updates local state; Stripe hands off to its portal ([manage-subscription-button.tsx](../../src/components/profile/manage-subscription-button.tsx#L18-L85)). Add a dedicated compliant path; do not rely on the present button or account-only discoverability without counsel/provider verification.
- Separately from the trial flow, current [§ 356a BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__356a.html) requires a consistently available, prominent `Vertrag widerrufen`-equivalent function during the withdrawal period, followed by durable confirmation. The Bundestag records the enacted electronic withdrawal button as part of the implementation of Directive 2023/2673 ([official legislative record](https://www.bundestag.de/parlament/plenum/abstimmung/abstimmung/?id=991)). This is broader platform compliance, not a reason to add product messaging beyond the legally required path.
- The existing withdrawal page supplies an email/form template but no online submission function ([widerruf/page.tsx](../../src/app/widerruf/page.tsx#L33-L105)). It also says digital-content withdrawal lapses after consent/acknowledgement ([widerruf/page.tsx](../../src/app/widerruf/page.tsx#L68-L79)); the checkout must actually collect and preserve those declarations before relying on that result. A recurring, immediately available membership may instead be legally a service or mixed digital product. Do not guess its classification.

## Unresolved facts that must be proved before implementation is called ready

1. **Stripe/acquirer:** Visa trial descriptor mechanism and complete initial-confirmation delivery under the later seven-days-or-less clarification.
2. **Provider messages:** whether either provider's transactional emails/receipts meet the content, durable-medium, sender, timing, and audit requirements above. Otherwise Chaarlie must send the minimum transactional notices.
3. **Germany legal review:** classification of this membership as service/digital content/mixed, the correct early-performance consent/withdrawal treatment, and retention/legal basis for the anti-repeat identity record. This is legal validation, not a request to invent a VAT rate.
4. **Account deletion execution:** an approved process for active or canceled provider agreements, statutory billing records, and the eligibility claim. Existing repository evidence identifies this as already unresolved; no deletion behavior should be silently changed in the trial work.

### One bundled fact to obtain (only if a business input is needed)

Ask counsel/merchant-of-record adviser for one written launch classification: **For Chaarlie’s immediately available paid membership, is the contract treated as a digital-content contract, a service, or mixed; may we seek early-performance consent/loss of withdrawal; and what lawful retention/anonymization period/process applies to the minimal one-ever-trial proof after account deletion?** This one answer determines the withdrawal UI/confirmation and retention design. It does not reopen the seven-day trial, price, cancellation, or no-marketing-reminder decisions.

## Additional final contract findings

- **Binding action:** the approved `7 Tage kostenlos testen` offer CTA can remain where it leads to a non-binding selection/checkout step. At the step that actually concludes the paid subscription, §312j requires prominent material terms and an unambiguous payment-obligation confirmation. For an app-controlled binding button use `Zahlungspflichtig bestellen` or legally verified equivalent; a provider-hosted binding step must be inspected for the same requirement. Do not assume a free-only label suffices because the initial amount is zero. [§312j BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__312j.html).
- **Annual continuation:** §309 no.9 generally prohibits an AGB automatic extension into another binding fixed year for ongoing consumer services. Continuation is indefinite with termination possible at any time on no more than one month’s notice. The 99.99 annual rate is separate from the permitted commitment and prepaid settlement. Confirm applicability to Chaarlie, and resolve O4 before designing year-two cancellation/refunds. No automatic-refund formula or monthly replacement price is selected by this research. [§309 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__309.html), [transition rule](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgbeg/art_229__60.html).
