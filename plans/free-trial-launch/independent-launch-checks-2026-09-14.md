## 2. Trial-use history after account deletion

**Resolved technical direction:** the approved server-only HMAC claims, key/policy versions, consumed timestamp and opaque enrollment linkage minimize the retained relation. Keep it independent of profile cascade, without using it to block profile deletion. HMAC remains personal data. [Assessment](/Users/nick/AI_work/hair_conscierge/.worktrees/free-trial-launch/plans/free-trial-launch/trial-history-implementation-assessment.md:7).

**Before the first real claim write**, the controller must accept a concrete lawful-basis/necessity/balancing assessment. It must substantiate the present interest, why each retained identifier and continued matching are necessary, less intrusive alternatives, shared-payment-method mistakes and correction safeguards. Contract performance or invoice retention does not automatically authorize anti-repeat matching. No reviewed source establishes a statutory anti-repeat period or indefinite entitlement to retain it. Annual necessity review alone does not establish lawful duration: specify measurable retention/end criteria, review responsibility and deletion/anonymization execution. [EDPB lawful processing](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/process-personal-data-lawfully_en).

The disclosure must explain the purpose, basis/interest, data categories, retention period or criteria, denial consequence and rights. Restriction normally permits storage while barring eligibility matching, subject to statutory exceptions. An objection requires the relevant overriding-ground assessment; a valid erasure outcome must remove the affected linked claims. Respond within one month, document decisions and inform recipients where required. [EDPB rights guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/respect-individuals-rights_en).

**Implementation acceptance:** synthetic tests must demonstrate profile deletion/recreation, all linked claim dispositions, correction, restriction, accepted erasure, key rotation, concurrent admission and late webhook/backfill/support replay. Erased or restricted claims must not be silently reconstructed from retained provider records. A narrowly justified event/operation suppression record may prevent replay; it must not become a permanent identity blacklist that continues forbidden matching. If a fully erased identity is no longer recognizable, the implementation cannot guarantee cross-account detection. This is already acknowledged in [foundation resolution](/Users/nick/AI_work/hair_conscierge/.worktrees/free-trial-launch/plans/free-trial-launch/foundation-review-resolution.md:12).

**Gate scope:** controller acceptance + effective disclosure + tested rights operation precede any real claim write, including migrations, backfills, callbacks and imports. An enrollment flag alone is insufficient. Empty local schema and synthetic tests may continue under the existing authorization. No fixed lifetime or automatic trial reset is proposed here.
