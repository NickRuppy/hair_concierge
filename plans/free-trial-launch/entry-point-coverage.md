## Common offer contract

Eligible new subscription customers receive the same seven-day authorized trial: monthly EUR 9.99, or annual EUR 69.99 for the first paid year under the launch offer then EUR 99.99/year. No quarterly option for the new cohort. Existing subscribers keep their terms. An existing account alone does not disqualify a never-paid, never-trial customer; previous trial users or subscribers must not receive another trial. One-time buyers and special cohorts still require their recorded eligibility decisions.

Use one eligibility and commercial contract across surfaces; changing the landing-page wording alone cannot enforce this. Lead existence, source strings, query parameters or a stored old offer must not independently grant a trial or select obsolete new-customer billing.

## Checkout enforcement and acceptance checks

The app has three named subscription checkout sources: `quiz_result_offer`, `pricing_page` (including `membership_reactivation`), and parked `premium_sheet`. Both Stripe and PayPal need the same approved eligibility/offer rules. The one-time checkout uses a separate commerce contract and PayPal order endpoints.

- Stripe: `src/app/api/stripe/create-checkout-session/route.ts:81`, `:387`, `:686`; shared `PaymentMethodCheckout` and Stripe offer Elements checkout.
- PayPal: `src/app/api/paypal/create-subscription-intent/route.ts:46`, `:83`, plus `/api/paypal/approve-subscription` and activation verification.
- Never let old monthly/quarterly/yearly selection, saved lead assignment, client source, cached/prepared checkout or provider return bypass the agreed new-cohort contract. Previously authorized in-flight sessions need an explicit cutover policy; do not silently mutate them.
- Exercise E01–E12 with eligible new customers, prior trial, prior paid subscription, active legacy subscription, uncertain access, pending payment/activation and applicable special cohorts. Check both providers, mobile and desktop, direct entry/revisit, resume, checkout abandonment, stale interval links, expired access and payment recovery.
- All visible trial claims, checkout totals and provider schedules must agree. Trial activation is not a paid purchase event. Marketing attribution follows the original entry through the first successful payment.
