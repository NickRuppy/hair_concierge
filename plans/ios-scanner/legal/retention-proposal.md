| Data | Proposed rule | Status / implementation qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Haarprofil, account details and Merkliste | Keep while providing the account; erase promptly on verified deletion request, operational target no later than 30 days for ordinary live-system erasure. | New explicit deadline proposed. This is a maximum service target, not permission to wait 30 days or a substitute for applicable legal response deadlines. Normal successful deletion should be much faster. Cancellation uncertainty follows the existing recovery contract. |
| Dormant free scanner accounts | After 24 months without authenticated activity across web/app, give 30 days' service notice before deleting unless user returns. | Consequential proposal, not approved. Exclude accounts with paid access or purchased-content rights; review their continuing purpose separately. No marketing opt-in required for the necessary account-expiry notice. Must not silently erase a paid web account. |
| Quiz answers without account | Delete after 90 days. | Existing public policy retained; verify actual cleanup and distinguish any independent consent record. |
| Camera frames | Decode on device without uploading or saving the camera feed. | Proposed native acceptance requirement; do not publish as current until verified. No scanner history introduced. |
| Ordinary scanner troubleshooting records | Maximum 30 days from event, with minimum identifiers and no camera content/full hair answers. | Proposed cap; do not extend existing seven-day web-server-log promise. Verify every relevant collector/provider, including error traces. Specific justified incident evidence is separately restricted and reviewed. |
| Product research requests | Personal request association needed while open; remove within 90 days after completion/closure, or earlier on account deletion. Public product facts can remain without submitter identity. | Proposed; pending requests need a six-month necessity/status review rather than indefinite unattended storage. Catalog contribution and personal request are separate. |
| Payment support cases | Necessary case/contact while open, then delete 90 days after resolution. | Existing public period and approved minimal-contact exception. Open case must not retain hair/list/account data. Required financial evidence is copied only into its separate record class. |
| Push registration | Remove/revoke account association on logout, account change or deletion; stop sends immediately when deletion requested. | Approved behavior. Invalidate provider-rejected tokens and prune stale tokens; implementation must not rely on uninstall detection. |
| Backups | Deleted live data expires from backups within 90 days after live erasure. | Proposed cap, conditional on actual provider configuration. Backups isolated from normal use; restore must reapply deletion/suppression before reopening service. Do not claim 90 days total from request if live erasure has not completed. |
| Marketing data | Stop on withdrawal/deletion immediately. Retain only justified minimum consent evidence and suppression record separately. | Approved behavior; exact proof expiry still requires purpose/record-specific determination. Suppression must not recreate a marketing account. No silent campaign changes through this document. |
| Invoice/payment/contract evidence | Only required fields, restricted separately, with documented applicable expiry. | Tax classification is confirmed; exact record obligations and OSS use are still not established. Do not make all app data ten-year records or guess a universal financial period. |

The public notice will describe the agreed periods after enforcement and provider limits are verified. User access to existing purchased content is not shortened by a new dormant-free-account rule. Review records annually and when a new processing purpose is added.

Sources: [Irish DPC on retention](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/faqs/responsibilities-data-controllers/how-long-should-personal-data-be-held-meet-obligations-imposed-gdpr) explains that GDPR does not prescribe a generic duration for every data type. The 24-month, 30-day and backup-90-day proposals above are our suggested operational choices, not legal numbers or a measured industry consensus. [EU Commission OSS records](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop/record-keeping-and-audits-oss_en) addresses a separate financial class.
