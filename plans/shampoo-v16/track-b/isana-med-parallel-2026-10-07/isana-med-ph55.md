## Fit for Viola: **keep, with conditions**

- **Suits her:** It is weightless, so it won't flatten fine wavy strands. It is silicone-free. At pH 5.5 with urea and panthenol, it is friendly to a dry-feeling scalp. Used weekly, it is a good way to clear residue from the glaze mask and oil without a harsh clarifier.
- **Doesn't help:** It gives no slip, so it adds no detangling help for bleached hair that snaps. The SLES base can dry porous lengths if she scrubs them. Her main problems (dry, frizzy, tangled lengths) belong to the conditioner/leave-in step and to wash technique, not to this shampoo.
- **Watch for:** lengths that feel squeaky or rough right after rinsing (then swap to a gentler, more conditioning fine-hair shampoo). Also watch for more breakage or knots while washing, and waves dropping by day 2–3 (more likely mask/oil buildup than this shampoo).
- **Engine note:** The projection only gives the sensitive-scalp bucket. If her scalp bucket is "trocken", the app would not show this product to her. That is not a reason to tell her to stop using it.

**Ask in the call:**
1. Which shampoo do you use for the other weekly wash? How do your lengths feel right after rinsing each one?
2. Do you put shampoo only on your scalp or also through the lengths? Do you get many knots or broken hairs while washing?
3. How much glaze mask and oil do you use, and does your hair feel flat or coated by day 2–3?
