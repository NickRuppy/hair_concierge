## Fit for Viola: **depends, leaning swap**

- **Built for someone else.** It is an anti-dandruff shampoo for oily hair. She has a dry scalp, washes once a week and reported no scalp problems on Oct 3.
- **The flakes are the deciding question.** "Trockene Schuppen" on a dry scalp sounds more like dryness flakes than classic dandruff. If so, she doesn't need the active. Swap to a mild, low-fragrance shampoo for a comfortable scalp that adds a little slip. If the flakes come back whenever she stops, keeping it at her weekly wash is a reasonable maintenance habit.
- **Good for fine hair.** Light and not weighing; the moderate cleansing is fine at once a week and removes oil/leave-in build-up.
- **Downsides:** menthol and fragrance can feel tightening on a dry scalp. Zero conditioning means wet tangles before the mask, which is risky for bleached hair that snaps. Shampoo the scalp only.
- The shampoo won't fix dry, snapping lengths or lost wave shape. The mask, leave-in and gentle handling are the bigger levers.

**Ask in the call:**
1. In June, what were the flakes like: small, dry and white with a tight scalp, or bigger and sticky? Did they come back on another shampoo?
2. How does your scalp feel after this shampoo and over the week: tight, tingly or itchy? Is it ever oily before the next wash?
3. How tangled is your hair right after shampooing, before the mask? Do you wash the lengths too?
