## Fit for Viola: **depends**

**OK to keep as her once-a-week wash if her scalp likes it.** But it doesn't help her main problem.

- **For:** light, with no silicones or oils, which suits her fine strands and waves. Once a week it can clear residue from the lamination mask and oil.
- **Against:** it's made for itchy scalps, and she has none right now. It's a normal sulfate wash, not a gentle one, which matters for bleached hair that snaps. On its own it detangles little.
- **Watch:** the menthol and perfume can make a dry scalp feel tight or tingly. Swap it if her scalp feels tight or her lengths feel rough or tangled after washing.
- **Technique:** scalp only, let the foam run through the lengths, always follow with the mask. Her flat waves more likely come from damage and heavy layering than from this shampoo.

**Ask her:**

1. What do you use for your other weekly wash, and why did you pick this one? Did you have an itchy scalp at some point?
2. How do your scalp and lengths feel right after this one compared with the other? Tight or tingly? More tangles or frizz?
3. Scalp only or the whole length? How much mask and oil do you use, and do your waves look flatter after the oil?

*Cosmetic guidance only. If an itchy or flaky scalp comes back, refer her to a dermatologist.*
