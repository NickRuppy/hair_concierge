## Fit for Viola: **depends (keep for now, likely switch later)**

- **Works for her:** light enough for fine waves, and fragrance-free and soothing for a dry scalp. Cleansing is moderate, so not harsh, and she only uses it once a week.
- **Not needed long-term:** the anti-dandruff active targets classic dandruff. She described *dry* flakes and reports no scalp problems now. Once the flakes stay away, a mild, fragrance-free everyday shampoo for dry or sensitive scalp fits her better. One option to check is ISANA MED Jeden Tag Ultra Sensitiv, which has not been classified yet.
- **Not her lengths problem:** this shampoo has low conditioning. Her dryness, frizz and tangling are handled by the mask, leave-in and oil, so changing the shampoo will not fix her lengths. Tip: lather only on the scalp and let the rinse run through the lengths.
- If flakes return strongly, or her scalp gets red, sore or very itchy, she should ask a pharmacist or doctor.

**Ask in the call:**
1. Are the flakes completely gone, and did they come back when you used another shampoo? Were they fine, white and dry, or larger, yellowish and itchy?
2. How does your scalp feel a few days after washing (tight or itchy)? Do you lather your lengths too?
3. Right after shampooing, before the mask, do your lengths feel squeaky or rough?
